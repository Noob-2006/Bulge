# Code of conduct

> 中文摘要：在這個專案的所有地方（issue、PR、討論、聊天室）都要互相尊重。騷擾、歧視、人身攻擊、公開他人隱私都不被接受。違規請私下向維護者回報，我們會保密處理。

This code of conduct follows the principles of the [Contributor Covenant, version
2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in SE Agent to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent; disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information (such as an address or email) without their
  explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or other conduct that would reasonably be considered
  inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat rooms, events)
and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report incidents privately to the maintainers through the contact listed in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (mark the report "code of conduct"). Reports are handled confidentially;
the identity of the reporter is protected.

Maintainers may, depending on the severity, respond with a private warning, a request to stop, a
temporary ban from project spaces, or a permanent ban. Maintainers who do not follow this code
in good faith may face the same consequences.
