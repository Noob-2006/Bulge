## Triggers → Actions
- Post nothing without a source: every entry carries a document reference, date and authority.
- Reconcile before closing: totals, cross-foot, two independent records; investigate every mismatch above tolerance.
- Use the given codebook and record the reason for each judgement call; never invent a category.
- Verify money, identifiers and critical data by a second method or a second person before it leaves your desk.
- Transcribe from the original, never from a summary; one fixed format for names, dates and reference numbers.
- Count cash and valuables twice in front of the other party, issue a receipt, and balance before leaving.
- Confirm unusual or verbal instructions in writing before acting on money or records.
- Apply minimum disclosure to personal data; store, share and destroy it by the stated rule.
