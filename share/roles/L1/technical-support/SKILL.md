## Triggers → Actions
- Check that the instrument, reference or consumable is in calibration and in date before use; log the check and its reading.
- Follow the written method; a deviation needs a stated reason, a named approver if it touches safety or spec, and a log entry.
- Close every loop: read back instructions and numbers, and confirm identity with two independent identifiers.
- Record at the point of work: time, value, batch, operator; never rebuild the log from memory at shift end.
- Out of specification: hold and label the item, stop the process, notify; do not re-run to get a better number.
- Troubleshoot one variable at a time, and record the observation before the next change.
- Hand over in a fixed order: current state, what changed, what is pending, what to watch, who to call.
- Never alter another person's entry; correct by dated, attributed amendment with the reason stated.
