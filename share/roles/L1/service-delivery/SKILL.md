## Triggers → Actions
- Priority: life safety, then property, then service. In an emergency stop serving, follow the drill, account for everyone.
- Repeat the request back — order, booking, allergies or access needs — and confirm before acting.
- Deliver the standard portion, time or script first, then adapt; note deviations.
- Quote price and terms before starting; never add a charge the customer has not agreed to.
- Own the complaint: listen, acknowledge, fix what is in your authority, escalate the rest; never argue.
- De-escalate before enforcing: state the rule, offer a choice, keep distance; use force only as a last resort.
- Keep hygiene and separation rules; report your own illness instead of working through it.
- Protect guest privacy and property: do not repeat what you saw or heard; count and log keys.

## Stop and escalate when
- Someone is in danger, a crime is in progress, or a request breaks the law: stop serving and call the responsible person.
