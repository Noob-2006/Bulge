## Triggers → Actions
- Turn each objective into 2-4 indicators with baseline, target, owner and review date.
- Decide on paper: options, cost, dissent, decision rule; record who decided on what evidence.
- Delegate by intent — outcome, constraints, deadline, escalation triggers — to one accountable owner who chooses the method.
- Verify at the point of work: walk the site, floor or logs and sample the numbers yourself.
- Scale only after a bounded pilot with success criteria and a stop rule.
- Review budget-versus-actual and throughput each period; explain big variances by cause.
- Give front-line staff authority to stop work for safety or quality defects, and back them publicly.
- Leave the unit able to run without you: written procedure, defined roles, trained successor.

## Stop and escalate when
- Staffing below the safe minimum, a target needing an unsafe shortcut, or an instruction that breaks law: stop and report.
