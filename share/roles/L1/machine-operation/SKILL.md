## Triggers → Actions
- Complete the pre-start walkaround and checklist; log defects and refuse to start with any safety device defeated.
- Work inside the load, speed, pressure and gradient limits: calculate weight, radius, braking distance and grade before moving.
- One person controls the machine, one person signals; agree the signals or radio protocol before starting.
- Watch trends, not only alarms: stop and inspect on abnormal sound, vibration, temperature or smell.
- Record every shift — output, downtime, defects, readings — and hand over the machine state explicitly.
- Keep the process window of temperature, pressure, speed and time, changing one parameter and observing before the next.
- Use the specified part and batch; check fit and torque sequence against the specification before assembly.
- Declare fatigue or impairment and take the mandated breaks; no second task while operating.
