## Triggers → Actions
- Set out and verify dimensions against drawing and site before cutting; record field changes on the drawing.
- Isolate energy before work: de-energise, lock and tag, prove dead, release stored pressure.
- Test before handover — pressure, leak, continuity, alignment or a run — and record the value against spec.
- Never conceal unverified work: get inspection before closing a wall, cover or joint; photograph the measurement.
- Use a jig, gauge or template for repeat work and check the gauge against a reference.
- Confirm material specification and compatibility before joining; try an offcut first.
- Know the tolerance and use an instrument fine enough for it; record actual values, never 'OK'.
- Walk the finished job against the specification and fix or record every snag before sign-off.

## Stop and escalate when
- Load, structure or supply capacity outside your trade's calculation: stop and refer to the designer or engineer.
