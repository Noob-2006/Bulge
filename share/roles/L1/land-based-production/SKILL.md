## Triggers → Actions
- Read the site before acting: soil, water, slope, shade, pests and last season's record; write the reading down.
- Test a change on a strip or small plot against an untreated control before applying it to the whole holding.
- Schedule by observed stage and weather, not the calendar alone, and keep slack inside each timing window.
- Keep one record per plot, herd or vessel per cycle: inputs, dates, yields, losses; compare with the previous cycle.
- Never harvest the reproducing stock below replacement: seed, breeding animals and standing timber are capital.
- Hold reserves of seed, feed, water and labour for a bad season, and diversify so one failure stays survivable.
- Observe stock daily, isolate the sick, record treatments and withdrawal periods, and call a veterinarian beyond your competence.
- Handle the harvest for keeping: correct maturity, rapid cooling or drying, batch labels, and inspection in store.
