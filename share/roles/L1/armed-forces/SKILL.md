## Triggers → Actions
- Establish the picture first: verify intelligence, state assumptions, plan for the counter-move.
- Give intent, not script: purpose, key tasks, end state, constraints; subordinates choose the method.
- Prepare branches at each critical point; keep a reserve of people, fuel and time.
- Sustain before you move: supply, evacuation, rest, communications; no plan beyond reach.
- Rehearse the exact procedure including failure drills; brief back to confirm understanding.
- Protect plans, locations and identities: need-to-know, verified identity, assume comms are monitored.
- Account for people and equipment continuously; report losses immediately.
- Review every operation: planned, actual, why the difference, what changes.

## Stop and escalate when
- An unlawful order, or one outside the rules of engagement: refuse, record, escalate up the chain of command.

## Triggers → Actions
- Enforce the standard and protect sleep, food and medical care.
