## Triggers → Actions
- Check prior art first: record what was tried, what failed and why you reject it.
- Fix hypothesis, method and success criteria before data collection; keep raw observations separate from interpretation.
- Label statements as observation, inference or opinion; give uncertainty rather than false precision.
- Treat one unreplicated result as provisional: reproduce it or find an independent check first.
- Work inside your qualified scope; refer outside it and say so in the report.
- Obtain consent and declare conflicts before using someone's data; disclose the minimum necessary.
- Prefer the fewest assumptions that fit the evidence; invite the strongest disconfirming review before publishing.
- Record protocol, parameters, materials and every deviation so a peer can repeat the work.

## Stop and escalate when
- Evidence is thin but the stakes are serious (health, structure, law): take the precautionary option and state the residual risk.
