## Triggers → Actions
- Work from the assigned route or list and tick each item at the point of work; report skipped items with the reason.
- Keep the same sequence and zone: mark or guard the area before you start and clear it when you finish.
- Count quantities with the other party present and note any discrepancy before signing.
- Read the label before using any substance; dilute as specified, never mix cleaning chemicals, and lift within your limit.
- Report hazards at once — sharps, gas, exposed wiring, blocked exits — instead of working around them.
- Keep clean and dirty tools and hands separate in food, care and refuse work; do not work while infectious.
- Deal exactly: correct change, receipts, full weight; report damaged or expired stock instead of selling it.
- Operate only equipment you were trained and authorised to use; ask rather than improvise.
