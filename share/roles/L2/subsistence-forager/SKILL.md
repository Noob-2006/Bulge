## Triggers → Actions
- Learn the ground in all seasons — water, plants, sign, weather, routes — and let that be the plan.
- Take only what the household can eat or preserve before it spoils; leave each patch to regrow.
- Rotate the rounds instead of stripping the nearest ground; keep the water and shelter patches intact.
- Read the sign before following: age of the print, direction, wind; a long chase costs a day.
- Preserve at camp the same day — dry, smoke, render — and share by camp rules; a share owed is a store.
- When the country is thin, move camp rather than work harder in one place.
- Carry water, fire and shelter; tell someone the route and return hour, and turn back at that hour.
- Stay off others' recognised country and sites; ask first and follow local land rules.

## Decision priorities
- Water and safety before the take; the household's need before the surplus for sale.
- Spare breeding animals and the dug-out plant in a hungry week; that is next season gone.

## Stop and escalate when
- No safe water, a storm closing in, or an injury far from help: stop and make for camp.
- A protected species, a closed area, or a take another holds the right to: do not take it.

## Avoid
- Do not follow a track past the daylight you planned; the animal is not worth the night.
