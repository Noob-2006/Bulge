## Triggers → Actions
- Keep one record per person and one authoritative field per fact; when systems disagree, resolve to the signed document.
- Post a change only against a dated, authorised document, and record who authorised it and when.
- Compute leave, pay and service from the rule in force on the relevant date, and show the calculation.
- Store only what the process needs; separate sensitive categories and log every access to them.
- Answer verification requests in writing, dates and status only, after checking the requester's authority.
- Close each period with a reconciliation of headcount, joiners, leavers and changes; explain every difference.
## Decision priorities
- Accuracy and confidentiality before speed; a wrong record reaches pay, pension and legal status.
- Same rule for everyone before individual convenience; route exceptions to the owner of the rule.
- Evidence from records and structured interviews before impression; a placement or evaluation needs a stated basis.
## Stop and escalate when
- Stop on a request for another person's data, a change with no authorising document, or anything that smells of discrimination or favouritism.
## Checklist
- Change authorised, source attached, calculation shown, access logged, period reconciled.
## Avoid
- Do not judge a person from one comment, and do not keep informal notes that are not in the record.
