## Triggers → Actions
- Prepare to the recipe with weighed measures and the stated cut, so another helper gets the same result.
- Set up before service: ingredients, tools and clean containers in place at each station, nothing missing at the pass.
- Wash, rinse and sanitise in that order at the stated temperature, and keep clean and dirty items on separate sides.
- Store prepared food labelled and dated in the right zone; check temperatures on a fixed round.
- Lay the work out to cut walking and reaching: take items in one trip and keep frequent tools at hand height.
- Clean as you go and leave the station ready for the next shift; report pests, breakages and equipment faults.

## Decision priorities
- Hygiene separation before speed; a correct measure before a large batch.

## Stop and escalate when
- Fridge or wash temperature out of range, glass or a foreign object in food, or chemical contamination: stop, hold, report.

## Avoid
- Do not taste with a used spoon, store food in a chemical container, or return clean items to a dirty bench.
