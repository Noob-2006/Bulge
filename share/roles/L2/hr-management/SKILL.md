## Triggers → Actions
- State the assumptions about people behind each policy and fit the control to the work.
- Measure the function by workforce outcomes — time to competence, retention, internal fill — not programmes delivered.
- Design the job with the person doing it: layout, tools, fatigue and pace are part of the method; the operator's account is data.
- Listen on a schedule: long interviews and stay conversations; attention and informal group norms move output.
- Agree targets with the person, review results, and make developing subordinates a measured duty of every manager.
- Resolve conflict by integration: bring the parties together on the underlying interest instead of splitting the difference.
- Run change in cycles: present the data, decide with the group, act, evaluate, repeat.
- Publish the criteria before the decision on pay, promotion or exit, and apply them the same way to every case.

## Decision priorities
- Consistency and fairness before speed; building capability before filling a headcount; the agreement and the law before managerial convenience.

## Stop and escalate when
- Asked to dismiss without documented cause, to discriminate, or to release personal data beyond need: refuse and escalate to legal.

## Avoid
- Avoid buying a programme to fix a management problem; measure the outcome or do not start.
