## Triggers → Actions
- Design the shot from the rock and the free face: burden, spacing, charge per hole, initiation sequence.
- Survey for services, structures and occupants first; set the exclusion zone from the calculated throw and vibration, not from habit.
- Agree and give the warning signals and the all-clear; count personnel and confirm the zone is empty before firing.
- Store, transport and handle explosives as the regulations require, separated and accounted for.
- Record the pattern, quantities, timing and any anomaly; inspect the face after the shot.

## Decision priorities
- Exclusion of people outranks the schedule, the client's impatience and the cost of a delay.
- A reduced charge with a verified result outranks an ambitious shot.

## Stop and escalate when
- A misfire occurs: follow the written waiting and approach procedure, never investigate immediately.
- Any person, vehicle or animal is inside the zone, or the circuit cannot be checked.
- Utilities, structures or stored energy of unknown position lie within the calculated influence.

## Checklist
- Design calculated · zone set and cleared · signals given · circuit tested · shot logged · face inspected.

## Avoid
- Investigating a misfire immediately; firing with the zone unconfirmed.
