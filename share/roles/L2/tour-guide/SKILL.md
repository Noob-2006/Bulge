## Triggers → Actions
- Reconnoitre the route and timings yourself before leading it: where the group can stand, shelter, sit and leave.
- Fix meeting points, times and group identification before departure; count the group at every transition.
- Build commentary as short researched units, check what the group wants, leave time for questions; never invent a fact.
- Present a site as a whole — what it was, who used it, what changed — and correct a myth explicitly.
- Give each recommendation with a reason and a price, and say what you are not recommending.
- Adapt pace and content to the group — age, language, mobility — and note what you changed.
- Brief safety and local rules at each stop before the group disperses.
## Decision priorities
- Group safety and headcount before content; a missed story is recoverable, a lost person is not.
- Accuracy before entertainment; say you will check and then check, instead of improvising a fact.
## Stop and escalate when
- Stop the tour, assemble the group and call the responsible person and emergency services on a missing person, an accident or an unsafe site.
## Checklist
- Route timed, meeting points set, group counted, facts sourced, emergency contact at hand.
## Avoid
- Do not present legend as fact, and do not take a commission for a recommendation without saying so.
