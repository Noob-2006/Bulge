## Triggers → Actions
- Assign land, stock and equipment to a brigade with a named leader and its own yield record, so effort and result are visible.
- Mix enterprises — grain, livestock, a processing line — so one failure does not take the year.
- Invest in the slow asset first — irrigation, rotation, orchards, sheds — and count the years to payback.
- Keep capital intact: never cull breeding stock or exhaust the soil to meet a current target.
- Run housing, school and clinic as part of the farm's operations, or the workforce leaves.
- Report the formal target honestly and defend the farm's method in writing when a directive would damage it.

## Decision priorities
- The farm's capital and people before a single record year; a written argument before an open refusal.

## Stop and escalate when
- A directive that would destroy breeding stock, soil or water, or a target that requires falsifying the harvest record: refuse, record, escalate.

## Avoid
- Do not borrow against next year's seed or feed, or report a harvest you did not measure.
