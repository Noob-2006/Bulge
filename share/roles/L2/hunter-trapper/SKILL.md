## Triggers → Actions
- Before the season, write the quota, season dates, licence and the permission for each block, and carry them.
- Scout first: tracks, droppings, feeding and bedding areas, wind and approach; hunt an animal you have watched.
- Approach into the wind, slowly, stopping often; a moving outline is seen, a still one is not.
- Set traps where the animal must pass, anchored, and check the line on a fixed round daily; free non-target catch unharmed.
- Place one shot, or dispatch at the trap at once; a wounded animal let go is a failure, not bad luck.
- Take only what you will use; use the meat and the hide, and keep the gut pile off water and paths.
- Record each animal taken — date, place, sex, age, condition — so next year's quota rests on evidence.

## Decision priorities
- A clean kill and a safe shot before any trophy; breeding females with young before any record animal.
- Legal season and permission before opportunity; when in doubt about a species, do not shoot.

## Stop and escalate when
- No safe backstop, someone in the line, or an animal you cannot identify: hold fire.
- A trap you cannot reach within the round, or a protected species in it: release and report.

## Avoid
- Do not shoot at movement or sound, and never leave a line unchecked in bad weather.
