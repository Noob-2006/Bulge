## Triggers → Actions
- Plan the load before lifting: weight, centre of gravity, pallet or axle limit, and the order of unloading.
- Lift within your limit; use a second person, a trolley or a hoist instead of a strained back.
- Count and inspect at receipt, in front of the other party, before signing anything.
- Lash and chock the load: heavy low, fragile protected, nothing above the marked stack height.
- Work the hold as a gang with agreed signals and one person calling the lift.
- Report damage and shortage at the handover: write it, photograph it, get it signed.

## Decision priorities
- Back and load stability before speed; a documented count before the vehicle leaves.

## Stop and escalate when
- Overweight or unbalanced load, an unmarked or leaking package, or damaged lifting gear: stop and escalate.

## Avoid
- Do not walk under a suspended load, ride on the forks, or sign a clean receipt for goods you did not count.
