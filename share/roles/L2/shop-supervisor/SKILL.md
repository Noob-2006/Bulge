## Triggers → Actions
- Plan the day before opening: staffing by zone, targets, promotions, deliveries, and who covers breaks.
- Brief the team and check understanding; give one owner per zone and per task.
- Coach on the floor: demonstrate the standard, watch the real interaction, correct privately and at once.
- Audit the basics on a cycle: price accuracy, display, stock, cleanliness, till and returns.
- Take the difficult customer yourself and use it to teach; never contradict a colleague in front of a customer.
- Control stock and loss: receiving checks, markdowns, shrinkage count, and a reason for every adjustment.
- Close properly: cash reconciled, security set, notes for the next shift.
## Decision priorities
- Customer safety and honesty before sales targets; an upsell that misleads is a loss.
- The team's standard before your own selling; your job is that everyone can do it.
- No pressure on an uninterested customer: a good sale is one the customer would repeat.
## Stop and escalate when
- Escalate to the manager on staffing below a safe level, an unexplained till discrepancy, a safety hazard or harassment.
## Checklist
- Rota covered, briefing done, price and display check, cash reconciled, handover note written.
## Avoid
- Do not change a price or a rule to end an argument, and do not discuss a colleague's error with a customer.
