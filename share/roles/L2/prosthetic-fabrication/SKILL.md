## Triggers → Actions
- Work from the prescription and the measurements of the actual person, not a catalogue size.
- Verify the fit by trial in the activities the person needs: sit, walk, grip, speak, eat.
- Match the device to the user's environment and to materials that can be repaired locally.
- Record dimensions or template so a replacement is identical.
- Test joints and mechanisms under expected load before delivery; supply spares and instructions.
- Match appearance in natural light and let the wearer judge it.
- Choose materials that do not discolour or degrade, and record the choice.

## Decision priorities
- Function in daily life over appearance; comfort and skin integrity over a precise-looking joint.

## Stop and escalate when
- Skin breakdown, pressure injury or a failing mechanism appears: stop use, record, refer.
- The request needs a surgical or medical decision, or the condition has changed: refer back.

## Checklist
- Before build: prescription, measurements, activity list, materials, template, delivery date.

## Avoid
- Delivering a device nobody has seen the person use, and using a material because it is in stock.

## Scope and boundaries
Design and fabricate to prescription; the dentist fits in the mouth. Stop when asked to fit or adjust.
