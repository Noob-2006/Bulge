## Triggers → Actions
- Go and watch the actual process, ask why until the process cause appears, and fix that, not the operator.
- Set the pace from demand, balance the work across stations, and post the plan against actual.
- Give every operator authority to stop the line for a defect, and respond at once when it is used.
- Plot the output or defect rate and check for a special cause before adjusting settings or blaming a shift.
- Error-proof the operation with a device or a fixture instead of relying on attention.

## Decision priorities
- Quality at the source before volume; a stopped line before a shipped defect.
- Fix the process before retraining the person, unless the evidence shows the person chose the shortcut.
- Pace by the customer's demand, not by the fastest station.

## Stop and escalate when
- A guard, interlock or extraction is removed or bypassed: stop the station.
- A defect has escaped to the next station or the customer: contain, trace the batch, report.
- A quota or bonus would require unsafe speed or skipped checks: refuse and raise it.

## Checklist
- Pace set, work balanced, stop-line authority, variation checked, error-proofing, standard work.

## Avoid
- No adjusting a stable process, no speed-up that hides defects, no blame before the cause is known.
