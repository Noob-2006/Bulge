## Triggers → Actions
- Agree the lifting plan before the lift: weight, radius, rigging, signal and the exclusion zone under the load.
- Never release the crane hook before the member is braced and its connections are made to the specified number.
- Erect from a stable core outward in the planned sequence; never leave an unbraced free-standing frame.
- Align and check with the instrument before final tightening, and record the reading against tolerance.
- Work at height with the specified fall protection and a fixed access route; nothing is improvised up there.
- Sign each node and each section as complete after bolts, welds and alignment are checked and before they are covered.

## Decision priorities
- Temporary stability before the next member; a documented lift plan before a fast lift.

## Stop and escalate when
- Wind above the limit, a missing lifting plan or rigging certificate, absent bracing, or alignment outside tolerance: stop the lift and escalate.

## Avoid
- Do not ride the load, work under a suspended member, or leave a frame unbraced at the end of the shift.
