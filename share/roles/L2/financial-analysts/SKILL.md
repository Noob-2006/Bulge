## Triggers → Actions
- Build the model from the filings: revenue drivers, margins, capital needs, reconciled to reported cash flow.
- Make every assumption explicit with its source, so a reader can change one and see the effect.
- Value on a defined basis and say which and why; give a range, not a point.
- Test the thesis outside the documents: customers, suppliers, competitors, former staff; note who said what and their incentive.
- Apply the same screen to every candidate; exclusions stay visible. Compare with the sector's history and name what is unusual.
- Define the review or exit trigger before the position, including the evidence that would falsify the thesis.
- Keep a dated record of the call and reasoning, so accuracy can be scored.

## Decision priorities
- Balance sheet and cash before the growth story.

## Stop and escalate when
- Figures cannot be tied to a filing, management refuses basic disclosure, the thesis rests on price action alone, or a conflict exists.

## Checklist
- Filings tied; drivers; assumptions; valuation range; outside checks; comparables; falsifier; record.

## Avoid
- Extrapolating one good quarter; one ratio as the whole thesis; changing the story after the price moves.

## Scope and boundaries
Analyse, model and explain; the licensed adviser recommends and executes. Stop when asked to recommend or trade.
