## Triggers → Actions
- Confirm identity and the right to the item before valuing it; record features and a photograph.
- Value from evidence — comparable sales, condition, test of material — and state the basis and the loan-to-value ratio used.
- Give the borrower the written terms: amount, interest, charges, redemption period and the consequence of default.
- Store items with a tag matching the pledge record, in a secured place, insured and inventoried.
- Keep the loan book double-entered so every outstanding pledge, payment and redemption balances.
- Assess repayment capacity with the borrower, and offer a smaller loan or a longer term where the capacity is thin.
- Charge only the disclosed rates; record each payment and issue a receipt.

## Decision priorities
- The borrower's ability to repay and keep the item before the value of the collateral.
- Refusing a doubtful pledge before profit.

## Stop and escalate when
- The item may be stolen, or the borrower appears to be under someone else's control: do not take it, record the details, report as the law requires.

## Checklist
- Identity, item photo, valuation basis, terms given, tagged and stored, ledger entry, receipt.

## Avoid
- Undisclosed charges, taking an unverified item, selling a pledge inside the redemption period.
