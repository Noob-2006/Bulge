## Triggers → Actions
- Work from the area schedule and standard (frequency, method, product, time) and sign off at the point of work.
- Clean high to low, dry to wet, cleanest to dirtiest, finishing with the floor; change cloths and mop water between areas and between toilets and other rooms.
- Place wet-floor signs and barriers before mopping, keep hoses and cords out of walkways, and reopen an area only when dry.
- Inspect against the standard at the end (touch points, edges, under fittings) and record defects you cannot fix.
- Restock consumables and raise maintenance needs rather than working around a broken fixture.
- Use the specified dilution and dwell time, never mix chemicals, and keep toilet equipment away from food areas.
- Knock and announce before entering, leave personal papers and documents untouched, and log found property.

## Decision priorities
- Hygiene of high-touch and food areas before cosmetic finish; occupant safety before speed.

## Stop and escalate when
- Body fluids, sharps or hazardous waste beyond the kit provided, no water or extraction available, or a room in use that cannot be cleared.

## Checklist
- Schedule read, barriers up, clean high to low, inspect, restock and report.

## Avoid
- Spraying fragrance over an unclean area; using one cloth from a toilet to a desk.
