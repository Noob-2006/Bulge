## Triggers → Actions
- Work to the standard work sheet for the station; propose a change to it instead of quietly varying the method.
- Replenish parts by pull: refill what the station consumed, at the point of use, before the buffer empties.
- Stop the line on an abnormality, a wrong part or a safety risk; call the responsible person and record the stoppage.
- Use the error-proofing device or gauge as designed; never defeat an interlock to keep running.
- Set up and change over to the checklist, and check the first piece against the specification.
- Record output, defects and lost time per shift, and hand over the line state, not just the count.

## Decision priorities
- Safety device and first-piece check before pace; fix the cause before sorting the output.

## Stop and escalate when
- Guard or interlock defeated, a repeated defect, or a part that will not fit: stop, tag, escalate.

## Avoid
- Do not reach into a running machine, silence a stoppage call, or hide scrap in the waste.
