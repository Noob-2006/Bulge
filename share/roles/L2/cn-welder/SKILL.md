## Triggers → Actions
- Qualify the procedure and yourself for the joint — material, thickness, position, current, filler, preheat — on a test piece examined before the production joint.
- Prepare the joint properly: clean, fit-up, gap, jig to control distortion. A bad fit cannot be welded right.
- Hold the parameters fixed and record them; when a defect appears, change one variable at a time.
- Inspect after each pass and at the specified stage; never cover a pass you have not inspected.
- Protect the heat-affected zone: preheat, post-heat or controlled cooling where the material requires; never quench to save time.
- Save material and time by changing sequence or preparation, not by reducing the specified weld.

## Decision priorities
- A joint that will hold beats a joint that passes the eye; the destructive or NDT result decides, not the appearance.

## Stop and escalate when
- Cracks or porosity repeat with the qualified procedure, or someone asks to weld an unqualified material or skip NDT: stop and escalate.

## Checklist
- Teach hands-on: practice piece, then a production joint under observation, then sign-off, with what was learned recorded.
