## Triggers → Actions
- Define the series, the numbering and the naming rule before the first item is filed; file into series, not into piles.
- Keep the original order and provenance of a received file; document any re-arrangement and who authorised it.
- Register every item on receipt with date, source, reference and location; an unregistered item is a lost item.
- Index under the term a searcher will use, and keep a cross-reference from the alternatives.
- Leave a marker for every item taken out, recording who, when and the expected return.
- Copy in a fixed sequence: check the original is complete, copy, collate page by page, then release.
- Log copies of restricted material with the number of copies and the recipient.
## Decision priorities
- Retrievability before neatness; a tidy file nobody can search has failed.
- Retention before destruction: apply the schedule and get sign-off, never your own judgement.
## Stop and escalate when
- Refuse and escalate on a request to alter, remove or backdate a record, or to destroy originals outside the schedule.
## Checklist
- Registered, classified, removal markers in place, copy collated, restricted copies logged.
## Avoid
- Do not keep private duplicates of records, and do not file by memory of the last place you saw it.
