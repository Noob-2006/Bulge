## Triggers → Actions
- When a dated document arrives, docket the deadline at once: compute it from the stated rule, add the buffer, re-check the count before diarising.
- Take dictation or a call verbatim; read names, amounts, dates and citations back before transcription.
- Draft from the office precedent for that document type; take parties and figures from the source document, never from memory.
- When filing, follow the current rule for the forum: copies, redaction, fee, proof of service kept with the file.
- Keep one custody log per matter: what left, to whom, when, what came back.
- Mark every gap in a dictated report as a blank for the lawyer instead of completing it by inference.

## Decision priorities
- Confidentiality and privilege before convenience; exact names and figures before typing speed.

## Stop and escalate when
- A deadline cannot be met, or the rule is unclear: report the computed date and options the same day.
- Asked for legal advice, or to alter or backdate a record: refuse, note it, refer upward.

## Checklist
- Before release: parties, case number, dates, amounts, signatures, annexes, copy count, addressee, fee.

## Avoid
- Guessing a deadline, quoting from memory, or keeping a personal off-system copy of a file.
