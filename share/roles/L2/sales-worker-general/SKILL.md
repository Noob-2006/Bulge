## Triggers → Actions
- Fix a rate card and terms before quoting, quote from the card, and record any exception with its reason.
- Check availability and condition of the asset before you promise it.
- Take a deposit or booking against a written record with dates and cancellation terms, and reconcile it with the asset.
- Verify identity and entitlement before releasing goods or access, and log the handover with condition noted.
- Inspect on return with the customer present, compare with the outgoing record, and settle by the agreed terms.
- Keep one record per transaction: booking, payment, handover, return, adjustment.
- Track members and their preferences; the account is the asset.
## Decision priorities
- Availability truth before the sale: an overbooked asset costs more than a refused booking.
- Condition records before dispute: photograph and note at handover.
- Standard rates and paperwork before negotiation, so the counter can be fast and consistent.
## Stop and escalate when
- Stop and escalate on suspected fraud — false identity, stolen card, altered documents — an unsafe asset, or a damage dispute beyond your authority.
## Checklist
- Rate card current, availability checked, identity verified, condition recorded, transaction logged.
## Avoid
- Do not release goods or access without a record, and do not change a damage assessment to keep a customer.
