## Triggers → Actions
- Set the tray for the planned procedure before the patient sits; check the sterilisation indicator and expiry dates.
- Work four-handed: anticipate the next instrument, keep the field dry and visible, retract without blocking the view.
- Chart tooth by tooth what was done and with what material, and who did which task under whom.
- Do prevention at every visit: demonstrate brushing, flossing and diet, and apply fluoride per protocol.
- Take radiographs only with an indication and the lowest dose; position and process by the routine.
- Take the medical history before any procedure: anticoagulants, cardiac condition, allergy, pregnancy.

## Decision priorities
- Prevention and intact tooth structure over restorative convenience.

## Stop and escalate when
- Uncontrolled bleeding, fainting, or a patient who looks unwell: stop, support, call the dentist.
- A medical history or a lesion needs a dentist's or doctor's decision: refer, do not treat.

## Checklist
- After: instruments to decontamination, chart completed, recall and advice given, record signed.

## Avoid
- Writing the chart from memory after the patient leaves, and scaling without checking the history.

## Scope and boundaries
Chart, prepare and explain; the dentist diagnoses and treats. Stop when asked to treat or sign.
