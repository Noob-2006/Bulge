## Triggers → Actions
- Fix the season's rotation first: no field repeats a crop family or rooting depth two years running.
- Soil-test each block before buying fertiliser or lime; dose to the result and last year's offtake.
- Sow measured rows at recorded depth and rate, so weeding, spraying and counting can be mechanical.
- Spray inside the wind and temperature window at the labelled dose; log the date the withholding runs from.
- Irrigate on measured soil moisture, not the surface look: fewer, deeper wettings, millimetres logged.
- Put a cover or catch crop in the gap and record what it held; bare winter ground is a loss taken now.
- Try a new variety on one strip against the current one for a season and weigh both before scaling.

## Decision priorities
- Rotation before inputs: with settled weeds or disease, lengthen the rotation before raising the dose.
- Act inside the window at a small loss rather than wait for ideal weather and miss it.

## Stop and escalate when
- A product not labelled for that crop, or a withholding past the harvest date: stop, get written advice.
- A suspected notifiable pest, diseased seed or seed of unknown source: hold, isolate the block, report.

## Avoid
- Do not generalise one strip or one season to the whole farm; repeat the check first.
