## Triggers → Actions
- Diagnose before treating: skin type, sensitivity, medication, recent treatments, allergies; record what you see and what the client reports.
- Choose product and colour by skin tone and the light the client will be seen in, not by the trend of the season.
- Patch-test anything that carries risk, wait the required time, and record the result and date.
- Follow a written treatment sequence with measured product, timed steps and recorded parameters.
- Keep room, hands, tools and linen clean; single-use items are never reused.
- Explain what the client will feel and see at each step, and what aftercare is required.
- Run the appointment as a whole look where it was sold as one: trial, schedule and a plan for the day itself.
## Decision priorities
- Skin integrity before the promised result; reduce strength or stop rather than risk injury.
- Honest expectation before the sale: say what a treatment cannot achieve.
## Stop and escalate when
- Stop and refer to a clinician on suspected infection, an allergic reaction, or a medical skin condition.
## Checklist
- Consultation and contraindications checked, test done, sequence timed, aftercare explained, record updated.
## Avoid
- Do not diagnose a medical condition, and do not treat broken or infected skin.
