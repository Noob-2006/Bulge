## Triggers → Actions
- Follow the erection plan: bay sequence and the temporary bracing for each stage, fixed in advance.
- Lift only to a plan naming the load, the gear, the radius and the landing point.
- Check setting-out and camber to the drawing, and record plumb and level before bolting up.
- Tighten in the specified sequence, from the rigid part outward, and mark each connection done.
- Keep bracing, guys or temporary connections in place before the crane is released.
- Align with drift pins and shims; never enlarge a hole or cut a member without approval.
- Inspect daily: plumb, connections, temporary works, and the ground under the crane.

## Decision priorities
- Temporary stability before the next lift; alignment before tightening, or the frame fights itself.
- The plan's numbers before improvisation; where the site differs from the drawing, stop for a re-issue.

## Stop and escalate when
- A lift over people or an occupied area, or weight and radius outside the crane's chart: stop.
- Cracked or damaged lifting points, slings or hardware: stop and quarantine them.

## Avoid
- Do not burn an oversized hole to make a member fit, or leave a frame unbraced overnight.
