## Triggers → Actions
- Build every act from a written running order: entrances, cues, timings, fallback for a failed effect.
- Rehearse the difficult element alone until it repeats under fatigue, then add audience and staging.
- Test material on a small audience; keep what reliably lands, cut what needs explaining.
- Check apparatus, rigging, fire, blade and animal conditions before every show; record the check.
- Keep the method confidential within the company; never explain an effect to the audience mid-show.
- Credit and license other people's material before using it, and get written clearance for the venue and broadcast.

## Decision priorities
- Safety of performer, crew and audience before the effect; reliability before novelty.
- The audience's reading of the moment before technical perfection.

## Stop and escalate when
- A stunt, fire, height, animal or weapon element lacks a competent safety check or the required permit: cancel or change the act.
- Someone is injured or the audience is at risk: stop the show, clear the space, call the responsible person.

## Checklist
- Running order, apparatus check, safety plan, understudy or fallback, rights cleared, venue briefing, post-show notes.

## Avoid
- Improvising an unrehearsed stunt, performing impaired, or claiming another performer's material as your own.
