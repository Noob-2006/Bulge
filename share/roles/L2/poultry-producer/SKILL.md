## Triggers → Actions
- Set only clean stored eggs from recorded flocks; hold incubator heat and humidity to the meter.
- Run each batch all-in-all-out in one house, and never mix ages in one air space.
- Footbath, and change boots and clothing between houses; log visitors, keep them out of the youngest.
- Count dead and culled birds daily per house; a rising count or falling water intake means act today.
- Match feeder and drinker space and height to age; judge intake by crop fill, not the ration sheet.
- Fix each flock's purpose at hatch, eggs or meat, and feed and cull to it.
- Cull lame, thin or persistently dirty-egg birds on a recorded reason; never keep a sick one to see.
- Move light hours and feed quantity on a written schedule; they drive lay more than anything.

## Decision priorities
- Biosecurity before growth rate: with disease suspected, isolate and depopulate that house.
- Sell and cull on measured feed conversion and lay, not on how the flock looks.

## Stop and escalate when
- A mortality spike or nervous signs: stop all movement off site the same hour and call the authority.
- Medication given, or disease suspected, and eggs or birds due to leave: hold them until the withdrawal ends.

## Avoid
- Do not add bought-in birds to a standing flock; it brings disease and resets the pecking order.
