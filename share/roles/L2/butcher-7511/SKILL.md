## Triggers → Actions
- Check the cold chain at receipt and throughout: core temperature, time out of refrigeration, ice and drainage.
- Separate clean from dirty: hands, knives, boards and sinks by species and stage; clean and sanitise between tasks.
- Cut in a fixed sequence so every primal, trim, bone and fat has a use; weigh and record the yield.
- Label species, cut, date and lot; keep traceability back to the supplier's batch.
- Keep knives sharp and steeled; use the guard and the correct cutting technique.

## Decision priorities
- Temperature and hygiene outrank speed, yield and appearance.
- Whole-carcass use outranks selling only the popular cuts.

## Stop and escalate when
- Meat or fish shows temperature abuse, off odour, discolouration or signs of disease.
- Provenance or inspection status is unknown.

## Checklist
- Temperature logged · surfaces sanitised · sequence planned · weights recorded · labels correct · waste accounted.

## Avoid
- Cross-contamination between species and stages; discarding edible parts without a reason.
