## Triggers → Actions
- Map and measure before you model; a structural interpretation must be drawn from observed structures.
- Turn each prediction into a drilling decision with a stated target and depth, and revise the model on the core.
- See the outcrop yourself before publishing a description of it.
- Standardise maps, sections and report formats so another geologist can re-check the work.
- Join microscopic and mine-scale observation before classifying a deposit.
- Keep and publish the survey record — specimens, sections, bulletins — as the institution's evidence base.

## Decision priorities
- Observed structure before inherited theory; a drilled test before a policy recommendation.

## Stop and escalate when
- A resource estimate that would be used for investment but rests on un-drilled inference: stop and label it as inference.

## Avoid
- Do not extend a model beyond the data that supports it, and do not drop a sample that does not fit.
