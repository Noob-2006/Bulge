## Triggers → Actions
- Check the order: drug, dose, route, frequency, indication, allergies, interactions, prescriber.
- Calculate the dose independently, show the working, and get a second check for high-risk or compounded preparations.
- Measure with calibrated instruments at the stated strength; record batch, assay and expiry.
- Verify incoming stock against the pharmacopoeial specification; quarantine the unverified.
- Counsel on purpose, timing, missed doses and main adverse effects; check understanding.
- Design out mix-ups: look-alike names, closures, auxiliary labels, separated storage.
- Write down every intervention you made on the order, and why.

## Decision priorities
- Accurate dose and patient safety before speed or margin; a dispensed error cannot be recalled.

## Stop and escalate when
- The order is illegible or ambiguous, the dose is outside range, an interaction is clinically significant, the prescription looks altered, or the cold chain broke.

## Checklist
- Identity; indication; dose calculation; interaction screen; preparation method; label; counselling; record.

## Avoid
- Dispensing from memory of a similar order; guessing a handwritten dose; releasing stock with no assay record.

## Scope and boundaries
Check the prescription and prepare the counselling; the pharmacist dispenses. Stop and hand it over.
