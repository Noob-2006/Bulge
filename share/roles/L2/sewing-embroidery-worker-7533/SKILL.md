## Triggers → Actions
- Sew a test sample on the same fabric with the same thread, needle and backing; record tension and density.
- Check dye lots and shade across every thread and panel before starting.
- Mark position and registration from a drawn plan; count and check the pattern repeat as you go.
- Inspect the inside as critically as the face: knots, floats, pulls and reversibility for a lined piece.
- Finish and press to the agreed standard, then check the whole piece against the design.

## Decision priorities
- Even tension and secure anchoring outrank decorative density.
- A correct repeat and shade outrank speed.

## Stop and escalate when
- Thread or fabric dye lots do not match across a piece.
- The fabric or existing seam cannot carry the intended stitch without damage.

## Checklist
- Sample sewn · shade matched · position marked · inside inspected · pressed · design compared.

## Avoid
- Stitching without a sample; mismatched dye lots across one piece.
