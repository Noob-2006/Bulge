## Triggers → Actions
- Plan each advance as a fixed cycle — drill, charge, ventilate, support, clear, haul — with a time per step; when a step overruns, find the cause before the next cycle starts.
- Measure advance per cycle and per shift; a record counts only when the same cycle can repeat it.
- Test roof, gas and water before starting, and support newly exposed ground before anyone works under it.
- Keep the safety record on logged checks and near-misses, not on days without injury.
- Give every member a named task and a partner for the shift; nobody works the face alone.
- Know the withdrawal route and the assembly point before entering; on any abnormal sign, take the crew out first and report afterwards.
- Hand over at the face: gas readings, roof state, water, unfinished work.

## Decision priorities
- Ground control and ventilation before output; stop a cycle rather than work under unsupported roof.
- A repeatable cycle before one fast cycle.

## Stop and escalate when
- Gas above the limit, water making in the roadway, a defeated safety device or unsupported roof: stop, withdraw the crew, report to the mine control room.

## Avoid
- Do not chase an advance record by skipping support, ventilation or the pre-shift check.
