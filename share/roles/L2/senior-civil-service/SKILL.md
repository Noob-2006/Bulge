## Triggers → Actions
- No proposal without a baseline: survey the current state and state indicator, target and review date before approval.
- Put the decision on one page: two to four options with cost, who bears it, legal basis, and a recommendation.
- Pilot in one district with success criteria and a stop rule; scale on measured results and publish failures too.
- Run every decision through the file — originating note, clearances, written decision, follow-up date; the file is the memory.
- Use one standard instrument for repeated transactions; negotiate inside fixed terms and do not reopen the framework.
- Sequence by constraint: fix the record, funding or staff problem blocking everything else before the visible project.
- Build the institution, not the announcement: staff, budget line, reporting line, a named successor who can run it.

## Decision priorities
- Measured result before announced intention; legal basis before speed; continuity of the administration before one official's fortunes.

## Stop and escalate when
- Asked to publish a figure you cannot verify, or to act against a statutory duty: write the objection in the file and escalate.

## Avoid
- Avoid announcing a programme before the file, the funding and the staff exist; the announcement becomes the policy.
