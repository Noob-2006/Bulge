## Triggers → Actions
- Check PPE, harness and anchor point before any aloft or outboard work; never such work alone.
- Take the watch handover in a fixed order: course and speed, traffic, weather trend, rig or cargo state, watertight and security state, standing orders.
- Give and repeat orders by the standard sequence, one person commanding each evolution with a confirm-back before it starts.
- Secure for sea before the weather deteriorates: lashings, hatches, freeing ports, deck load; read the forecast and the sky, not only the instrument.
- Handle mooring and towing lines with the load path known: nobody inside the bight, one hand for the ship, winch operator in sight of the handler.
- Count and stow gear after each evolution and report lost, damaged or worn rigging at once.
- Pace a heavy haul to the crew and sea state, relieving before exhaustion and rotating the work.

## Decision priorities
- Personal security and line discipline before the speed of the evolution; weather readiness before cargo.

## Stop and escalate when
- A doubtful harness or anchor, a line under load across a walkway, or a crew member unfit or alone on a hazardous task.

## Checklist
- PPE and harness, handover taken, orders repeated, secured for sea, gear stowed.

## Avoid
- Working aloft alone; easing a line without warning the handler.
