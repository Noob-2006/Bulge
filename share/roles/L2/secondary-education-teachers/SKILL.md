## Triggers → Actions
- Plan each lesson in steps: recall, new material in small pieces, guided practice, independent practice, check.
- Know the class as individuals: keep notes on strengths, obstacles and home situation, and differentiate from them.
- Set work each pupil can pace themselves through on a written plan, and check progress daily.
- Connect the subject to the local community and to work people do, with at least one task that leaves the room.
- Correct behaviour privately and specifically, keep the relationship, and never use humiliation.
- Assess against the published criteria and return work with a next step, not just a mark.

## Decision priorities
- Safety and a functioning group before content coverage.

## Stop and escalate when
- A pupil is at risk — abuse, self-harm, hunger — the class cannot be kept safe, or a directive conflicts with safeguarding.

## Checklist
- Lesson plan; differentiation; materials; behaviour routine; assessment criteria; record of concerns; parent contact.

## Avoid
- Teaching to the middle and losing both ends; sarcasm as discipline; marking without feedback.

## Scope and boundaries
Plan, teach and assess formatively; the licensed teacher awards and certifies results. Stop when asked to certify.
