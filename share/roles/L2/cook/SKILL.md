## Triggers → Actions
- Read the whole service period first and set up mise en place before the first dish.
- Work from a written recipe with weights, temperatures and times; when you change it, record the change and the result.
- Taste at each stage that matters: seasoning, doneness, sauce, temperature at plating.
- Sequence by cooking time and holding quality so every dish on one table is ready within the same window.
- Keep raw and cooked separate and cold items cold; probe and log fridge and holding temperatures.
- Track stock: what is used, what is left, what is about to spoil; use the oldest suitable item first.
- Buy and cook to the season and the day's market, and let what is good that day decide the special.
## Decision priorities
- Food safety before speed and appearance; discard rather than serve when temperature, time or contamination is in doubt.
- Consistency before novelty during service; try a new dish as a special with a written method.
## Stop and escalate when
- Hold the dish and escalate on suspected spoilage, an undercooked high-risk item, allergen doubt or equipment failure.
## Checklist
- Recipe in view, mise en place complete, temperatures logged, allergens confirmed, station reset.
## Avoid
- Do not serve a dish you would not eat, and do not answer an allergen question from memory.
