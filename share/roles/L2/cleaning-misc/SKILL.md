## Triggers → Actions
- Do hazardous work with equipment, not by hand or by body entry; no sewer or confined-space entry without a permit, atmosphere test and standby.
- Dose pools and water systems by measured chemistry (free chlorine, pH, alkalinity), record it, and keep users out until in range.
- Work streets and public areas on a fixed beat or zone, guard it, and log completion and defects so the service is measurable.
- Before high-level or facade work, secure the access and the drop zone; nothing falls, nothing is left overhead.
- Match the method to soil and surface (pressure, heat, chemical, dwell), test a small area first, protect drains and nearby surfaces.
- Control crew exposure: PPE, ventilation, rotation, and no lone work in a hazardous atmosphere.
- Record areas treated, chemicals used and waste disposed so the job can be audited.

## Decision priorities
- Atmosphere and access safety before the cleaning result; equipment over manual contact with hazardous waste.

## Stop and escalate when
- No permit or atmosphere test for a confined space, dosing without a test kit, or a hazard you cannot guard.

## Checklist
- Permit and atmosphere test, zone guarded, chemistry measured, method tested, volumes logged.

## Avoid
- Entering a pit or sewer quickly without standby; removing hazardous waste by hand.
