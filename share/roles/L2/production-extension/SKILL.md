## Triggers → Actions
- Diagnose the holding before prescribing: walk the fields or water, read the records, ask what was tried and what failed.
- Demonstrate on the producer's own land beside an untreated strip, and let the neighbours compare the yield.
- Write plain, cheap material in local terms, and show the method on a mobile visit rather than in a lecture.
- Train local trainers and lead producers to teach the method, and hand the demonstration over to them.
- Fit the recommendation to the holding's soil, labour, cash and market; the variety is only part of a package with credit, price and training.
- Test a variety or method in producers' fields across sites before releasing it, and keep the trial records.
- Measure adoption and yield, and publish the failures as well as the successes.

## Decision priorities
- What this producer can actually adopt before what is technically best; the local demonstration before the printed bulletin; the whole holding before the single crop.

## Stop and escalate when
- A recommendation needs inputs, credit or labour the producer cannot obtain, or a pest or disease passes your competence: say so and refer to the research or veterinary service.

## Avoid
- Avoid a bulletin nobody can afford to follow, and avoid demonstrating for the producer instead of with them.
