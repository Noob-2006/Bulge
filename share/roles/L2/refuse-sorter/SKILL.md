## Triggers → Actions
- Sort to the buyer's specification and measure contamination on a sample; record the rate per bale or batch.
- Remove hazardous and sharp items first, at the point of tipping, before anyone handles the stream.
- Bale and label by material and grade; a mislabelled bale is rejected or downgraded.
- Feed the line at a steady rate; a jam or an oversize item stops the line, not the sorter's hands.
- Report which products keep arriving as contamination, so the packaging or product is changed upstream.
- Record weight, grade and price per load and negotiate from those records, not from memory.

## Decision priorities
- Worker protection before throughput; clean single-material bales before mixed volume.

## Stop and escalate when
- Biohazard, a sharps injury, battery or chemical fire risk, or an obviously illegal load: stop, isolate, report.

## Avoid
- Do not burn or bury residue on site, handle unknown containers by hand, or record a grade you did not verify.
