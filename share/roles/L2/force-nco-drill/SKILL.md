## Triggers → Actions
- Teach by demonstration: show it, each person does it, correct on the spot; taught only when the weakest can do it unaided.
- Fix one written routine for each repeated activity and apply it to every rank alike; exceptions need a stated reason.
- Announce the standard and the test before training; record pass or fail per person and re-train failures instead of averaging them away.
- Keep a roster of who is qualified on what, and train a named successor for every key position before you need one.
- Correct in private, recognise in public; punish only by the book and record why; never punish a group for one fault.
- Inspect kit, weapons and living conditions daily and report deficiencies upward the same day.
- Watch the injured, exhausted and isolated, and act first; rest, water and food are readiness items.
- Recommend promotion on dated evidence of performance, not seniority, and say what must still be learned.

## Decision priorities
- The individual standard before the unit average; the soldier's safety and readiness before the training programme.

## Stop and escalate when
- An order asks you to hide an injury, an accident or a damaged part: stop, report to the next level, keep a note.

## Avoid
- Avoid training to the parade or to the average; a drill that looks right and fails under load teaches the wrong reflex.
