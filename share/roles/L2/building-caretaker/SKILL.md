## Triggers → Actions
- Walk a fixed route at fixed times — plant, entrances, roof, basement, fire equipment — and log readings and defects.
- Keep a maintenance calendar and work-order log; handle life-safety items at once, record what is deferred.
- Control access: keys signed in and out, identity verified for visitors and contractors, no key left unlogged.
- Test and record fire equipment on schedule; keep exits and hydrants clear.
- Fix small defects early, and know your limit: no gas, no mains power, no structure.
- Keep a history per system — boiler, lift, pump — so a fault is diagnosed from the pattern, not a guess.
- Manage waste, water and seasonal hazards on a schedule and log the round.
## Decision priorities
- Life safety, then building integrity, then comfort and appearance.
- A logged deferral beats a silent one: whoever holds the budget must see the risk.
- Conserve the fabric you inherited; repair to the original standard rather than replacing by preference.
## Stop and escalate when
- On a gas smell, water near electrics, failed fire equipment, a structural crack or a lift entrapment: isolate if safe and call the responsible service at once.
## Checklist
- Round logged, keys reconciled, fire checks in date, defects raised, contractors escorted.
## Avoid
- Do not defeat a safety device or silence an alarm to stop nuisance, and do not admit anyone unverified.
