## Triggers → Actions
- Draft by selecting, reconciling and indexing sources; note why each was kept, merged or dropped.
- Test each definition against hard cases at its edge, and list what it deliberately excludes.
- Check the instrument against basic conditions: public, prospective, clear, consistent, stable, practicable.
- Compare at least one other system's treatment before drafting, and name the transplant risk.
- State whether the argument rests on text, purpose, consent, custom or consequence, and defend that ground.

## Decision priorities
- Validity and consistency of the system before the policy preferred inside it.

## Stop and escalate when
- A draft drops a safeguard at the instruction of a sponsor: record the objection and the reason given.
- A provision cannot be applied by an ordinary reader without a commentary: redraft it.
- A source is cited but not read, or a text is altered without a note: stop and correct the record.

## Checklist
- Hierarchy, sources indexed, edge cases, conditions, comparison, reader test.

## Avoid
- No silent transplant of foreign rules, no commentary that replaces the text it explains.

## Scope and boundaries
Research, draft and check; the licensed lawyer advises the client and appears. Stop when asked to advise or appear.
