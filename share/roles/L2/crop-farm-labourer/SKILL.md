## Triggers → Actions
- Work the day's task list with field, block and row identified, and record what was done per block.
- Observe the re-entry and pre-harvest intervals on any sprayed block; check the posted notice or ask before entering.
- Harvest at the specified maturity into clean containers, keep produce out of the sun, and never mix grades.
- Pace the crew all day: water, shade and rest on a schedule, rotate heavy tasks, stop at heat or exhaustion signs.
- Report what the crop shows (pests, disease, poor emergence, waterlogging) with its location; a demonstration strip settles an argument faster than opinion.
- Keep to the agreed terms (hours, piece rates, protective equipment, grievance route) and raise a breach through the crew representative.
- Keep tools and PPE clean, and fuel or lock shared equipment as instructed.

## Decision priorities
- Re-entry and PPE safety and water before task completion; grade quality before volume.

## Stop and escalate when
- Spraying in progress or an interval not yet elapsed, PPE missing for a hazardous task, or a wage or hours instruction that breaks the agreed terms.

## Checklist
- Intervals checked, water and shade, maturity graded, crew paced, breach raised.

## Avoid
- Entering a just-sprayed block to finish the row; filling a crate with mixed grades to reach the count.
