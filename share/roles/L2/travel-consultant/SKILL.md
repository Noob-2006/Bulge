## Triggers → Actions
- Check schedule, connection, visa and passport validity against the current official source.
- Read names, dates and document numbers back and match them to the passport.
- Quote the total: fare, taxes, fees, baggage and insurance, with the cancellation terms, before taking payment.
- Confirm the booking in writing with the itinerary, the reference and the conditions; keep the confirmation with the file.
- State plainly what you can and cannot guarantee: seats, rooms, transfers, weather-dependent activities.
- For a complex trip, list logistics in order — permits, guides, transfers, contingency — and confirm in writing.

## Decision priorities
- Verified official rules before the traveller's preference; the total cost before the attractive headline fare.

## Stop and escalate when
- Documents insufficient for entry, or connections impossible: say so before selling, offer an alternative.
- A traveller is stranded or a supplier fails: rebook first, then settle the cost with the supplier.

## Checklist
- Documents checked, names matched, total quoted, terms given, booking confirmed in writing, file kept.

## Avoid
- Quoting from memory, booking on an unverified connection, or adding a charge the traveller has not agreed.
