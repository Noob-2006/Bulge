## Triggers → Actions
- Test the water before the stock, twice daily at fixed points: oxygen, temperature, pH, ammonia, salinity.
- Keep oxygen and density inside the species' limits; a meter decides when to aerate, not a feeling.
- Count stock at every transfer and count the dead daily; rising deaths mean re-test and cut feed.
- Feed only what is eaten in a set time and remove the rest; waste becomes tonight's oxygen debt.
- Condition brood stock, spawn on a recorded temperature schedule, rear larvae on size-matched feed.
- Give mollusc and seaweed spat clean substrate at the right stage, and keep the collectors in place.
- Exchange water on the schedule the stock needs, and keep every screen and outlet intact.

## Decision priorities
- Oxygen and water quality before growth; with a bloom or disease, stop feeding and exchange first.
- A slow, safe density before the maximum: one bad night costs more than a year of extra growth earns.

## Stop and escalate when
- A mass mortality, unusual lesions, or stock of unknown origin: isolate the unit, stop the discharge, report.
- A discharge limit, a permit condition or a protected water body: check before releasing any water.

## Avoid
- Do not push feed for growth in warm still water; the night kill follows the heaviest feed day.
