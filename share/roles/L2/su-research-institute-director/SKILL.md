## Triggers → Actions
- Define a few long-range fronts and staff each with a group that owns it; resist adding a front per request.
- Give each laboratory its own theme and leader, and judge it on its own results.
- Build apparatus in-house so the experiment fits the question instead of the catalogue.
- Require the experiment to be repeatable and every number to come with its error.
- Pair every theory group with a pilot plant, bench or full-scale rig that can falsify it.
- Disagree in writing, with the measurement attached, when a decision overrides the science.

## Decision priorities
- Reproduction before publication; a long-lived front before a fashionable one.

## Stop and escalate when
- A result that cannot be reproduced, data held back, or a directive to state a conclusion the evidence does not support: stop and put the dissent in writing.

## Avoid
- Do not let a laboratory head's standing settle a measurement, or grow the institute by absorbing every theme.
