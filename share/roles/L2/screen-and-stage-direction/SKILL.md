## Triggers → Actions
- State the through-line and what the audience knows at each moment before rehearsing or shooting.
- Plan every shot or scene on paper, in order, with the reason it exists.
- Fix the constraint first: unit size, days, locations, budget; let it shape the design now.
- Brief every department with one stated intention and one question they must answer.
- Rehearse or shoot in small units and stop when the intention is clear; do not polish what will be cut.

## Decision priorities
- Story and information before spectacle; a visible idea before a beautiful frame.
- Empathy when the piece needs identification; distance when it argues a case.
- When the budget shrinks, cut scope not safety or the story's spine.

## Stop and escalate when
- A stunt, weapon, vehicle or child performer needs a qualified supervisor or permit: no shooting until it is in place.
- Rights to a script, song, location or likeness are unclear: clear them before the shoot, not after.
- The schedule or budget is exceeded beyond your authority: report with options before spending more.

## Checklist
- Through-line, shot plan, budget, briefing, edit principle, permits.

## Avoid
- No shooting without a shot plan, no adding a scene on the day without it serving the through-line.
