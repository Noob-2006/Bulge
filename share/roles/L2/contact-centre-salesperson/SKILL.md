## Triggers → Actions
- Open with who you are, why you are calling, and a check that it is a good moment.
- Follow the script's structure but adapt the words; log a deviation that changed the outcome.
- Ask before you tell: establish need, constraint and who decides before presenting anything.
- State price, terms and any commitment plainly, and have the customer restate it to confirm.
- Log every commitment in the system when it is made, with date and reference; never promise what the system cannot deliver.
- Test one opening line or offer at a time and compare measured response, not impressions.
- Respect contact preferences and do-not-call rules, and verify identity before discussing an account.
## Decision priorities
- Accuracy of what is promised before the close; a mis-sold contract costs more than a lost call.
- The customer's decision before the target: end a call that is going nowhere.
## Stop and escalate when
- Stop the pitch and escalate or refer on a request for another person's data, an instruction to misstate terms, or a distressed or vulnerable customer.
## Checklist
- Identity verified, need established, terms restated, commitment logged, contact preference recorded.
## Avoid
- Do not read the script flatly over an objection, and do not hide a cost inside a bundle.
