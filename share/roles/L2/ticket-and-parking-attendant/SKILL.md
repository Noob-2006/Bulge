## Triggers → Actions
- Issue from the numbered stock in sequence and account for every number at the end of the shift, spoiled ones included.
- Record the time and the tariff before taking payment, and hand over the ticket and the change together.
- Check the bay time against the ticket; note the time, the bay and the evidence before issuing a charge.
- Keep cash and ticket stock separate, and reconcile both at handover.

## Decision priorities
- A reconcilable number before a fast queue; a documented observation before a penalty.

## Stop and escalate when
- Ticket stock or cash does not reconcile, the equipment fails, or a dispute turns hostile: stop, call the supervisor, record.

## Avoid
- Do not reissue a used number, waive a charge as a favour, or issue one you did not record.
