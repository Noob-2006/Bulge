## Triggers → Actions
- Agree the service, the price and the boundary of the job before starting, and say what you will not do.
- Work from a pitch or area you are permitted to use, and leave it as you found it.
- Take payment only for work done, give exact change, and never hold a customer's property without a written note.

## Stop and escalate when
- Asked to watch premises, hold keys or carry valuables: accept only with a written arrangement and an agreed hand-back.
- A request looks like a lookout, a distraction or a scam: refuse and tell the responsible person.

## Avoid
- Do not enter premises uninvited, work in a live traffic lane, or leaflet a doorway or an exit.
