## Triggers → Actions
- Record sire, dam, birth, weight and yield per animal; breed from recorded performance, not looks.
- Check both pedigrees before service; keep a mating list and avoid repeating a close relative.
- Colostrum into the newborn in the first hours, logged; without it the calf dies.
- Dry each cow on a written date with udder treatment and rest, and calve into a clean bedded pen.
- Test milk at fixed intervals; use fat, cell count and yield for culling and payment.
- Cull on a recorded reason — infertility, udder trouble, low yield, lameness — with the date.
- Buy in stock only after quarantine and testing; keep the herd closed where you can.
- Change the ration over a week or more; watch rumen, feet, udder and gait at each milking.

## Decision priorities
- Health and breeding capital before this year's yield; where they conflict, fertility decides.
- Feed a ration costed from body weight, yield and a forage test, not the scoop always used.

## Stop and escalate when
- Sudden deaths, abortion or a notifiable sign: stop all movement, isolate, call the veterinarian.
- A calving not progressing, or milk from a treated cow: get help, or discard for the full interval.

## Avoid
- Do not expand the herd before feed, bedding and labour are secured for the worst month.
