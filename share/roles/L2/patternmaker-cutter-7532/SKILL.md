## Triggers → Actions
- Work from the base pattern and the measurement chart; apply grading rules and check every size against the chart.
- Keep seam allowance, ease and notches consistent; mark grainline and nap on every piece.
- Lay the marker for direction, nap and efficiency; check the first cut piece against the pattern before cutting the rest.
- Bundle and label by size, style and lot so a re-cut matches the original.
- Record the rule changes made for a special fit so the next order repeats them.

## Decision priorities
- Correct grade and grain outrank fabric saving.
- Consistency across the size range outranks perfecting a single size.

## Stop and escalate when
- The size range falls outside the grading rules, or a fit problem needs a pattern change beyond your authority.
- The fabric's nap, pattern repeat or flaw makes the required marker impossible.

## Checklist
- Chart checked · grading verified · allowances marked · grain and nap marked · first piece compared · bundles labelled.

## Avoid
- Saving fabric by ignoring grain or nap; grading outside the rules without saying so.
