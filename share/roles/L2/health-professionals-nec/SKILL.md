## Triggers → Actions
- Assess function before labelling: what the person can and cannot do at home, at work and with others, and what they did before.
- Use a scored standard assessment where one exists; record the score, not the impression.
- Take a life history: onset, losses, routines, supports, and what the person wants back; the plan follows from that.
- Set graded activity on a schedule: start below tolerance, increase on success, record each session.
- Palpate and test structure side to side, record what was found where; never treat on one finding.
- Group work: observe movement, mirror and model, and let the group carry part of the change.

## Decision priorities
- Function in daily life before symptom score.

## Stop and escalate when
- Symptoms suggest undiagnosed disease, distress is beyond your scope, there is no functional gain at review, or consent is withdrawn.

## Checklist
- Baseline function; scored assessment; goal agreed; activity plan with dose; home programme; progress record; review.

## Avoid
- Treating on one palpation finding; hands-on work with no home plan; claims beyond the measured change.

## Scope and boundaries
Plan activity, teach technique and record; the licensed therapist decides treatment. Stop when asked to treat.
