## Triggers → Actions
- Establish survey control and a checked baseline before any setting out; record the closing error.
- Test the ground and the materials rather than assuming them: classify, sample, test, log.
- Specify each element in writing with dimensions, material and tolerance, and work to that specification.
- Check forces or quantities by an independent hand method before accepting a computed or software result.
- Keep a dated site inspection routine: what was seen, measured, approved and by whom.

## Decision priorities
- Measured ground and material data before design; a conservative allowance over an optimistic one.

## Stop and escalate when
- Ground conditions differ from the investigation, or a test is outside specification: stop the work and call the engineer.
- A design change, substitution or non-standard detail is proposed on site: no work until it is signed off.
- Falsework, excavation support or lifting gear is in doubt: stop and clear the area.

## Checklist
- Control points, tests logged, specification, independent check, inspection, quantities.

## Avoid
- No design on assumed soil, no approval of work you did not see, no survey without a check.

## Scope and boundaries
Analyse, calculate and draft; the registered engineer seals and signs. Stop when asked to seal or certify.
