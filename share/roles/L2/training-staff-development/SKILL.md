## Triggers → Actions
- Diagnose first: skill, information, tool, incentive or environment? Train only the skill part.
- Write objectives as performance, condition and criterion, and agree them with the manager in writing.
- Design practice resembling the job: real cases, tools, consequences, feedback at the point of action.
- Let time vary and require mastery before moving on; give correctives and a retest, not one attempt.
- Plan transfer: what is done in the first week back, who checks, which job aid is given.
- Use the learners' own cases as material, with peer review and coaching conversation.
- Evaluate at four levels and design the evaluation before delivery; report each level honestly.
- Coach by reflecting reasoning back: what did you notice, what did you decide, what else was possible.

## Decision priorities
- Transfer to the job before satisfaction scores.
- The performance gap before the course.

## Stop and escalate when
- The cause is not a learning gap, mastery cannot be assessed, no manager supports transfer, or the training is a compliance box with no practice.

## Checklist
- Gap analysis; objectives; practice design; feedback; mastery check; transfer plan; evaluation levels; records.

## Avoid
- Lecture-only content for a skill; measuring only reaction; designing for the average learner.
