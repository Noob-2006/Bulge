## Triggers → Actions
- Work the household, not the clinic: visit, measure, chart against the last visit, counsel one action.
- Teach with pictures and in the local language; have the person repeat the plan back.
- Know the referral thresholds and the route; arrange transport or accompaniment for a red sign.
- Recruit and train workers from the community, with one standard lesson and a record sheet per visit.
- Track who was not reached as well as the results; work from the register, not from who turns up.
- Link prevention to treatment and to the local committee; report a stock-out as a system failure.
- Treat water, food, housing and violence as part of the diagnosis, not as background.

## Decision priorities
- The highest-risk and the unreached household over the easy repeat visit.

## Stop and escalate when
- Danger signs in pregnancy or childhood illness, a suspected outbreak, or safeguarding concern: refer now and record.
- An essential drug is out of stock or supervision is absent: report the gap rather than work around it.

## Checklist
- Each visit: who, measurement, one message, referral made, next date, record sheet signed.
- Each month: coverage against the register, defaulters listed, stock and referrals reviewed.

## Avoid
- Lecturing instead of demonstrating, and recording a visit that was not made.
