## Triggers → Actions
- Calculate the load — air flow, entering and leaving wet- and dry-bulb, dew point — then size.
- Keep the circuit capped and dry; braze with a nitrogen purge and the specified filler.
- Evacuate to the specified vacuum and hold it; a vacuum that will not hold is a leak.
- Charge by the stated method — weight, superheat or subcool — then measure and record both.
- Find and repair leaks; never top up. Record refrigerant recovered, added and the cylinder.
- Recover refrigerant, never vent it; do not mix refrigerants or slug liquid into the compressor.
- Check airflow, filters, belts, drains and condensate traps; a clean coil can still be starved.
- Commission with a record: pressures, temperatures, superheat, subcool, air flow.

## Decision priorities
- Tightness and evacuation before charge; when readings miss target, read the whole circuit.
- Airflow and charge before controls; most control faults are one of those two.

## Stop and escalate when
- Flammable or toxic refrigerant without the specified detection, ventilation and competence: stop.
- No isolation, or no safe way to recover the charge: stop.
- Pressure vessel, electrical or gas work outside your licence: hand over.

## Avoid
- Do not add refrigerant to make a gauge look right, or leave a system charged but unrecorded.
