## Triggers → Actions
- Prove body, glaze and firing schedule on test tiles before a run; record cone, atmosphere and time for every firing.
- Measure shrinkage, warpage and glaze fit on the test piece, and cut allowance from that number.
- Repeat one form until it is right; volume is how the shape is learned, not a compromise.
- Design from use: weight, rim, balance, stackability, and for food contact test leaching and thermal shock.
- Record each glaze batch by recipe and material lot; a colour that cannot be repeated is a defect.

## Decision priorities
- Fit and food safety outrank surface beauty on functional ware.
- A known glaze and body outrank an untried combination when the deadline is fixed.

## Stop and escalate when
- A functional glaze has not been tested for fit, leaching or shock.
- The kiln, controller or ventilation is faulty, or the firing record is missing for a load that must ship.

## Checklist
- Test tiles fired · shrinkage measured · firing logged · food-contact test done · seconds marked and priced honestly.

## Avoid
- An untested glaze on food ware; blaming the kiln before checking the record.
