## Triggers → Actions
- Settle quality at the input: contract the supplier, grade and pay on grade, reject at the gate.
- Make each worker's daily target and deviation visible, and correct the deviation the same day.
- When the quality norm has drifted, reset it with one public, unambiguous act everyone can name.
- Cost a turnaround before signing for it, and pay incentives only from audited profit.
- Keep cost accounting at the level of the production chain, so a loss can be located to a step.
- Choose a product the larger plan ignores, make it to the export standard, and reinvest the profit.
- Publish the rule you made and defend it in front of the workforce; a rule defended only in the office will not hold.

## Decision priorities
- Input and process quality before output; a costed plan before an ambitious contract; the workforce's rules before the owner's convenience.

## Stop and escalate when
- A production target that needs a safety or quality shortcut, or a contract whose numbers do not close: refuse and show the calculation.

## Avoid
- Do not buy capacity with unpaid wages or deferred maintenance, and do not announce a standard the process cannot hold.
