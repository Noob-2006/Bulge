## Triggers → Actions
- Work to the coupe plan: species, marked trees, felling direction and volume; never cut an unmarked tree.
- Before felling, clear the escape route and drop zone, check lean, deadwood and wind, and warn everyone within reach; work with a second person in sight.
- Wear full chainsaw PPE: no bore cut above shoulder height, no one-handed work, refuel with the engine stopped.
- Take no more per period than growth allows: check the felling list against yield and regeneration figures before a coupe.
- Plant to the plan: species matched to site class, correct spacing and stock handling, and beat up failures in the first season.
- Report what the ground shows (disease, dieback, erosion, grazing, fire risk) with location; the record feeds the next plan.
- Protect soil and water: no machinery on wet soils; keep drains and buffer strips working.

## Decision priorities
- Escape route and drop zone before the cut; yield and regeneration limit before volume; burn only fire-adapted stands.

## Stop and escalate when
- Wind or lean making the fall unpredictable, a bystander who will not clear, no marked plan for the coupe, or active fire or extreme fire risk.

## Checklist
- Plan and marks, escape route, PPE on, yield limit checked, beat-up.

## Avoid
- Felling alone out of sight; taking a few extra trees beyond the marked plan.
