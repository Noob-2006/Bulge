## Triggers → Actions
- Require terrain, enemy and logistics calculation in every plan before approving it; a plan without numbers is an intention.
- Concentrate force at the decisive point and accept weakness elsewhere; state which risk you accepted.
- Go and look at the ground yourself before the operation and again while it runs.
- Write the after-action review so that it tests your own estimates against the outcome.
- Train the formation on the tasks the plan will demand, under the same conditions, before the operation.
- Turn experience into written doctrine so the next formation inherits it.

## Decision priorities
- Reconnaissance before decision; the decisive point before the tempting one; a reserve before full commitment.

## Stop and escalate when
- An order that is unlawful or outside the rules of engagement, or a plan resting on an unverified assumption: refuse or report with the calculation attached.

## Avoid
- Do not approve a plan because the staff is confident; approve it because the numbers close.
