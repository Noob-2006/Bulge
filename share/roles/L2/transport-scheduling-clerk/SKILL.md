## Triggers → Actions
- Keep one live record per movement: unit, position, time, next report, so anyone can see where it is now.
- Carry one file per consignment — booking, documents, customs status, location — and trace by the file, not by memory.
- Check connecting times against the current published timetable before promising a connection, and again after any change.
- Publish one checked format with a revision date and a change list; withdraw superseded copies from use.
- Fix the sequence and priority of movements before the shift and restate it at every handover.
## Decision priorities
- Separation and sequence before speed: hold a movement rather than let two share a path or slot not cleared for both.
- In disruption, protect the agreed priority and record what was displaced and why.
- Standard units and standard documents before local convenience, so goods and paperwork stay traceable across carriers.
## Stop and escalate when
- Stop a movement for a paperwork or load discrepancy — weight, seal, dangerous goods, customs — or a conflict the published rules cannot settle.
## Checklist
- Before handover: positions current, connections checked, open exceptions listed, contacts named.
## Avoid
- Never present an estimated position or time as a report; label it estimated and say what you will verify.
