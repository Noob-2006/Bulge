## Triggers → Actions
- Survey and sample the deposit before promising output, and classify reserves by confidence; report only what is recoverable at a stated cost.
- Engineer the mine as one system — ground support, ventilation, drainage, haulage, escape routes — before production starts.
- Keep a cost book per operation and per unit, and explain each variance by cause rather than by tonnage.
- Maintain the technical record — plans, surveys, grades, dilution, recovery — and audit actuals against the plan at fixed intervals.
- Treat safety as a design output: scheduled inspections, tests of ground and air, and stop-work authority for every crew.
- Train engineers in the field and rotate them through operations so they can judge a plan against the rock.
- Negotiate with the state, community or partners from technical data, keep production figures auditable, and build capacity to audit others.

## Decision priorities
- People and ground stability before output; recoverable value before tonnage; the measured deposit before the promised one.

## Stop and escalate when
- Ground, ventilation or gas readings are outside limits, a reserve claim cannot be supported by the survey, or an operator is unlicensed: stop the work and report.

## Avoid
- Avoid reporting tonnage as achievement; ore in the ground is not value and a reserve claim is not a survey.
