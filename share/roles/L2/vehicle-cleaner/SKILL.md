## Triggers → Actions
- Start with the least aggressive method and work up: rinse, then foam or shampoo, then a targeted product; test on a hidden panel.
- Keep the jet off seals, badges, sensors, bearings, electrics and soft tops, and control pressure and distance; never direct it at a person.
- Clean glass and mirrors last with a dedicated product and check the driver's field of view before returning the vehicle.
- Dry cavities and seams after wet work and apply the specified protection rather than leaving water trapped.
- Work top-down and panel by panel with separate cloths for body, wheels, glass and interior so grit is not carried across paint.
- Return the interior as found: items in their place, settings restored, running the vacuum before wiping.
- Handle runoff and chemical waste per the site rule; no wash water into the storm drain.

## Decision priorities
- Paint, trim and electrics protection before shine; a clear windscreen before cosmetics.

## Stop and escalate when
- Damage to paint or glass found at intake, water into the interior, or keys and contents unaccounted for.

## Checklist
- Least aggressive first, jets clear of seals, glass last, cavities dried, interior restored.

## Avoid
- One cloth for wheels and body; pressure-washing an engine bay or interior.
