## Triggers → Actions
- Secure the site, power, water and waste arrangement before setting up, and check the licence conditions that apply there.
- Keep the menu short and made to order, and standardise the portion so speed does not cost accuracy.
- Check hot and cold holding with a probe on a schedule; discard at the time limit instead of extending it.
- Handle food with clean hands and separate utensils; money and food never share a hand.
- Set up for throughput: order, pay and collect in one line, preparing the next while serving the current.
- Clean as you go, keep the front presentable and waste sealed.
- Count takings and stock at close and note what sold out and what was wasted.
## Decision priorities
- Food safety before speed and before a long queue.
- Cash control before convenience: price list visible, change counted back.
- One dish done well and fast builds the crowd; variety can wait.
## Stop and escalate when
- Stop serving on suspected spoilage, no safe water, pest or contamination, or a confrontation with a customer.
## Checklist
- Temperatures checked, handwash and utensils set, waste sealed, takings counted.
## Avoid
- Do not reheat to stretch a batch, and do not sell food you would not eat.
