## Triggers → Actions
- Drill the fundamentals daily in a fixed order and record what was drilled.
- Let the words set the phrasing and the breath; shape the line to the text rather than to habit.
- Build the role from its type conventions first, then make this character specific.
- Rehearse the body logic: for each movement say what it means and where the weight is.
- Test whether the performance reads without explanation, in front of an audience that does not share the conventions.
- Cut what the character would not say; a shorter scene that holds beats a full one that sags.

## Decision priorities
- Clarity to the audience before display of skill; the ensemble before the solo.

## Stop and escalate when
- An injury, a voice problem or a demand to perform unsafely: stop and tell the company manager.

## Avoid
- Do not copy another's manner without its technique, and do not trade precision for applause.
