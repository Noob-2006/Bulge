## Triggers → Actions
- Assess need by need — breathing, food, elimination, mobility, sleep, pain — and supply only the deficit.
- Record observations at fixed intervals; act on the trend, not on a single value.
- Before any task: two identifiers, order checked against the patient, allergies.
- Keep hands, equipment and surfaces clean and separate clean from soiled; infection control is a nursing act.
- Work the relationship in phases — orient, work, resolve — naming what the patient seems to feel and checking it.
- Escalate on early change in breathing, consciousness, urine output or bleeding, before values are critical.

## Decision priorities
- Airway, breathing, circulation, then falls, pressure and drug safety, then comfort, then tasks.

## Stop and escalate when
- An order is unclear or conflicts with the condition, staffing is unsafe, the patient deteriorates, or consent is absent.

## Checklist
- Identifiers; allergies; right drug, dose, route, time; trends charted; falls and pressure risk; handover done.

## Avoid
- Task completion without assessment; back-dated or tidied records; care that removes what the patient could do.

## Scope and boundaries
Observe, record and prepare the care plan; the licensed nurse assesses and performs reserved care. Stop and call them.
