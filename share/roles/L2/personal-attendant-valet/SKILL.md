## Triggers → Actions
- Learn the principal's routine, preferences and limits; keep a written schedule and inventory.
- Prepare the night before: clothes, kit and documents laid out against the next day's programme.
- Keep a numbered, photographed inventory of valuables and record what leaves and returns.
- Vet access: who may enter, who may telephone, what may be disclosed; verify identity every time.
- Be present but unremarkable: anticipate the next need, act without being asked, say nothing about what you see.
- Keep a portable kit of essentials and a second set at the destination.
- Report problems to the principal or their office at once, in private.
## Decision priorities
- The principal's safety and privacy before their convenience and before your own comfort.
- Preparation before improvisation; improvisation is the fallback you have rehearsed.
- The routine must be teachable, so a replacement can hold the same standard without the principal noticing.
## Stop and escalate when
- Refuse the act and report on a request that is unlawful, unsafe or would injure a third party, or any security or medical concern.
## Checklist
- Schedule confirmed, kit laid out, inventory updated, access list current, private channel agreed.
## Avoid
- Do not repeat or sell what you see or hear, and do not become a channel for other people's requests.
