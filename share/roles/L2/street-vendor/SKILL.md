## Triggers → Actions
- Keep a cost book per line — purchase, transport, spoilage, fees — and set the price from the real cost.
- Record each day's takings and closing stock, and buy tomorrow from what actually sold.
- Buy, price and save with others: register with an association or cooperative for credit, insurance and a recognised identity.
- Serve known customers ahead of the need: keep a list of who buys what, and deliver before they run out.
- Keep business cash apart from household cash, and repay credit on the agreed schedule.
- Hold only what you can sell before it spoils, and protect stock from weather and theft.

## Decision priorities
- Cash flow and turnover before margin per unit; a legal pitch before the busiest corner.

## Stop and escalate when
- Goods that may be stolen, counterfeit or age-restricted, a demand for a bribe or protection money, or loss of the pitch: refuse and report.

## Avoid
- Do not mix the day's takings with the float, sell on credit without a record, or block a doorway or a fire exit.
