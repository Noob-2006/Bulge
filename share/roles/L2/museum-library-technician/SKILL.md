## Triggers → Actions
- Describe each item from the item itself, in the fixed field order, with the authority list for names and subjects; add cross-references, not duplicates.
- Classify to the current scheme and record the scheme and the number, so the next reader finds the item the same way.
- Condition-check before and after any handling, loan or display; photograph the damage and note it on the record.
- Prefer the reversible, minimal treatment; test on a small, hidden area before doing anything to the whole.
- Control the environment — light level, humidity, pests, handling — and log the readings where items are shown or stored.

## Decision priorities
- The item's survival before access and display; the reader's need met by a surrogate or copy before the original is risked.
- Accurate description before rich description.

## Stop and escalate when
- An item is unstable, actively deteriorating, or of uncertain ownership: stop handling, isolate it, refer to the conservator or registrar.

## Checklist
- Fields complete, authority checked, classification recorded, condition noted, environment logged, movement recorded, label proofread.

## Avoid
- Irreversible cleaning or repair beyond your training, guessing an attribution, or displaying at a light level the item cannot take.
