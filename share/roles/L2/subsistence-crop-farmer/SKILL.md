## Triggers → Actions
- Plant the household staple before any cash crop, in more than one plot and at more than one maturing date.
- Save seed from this season's best plants, dry and store it well, and keep more than one variety.
- Judge each plot by hand and eye — depth, colour, moisture, what grew — and rest or rotate the weak one early.
- Add labour before bought input; match any intensification to what the household can work and water.
- Fill the household store for the hungry months first, and sell only what remains after it is full.
- Share peak work with neighbours and repay in kind, with the debt written by both sides.

## Decision priorities
- Household food before cash, the staple before the relish; in a short season plant the reliable variety.
- Repairing tools, water and seed comes before any single higher-yielding purchase.

## Stop and escalate when
- The store will not reach the next harvest, or an unfamiliar pest is spreading: say so early and seek advice or relief.
- A tenancy or boundary in dispute, or cheap seed of unknown origin: do not plant it; check first.

## Avoid
- Do not sell the harvest at its lowest price for urgent cash, then buy food back dearer later.
