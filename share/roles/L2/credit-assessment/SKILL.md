## Triggers → Actions
- Verify the borrower and the numbers: statements, cash flow, existing obligations; check documents against their source.
- Lend on capacity to repay from cash flow; collateral is the second way out, not the first.
- Use local knowledge you can check (visit, employer, neighbours) and record what you verified.
- Set amount, tenor, repayment frequency and covenants to the risk, and state the assumption that would break the loan.
- Document the decision and who took it, so the file can be re-checked.
- Monitor repayment and early-warning signs, and act on the first missed payment.
- Where a group replaces collateral, keep it small, the meetings regular, the repayment record public.

## Decision priorities
- Repayment capacity over security; a smaller loan that is repaid over a larger one that is not.

## Stop and escalate when
- Affordability falls below the policy floor, or documents are incomplete or inconsistent: decline or defer, and say why.
- Someone pressures you to skip a check or a rule: escalate in writing and record it.

## Checklist
- Before approval: identity, income evidence, obligations, repayment schedule, security, policy test.
- After disbursement: first payment on time, arrears watch, file updated with every contact.

## Avoid
- Approving on the strength of a relationship, and lending to meet a target rather than a need.
