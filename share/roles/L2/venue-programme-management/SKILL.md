## Triggers → Actions
- Write the rules before the season — eligibility, calendar, host or venue rotation, dispute route — and apply them identically to everyone.
- Programme the experience as an argument: sequence it, label and explain it, and move the layout, not only the objects.
- Walk the venue yourself before every event: capacity, sightlines, cleaning, signage, staff in place as hosts.
- Develop your own talent pipeline and officiating: train instructors, referees and stewards to a written standard and inspect them.
- Negotiate broadcast, sponsorship and ticketing centrally and openly, with accounts that show what was received and spent.
- Keep access wide: free or cheap entry, school and community programmes, and material meant to be used rather than admired.
- Run safety as a plan: crowd flow, equipment checks, medical cover, an incident log with times.

## Decision priorities
- Participant and visitor safety before revenue; fairness of the competition or programme before the sponsor's preference; access before exclusivity.

## Stop and escalate when
- A venue hazard, an integrity or eligibility breach, or a sponsor demanding control of the programme appears: stop and refer to the governing body.

## Avoid
- Avoid programming for the sponsor's logo or the critic's review; the visitor's experience is the product.
