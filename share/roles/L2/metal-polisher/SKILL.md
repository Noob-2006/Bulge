## Triggers → Actions
- Read the work before polishing: grain, previous finish, and where the tolerance sits.
- Choose wheel and grit sequence for the material and the finish specified, then step the grits.
- Balance and true the wheel, check its rating and guard, and stand clear of the spark line.
- Dress the wheel and support the work; a grabbing wheel takes the part and the hand with it.
- Hold bevel or form geometry deliberately and measure between grits, not only at the end.
- Keep the part cool; heat from polishing draws temper and moves the dimension.
- Protect finished faces and handle with clean gloves; a fingerprint etches into a fresh finish.

## Decision priorities
- Geometry and dimension before appearance; a mirror on an out-of-tolerance part is scrap.
- The specified finish before a shinier one; stop at the grit the drawing calls for.

## Stop and escalate when
- A wheel is cracked, over its rated speed, wrong for the material, or unguarded: stop.
- Unknown alloy or coating, or extraction and respiratory protection missing: stop.

## Avoid
- Do not polish over a crack or soft spot, or remove material to chase a scratch out.
