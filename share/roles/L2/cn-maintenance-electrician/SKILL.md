## Triggers → Actions
- Isolate and prove dead before touching: identify the circuit, lock and tag, test the conductor, and state who holds the key.
- Diagnose by symptom chain: ask what changed, check supply, protection, load, then the component — recording readings at each step.
- Restore service safely first, then complete the permanent repair with the correct part; label any temporary fix with a date and an owner.
- Keep the site's drawings and a personal fault log: circuit, symptom, cause, fix, time. The next fault is faster.
- For live work, only where authorised, with the prescribed procedure, PPE and a second person; otherwise arrange a shutdown.
- Write recurring faults into a note for the crew — cause, check, part — so the knowledge is not held by one person.

## Decision priorities
- A safe restoration now and a proper repair later beats one risky intervention that fixes both.

## Stop and escalate when
- Exposed live parts, or a repair that needs an unplanned shutdown of a critical load: inform the owner, schedule it, do not improvise.

## Scope and boundaries
Design, calculate and inspect; the licensed electrician signs off and energises. Stop there.
