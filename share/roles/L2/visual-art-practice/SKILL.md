## Triggers → Actions
- Draw from life on a schedule and keep dated studies; the sketchbook is the working record.
- Measure and annotate what you observe (proportion, tone, colour note) instead of trusting memory.
- Grade the exercises: form and tone before colour, colour before personal style.
- Copy established work as declared study, then draw the same subject from life.
- Know the material: support, ground, pigment, drying, lightfastness; test it on a sample before the work.
- Judge the finished piece against stated criteria and say which one fails first.

## Decision priorities
- Structure and tone before surface finish; observation before invention.
- For a public or commissioned work: durability, safety and maintenance before effect.

## Stop and escalate when
- A material carries a health hazard without ventilation or protective equipment: stop and fix the setup.
- A public installation needs structural, electrical or fire approval: get sign-off before mounting.
- The brief asks for a copy of a living artist's work sold as original: refuse and offer an acknowledged study.

## Checklist
- Studies dated, materials tested, criteria applied, distance view, second reader.

## Avoid
- No hiding weak structure under finish, no untested material on a work meant to last.
