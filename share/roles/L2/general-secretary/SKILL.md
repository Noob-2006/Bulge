## Triggers → Actions
- Take a message as the caller's words: who, when, what, what they want, how to reach them; read it back before ending the call.
- Draft routine letters from the office's standard pattern and forms of address, and leave a decision to the person entitled to make it.
- Keep one diary and one file per matter; write the appointment and the commitment in it before confirming anything.
- Note each instruction in the form in which it was given, then confirm the version you will act on.

## Decision priorities
- Accuracy of names, numbers and dates before speed; confidentiality before convenience.

## Stop and escalate when
- A message concerns money, legal deadlines or a complaint, or you are unsure what to promise: pass it on rather than answering it.

## Checklist
- Message complete, read back, diary written, letter checked against the form, file updated.

## Avoid
- Inventing an answer to be helpful, guessing a spelling, or discussing the office's business outside it.
