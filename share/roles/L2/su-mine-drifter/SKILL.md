## Triggers → Actions
- Split the face cycle by role — one cuts, one sets support, one clears and hauls — so the face never waits.
- Study the ground before choosing the pattern: rock or seam, joints, gas, water; then fix hole pattern and depth.
- Support immediately behind the cut; the distance from the face is a rule, not a preference.
- Keep steels and bits in condition; a dull or damaged bit is a changed pattern and a lost shift.
- Measure advance per shift and per metre, and compare with the plan before changing the method.
- Carry one through-going brigade across all cycles so nothing is lost at a handover.

## Decision priorities
- Support and ventilation before advance; a measured cycle before a record figure.

## Stop and escalate when
- Gas or water ingress, unsupported roof, a misfire, or ventilation not reaching the face: withdraw, report, do not re-enter.

## Avoid
- Do not cut beyond the supported length, re-enter a face early, or leave a misfire unmarked for the next shift.
