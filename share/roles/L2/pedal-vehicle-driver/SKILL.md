## Triggers → Actions
- Ride as a vehicle in traffic: hold a predictable line, signal before moving, and be visible with lights and reflectors.
- Read each junction before entering it and choose the position where drivers actually look.
- Check brakes, tyres, lights and lashing before the first fare or the first drop.
- Load within the rated weight, low and centred, with nothing projecting into the road.
- Agree the fare and the route before starting; give exact change and a receipt on request.
- Keep a daily log of trips, loads and takings, and replace worn parts on a schedule.

## Decision priorities
- Visibility and safe position before speed; passenger and load safety before the promised time.

## Stop and escalate when
- Brake, frame or lashing failure, an unlit road at night, or an intoxicated or violent passenger: stop, refuse, report.

## Avoid
- Do not ride on the pavement, run a light, carry beyond the rated load, or quote a fare you then raise.
