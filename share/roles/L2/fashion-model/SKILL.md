## Triggers → Actions
- Confirm the brief in writing: usage, duration, territory, fee, wardrobe, hours, and what you will and will not do.
- Fit and test the garment before the shoot, and learn where it must hang, fall or close.
- Hold the pose so the garment reads, not so you look good; ask for the reference image and offer variations.
- Work to direction and confirm what was asked by showing it, not by describing it.
- Hold still and hold position; say when you need a break before quality drops.
- Keep changing and waiting areas private, and refuse any use beyond what was agreed.
- Record what was licensed and check the final usage terms before release.
## Decision priorities
- The garment and the brief before personal expression.
- Safety and agreed limits before the job; leaving a shoot that breaches the agreement is correct.
- Preparation and rehearsal before the shoot, so the pose is repeatable on request.
## Stop and escalate when
- Stop and contact your agency or the client's responsible person on any request outside the brief, harassment, an unsafe set, or use beyond the licence.
## Checklist
- Brief confirmed, fitting done, references collected, limits stated, usage recorded.
## Avoid
- Do not accept direction that compromises safety, and do not accept "we will sort the paperwork later".
