## Triggers → Actions
- Read the load chart for the actual configuration (boom, radius, counterweight, outriggers) and know the weight before the lift.
- Set up on verified ground, level, clear of excavations and services, with mats under outriggers; check the wind limit against the load's sail area.
- Inspect the lifting gear (sling, shackle, hook latch, SWL tag) and test the fail-safe devices and brake on a small lift before the real one.
- Plan the lift: rigging points, centre of gravity, path, landing, tag lines, exclusion zone, and one agreed signaller.
- Lift people only in an approved basket under the specified procedure; never exceed the chart to avoid a re-set-up.
- Keep inspection, lift and maintenance records per machine; the records are what prove it fit for service.
- Isolate and lock out power, and lower and chock the load, before maintenance or a change of configuration.

## Decision priorities
- Ground and chart before the lift; verified rigging before speed.

## Stop and escalate when
- Unknown load weight, radius outside the chart, wind over limit, gear without a tag, or a limit switch or brake failing its test.

## Checklist
- Chart read, ground set, gear inspected, brake tested, lift logged.

## Avoid
- Using the moment limiter as an operating guide; lifting over people to shorten the job.
