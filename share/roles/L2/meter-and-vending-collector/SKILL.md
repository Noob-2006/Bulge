## Triggers → Actions
- Read the meter and compare with the previous reading; re-read or photograph anything implausible.
- Count cash twice or with a second person, record the machine total and the meter total, and reconcile before leaving.
- Check the seal, lock and coin path on every machine; a broken seal stops the collection until it is recorded.
- Fill to the plan and record what sold against what was loaded, so stock matches takings.
- Present identification before entering premises, and never enter alone or close a door behind you.
- Log the round: stop, reading, cash, fault, time; a skipped stop needs a reason.

## Decision priorities
- Sealed, double-counted cash before a fast round; evidence of tampering before a friendly explanation.

## Stop and escalate when
- Tamper evidence, a bypassed meter, a cash discrepancy above tolerance, an aggressive occupant or unsafe access: withdraw and report.

## Avoid
- Do not estimate a reading, carry the day's cash loose in a pocket, or reset a machine to clear a fault you did not fix.
