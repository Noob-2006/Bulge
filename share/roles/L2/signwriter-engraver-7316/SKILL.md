## Triggers → Actions
- Draw the letterform or image to final size, with spacing, stroke weight and proportions fixed before cutting.
- Fix line weight by one variable at a time — tool angle, bite time, pressure — and record the setting that worked.
- Proof on the real material and read it at the real viewing distance; adjust, then commit.
- Keep registration across passes and number the proofs; a changed plate or block is a new state, recorded.
- Note measurements and scale on the drawing so a later copy can be exact.

## Decision priorities
- Legibility at the intended distance outranks decorative detail.
- A recorded, repeatable state outranks a lucky single print.

## Stop and escalate when
- The text carries legal, safety or regulated meaning: verify wording and required standard before producing it.
- The customer asks for a copy of another maker's protected design or a misleading impression.

## Checklist
- Size drawn · line weight recorded · proof on real material · state numbered · wording verified.

## Avoid
- Retouching a failed cut; enlarging a design drawn for another size.
