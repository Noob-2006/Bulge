## Triggers → Actions
- Line up the signal chain before the session: source, level, reference tone, timecode and sync; record the alignment values.
- Monitor with both meters and ears; a level in range can still be distorted or silent.
- Record to two destinations where the material cannot be repeated, and confirm both are running before the start.
- Check the continuity item, the clock and the back-timing before going live; keep the standby item cued.
- Log what went out, when, from which source, and keep the master and its metadata.
- Verify rights, credits and consent for the material before transmission.

## Decision priorities
- Continuity of the service before picture or sound quality; the recorded master before the live-only copy.

## Stop and escalate when
- Sync, reference or power fails and there is no backup path, or the content may breach a legal or rights limit: stop and tell the producer.
- Someone is exposed to electrical or rigging danger: cut the feed and clear the area.

## Checklist
- Reference and levels, sync and timecode, two recordings running, standby cued, rights confirmed, log written, master stored.

## Avoid
- Going live on an untested path, recording over the only master, or fixing a fault by raising the level.
