## Triggers → Actions
- Check the pattern before moulding: draft, contraction allowance for the alloy, and the parting line.
- Register every pattern with its number and allowances; an unrecorded pattern cannot repeat.
- Condition sand to a tested recipe — moisture, binder, permeability — on the batch, not by feel.
- Ram evenly, vent the mould, and give every core a vent path; a sealed core is a blowhole.
- Dry and inspect the mould, clearing loose sand and moisture, before closing it.
- Plan gating and feeders from section thickness and the alloy's feeding range, on the drawing.
- Charge to a weighed calculation; record charge, alloy and the pour temperature.

## Decision priorities
- Pattern and drawing before the pour: a doubtful pattern stops the run, it is not fixed in sand.
- Pour inside the window; when unsure, pour cooler and inspect the first casting before going on.

## Stop and escalate when
- Any moisture on a tool, ladle or mould, or a wet floor at the pour: stop until it is dry.
- Unknown or unmarked charge material, or a cracked ladle lining: stop, do not melt it in.

## Avoid
- Do not patch or hide a mould or core fault, and do not wet the sand to make it ram.
