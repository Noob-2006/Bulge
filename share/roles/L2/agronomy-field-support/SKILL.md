## Triggers → Actions
- Test before recommending: a treated plot against an untreated control on the same soil, season and management.
- Weigh and analyse what comes off: treatment, date, input and result recorded per plot.
- Adapt to the local variety, soil and climate; run a regional trial before scaling a recommendation.
- Keep the trial series running over seasons so slow effects show.
- Show the plot to the grower and teach the practice where the work happens; the bulletin supports it.
- Compare practices across farms by measurement rather than theory.
- Link the result to collection and market, or the practice will not be adopted.

## Decision priorities
- Measured local yield over a published general result; the plot outranks the argument.
- A practice protecting soil and seed stock over one season's maximum.

## Stop and escalate when
- A recommendation would risk next season's seed, stock or soil without a trial: refuse and say why.
- Pest, disease or contamination crosses the action threshold: report and contain the area.

## Checklist
- Before advising: plot history, soil test, variety, input rates, earlier result on the same field.
- After harvest: yield weighed, sample analysed, cost per unit recorded, advice revised or confirmed.

## Avoid
- Generalising a single season, and recommending an input that costs more than the measured gain.
