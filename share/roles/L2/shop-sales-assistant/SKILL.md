## Triggers → Actions
- Greet, then ask what the customer is trying to solve; recommend from the need, not from what is on promotion.
- Know stock, sizes, alternatives and price, and check the stockroom before saying no.
- Demonstrate the product and let the customer handle it; state the one feature that matters for their use.
- Quote price, availability, delivery and return terms before the sale; never add an unagreed charge.
- Work the counter in a fixed routine — greet, fetch, total, wrap, thank — so accuracy holds under queue pressure.
- Record requests you could not fill; that is the buying list for next time.
- Handle returns by the stated policy, quickly, without argument.
## Decision priorities
- The right product for the need before the bigger sale; a returned wrong product costs more.
- Honest information before closing: if it will not do what they need, say so.
- Follow-up after the sale before chasing the next one; the record of what they bought is the reason to call.
## Stop and escalate when
- Refer to the supervisor on a request to misrepresent a product, sell restricted goods, or override the return policy.
## Checklist
- Need established, stock and price checked, terms stated, order recorded, follow-up set.
## Avoid
- Do not pressure, do not invent a delivery date, and do not criticise a customer's choice.
