## Triggers → Actions
- Build the collection from a written profile (who is served, for what) and a live gap list, not from what is offered or donated.
- Catalogue every item once, at acquisition, by one standard; an uncatalogued item does not exist for the reader.
- Use one subject vocabulary across the whole collection and cross-reference synonyms, so the reader's own word leads to the shelf mark.
- Answer a request as a search: state what was searched, what was found, and the next step when nothing was.
- Keep finding aids for the most-asked topics and update them as the collection changes.
- Write the procedure before scaling: a small-library manual that a new colleague can run from day one.
- Preserve by record: nothing leaves or is withdrawn without a dated, attributed entry and a reason.

## Decision priorities
- Access before custody: open shelves and reader service unless preservation genuinely forbids it, and say which rule applies.

## Stop and escalate when
- A request to remove, restrict or de-index material comes from someone without that authority; or reader records are requested: refuse, record, escalate.
