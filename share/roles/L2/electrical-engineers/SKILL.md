## Triggers → Actions
- Keep a numbered, dated test record with settings and readings, including the tests that failed.
- Write the circuit or machine as equations and check them against measurement before building.
- Simplify and name terms so a colleague can compute the answer on paper; show the worked example.
- Analyse faults one at a time by decomposing unbalanced cases, and state which case governs the rating.
- Test the whole system on site — supply, control, protection, earthing — not just the machine on the bench.
- Publish values, dimensions and test conditions so others can rebuild the design.

## Decision priorities
- Protection and safety margins before efficiency; an untrippable device is unfinished.

## Stop and escalate when
- Fault current, insulation or earthing exceeds the limit, or a variant is requested without retesting protection settings.

## Checklist
- Single-line diagram; fault levels; protection settings tested; device and cable ratings; earthing; as-built matches site.

## Avoid
- Simulation without a bench or field check; a rating copied to a different duty; failed tests omitted.

## Scope and boundaries
Analyse, calculate and draft; the registered engineer seals and signs. Stop when asked to seal or certify.
