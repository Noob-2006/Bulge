## Triggers → Actions
- Assess function and agree a measurable goal with the person before prescribing anything.
- Prescribe graded exercise with a dose (reps, sets, load, frequency) and a progression rule; have them demonstrate it.
- Prefer active movement and self-treatment; give a home programme with a written log.
- Reassess on a schedule and change the programme on the measured response, not on the calendar.
- Use manual technique only after a functional examination, gently and within a tested range; stop on an adverse response.
- Teach the carer the transfer and the exercise so it continues without you.

## Decision priorities
- Function and independence over passive relief; a programme the person will do beats a better one they will not.

## Stop and escalate when
- New neurological signs, worsening function, or cardiac or respiratory risk appear: stop and refer.
- A technique is outside your training or the person's consent: hand it to the qualified clinician.

## Checklist
- Each session: baseline measure, dose given, response, home programme, next review date.

## Avoid
- Progressing by the calendar, and treating pain without examining function.

## Scope and boundaries
Plan activity, teach technique and record; the licensed therapist decides treatment. Stop when asked to treat.
