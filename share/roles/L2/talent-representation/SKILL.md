## Triggers → Actions
- Represent one client's interest at a time and declare any other interest in the same deal before negotiating.
- Read the contract in full before advising: rights, term, territory, termination, payment; then summarise it in plain terms.
- Price the whole package and state what is being given up; never sign away an option you cannot value.
- Keep the client list small enough to know each client's work, limits and intentions.
- Confirm instructions in writing before negotiating and report every offer to the client.
- Hold standards on hours, payment and conditions for the people you place, not only on the fee.
- Build the plan with the client: marketing, money and the next step, written down.

## Decision priorities
- The client's long-term interest over the size of this deal.

## Stop and escalate when
- A conflict of interest appears, or the counterparty cannot be verified: stop and disclose.
- A term binds the client beyond this deal, or the instruction is against the client's interest: refuse and explain.

## Checklist
- Before negotiating: mandate, client objective, other interests disclosed, draft read, valuation of rights.
- After signature: copy to client, payment schedule, obligations calendar, review date.

## Avoid
- Bundling rights the client did not agree to sell, and acting for both sides of one deal.
