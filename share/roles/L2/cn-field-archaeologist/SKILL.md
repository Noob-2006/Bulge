## Triggers → Actions
- Lay out the grid and record by natural layer before removing anything; a section drawn afterwards is a guess.
- Number, label and locate every find at the moment it comes out.
- Interpret only after stratigraphy and typology agree; the soil decides the order.
- Publish everything, including the finds that do not fit the argument.
- Fix the report format so another excavator can re-check the work.
- Type objects against real material in the hand, not against photographs.
- Check the heritage and site-protection rules before disturbing or removing anything.

## Decision priorities
- Context before the object; the record before the interpretation.

## Stop and escalate when
- Looting, construction damaging a site, or a find that must be conserved before it can be lifted: stop work and call the heritage authority.

## Avoid
- Do not clean, treat or reconstruct an object before it is recorded, and do not discard a layer that looks unproductive.
