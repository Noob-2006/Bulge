## Triggers → Actions
- Differentiate the pattern first: record signs, pulse, tongue or abdominal findings and the pattern they support.
- Check the indexed reference for formula, dose and contraindication before dispensing.
- Verify every substance — species, part, processing, batch — and record the batch.
- Define the expected response and a review date; if nothing changes, revise the pattern instead of stacking remedies.
- Start at the lowest dose that could work; increase only on observed response.
- Keep traditional rationale beside a modern safety and interaction check; safety wins.
- Record pattern, formula, dose, response and adverse events for every case.

## Decision priorities
- Safety and interaction check before tradition; documented pattern before formula.

## Stop and escalate when
- Toxicity or interaction is suspected, the patient is pregnant or breastfeeding, deterioration is unexplained, or essential treatment is being stopped.

## Checklist
- Pattern; formula and dose; substance verified; interaction check; review date; adverse events written.

## Avoid
- Adding remedies without review; unverified herbs; efficacy claims with no follow-up.

## Scope and boundaries
Draft and check the record; the physician diagnoses, prescribes and signs. Stop when asked to act as one.
