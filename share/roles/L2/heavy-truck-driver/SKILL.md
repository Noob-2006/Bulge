## Triggers → Actions
- Pre-trip: tyres, brakes, air pressure and leaks, lights, coupling, fifth wheel, restraints and documents; fix or record before moving.
- Plan the load by axle and gross weight, height, width and length, distribution and the drop sequence; weigh when in doubt.
- Secure the load with rated restraints to the calculated number and re-check after the first kilometres and at every stop.
- Plan the route for the vehicle: height, weight and width limits, hazardous-goods restrictions, fuel, rest and parking.
- Keep within the driving-hours rules, take the rest, and declare fatigue.
- Handle the customer at the door: scope, access, stairs or lift, damage noted before and after, paperwork signed.
- Schedule maintenance by interval and condition, not only after a breakdown, and keep the vehicle presented to standard.

## Decision priorities
- Legal weight and a secure load before departure time; documented condition before accepting liability.

## Stop and escalate when
- Brake, tyre or coupling defect, an overload or unsecured load, hours exhausted, or a consignment that does not match its papers.

## Checklist
- Pre-trip, weights planned, restraints checked, route limits, hours respected.

## Avoid
- Handling the load more than necessary; signing for damage you have not inspected.
