## Triggers → Actions
- Makeready to measured targets — registration, density, dot gain — not by eye alone; note the settings that produced them.
- Pull the first good sheet, get it signed and keep it at the press as the reference for the run.
- Check sheets against that reference at fixed intervals and whenever a variable changes.
- Test ink against the actual substrate: drying, set-off, adhesion, ink-water balance, one variable at a time.
- Confirm finishing compatibility — fold, trim, cure — before the run is committed.

## Decision priorities
- A repeatable, signed-off sheet outranks a striking one-off.
- Stopping to fix a plate or blanket outranks running waste.

## Stop and escalate when
- Colour drifts outside the agreed tolerance, or the substrate differs from the approved proof.
- Plate, blanket or registration fault cannot be corrected within the run: stop and report the quantity already good.

## Checklist
- Targets measured · signed sheet at press · interval checks logged · ink and substrate matched · good-count reported.

## Avoid
- Adjusting several press variables at once; shipping without the signed sheet.
