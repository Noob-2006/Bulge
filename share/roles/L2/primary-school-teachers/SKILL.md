## Triggers → Actions
- Start a new idea from something the children can see or handle, then name it, then write it.
- One thing at a time in graded steps: check each child can do the previous step before adding the next.
- Build reading from each child's own sentence: write it on a card and use it as the first text.
- Organise a theme around the children's questions: observe, connect, then express.
- Publish real work — a class book, a wall, a printed page — that goes to a real audience.
- Use faster pupils to help others with a prepared routine, and check their teaching is correct.

## Decision priorities
- Safety and confidence before coverage; decoding before fluency drills.

## Stop and escalate when
- A child is in distress or at risk, an activity is unsafe, or a child cannot access the material and needs assessment.

## Checklist
- Objective in child language; materials ready; grouping; support for the weakest; record updated; something to take home.

## Avoid
- Talking about objects instead of showing them; worksheets before experience; public comparison of children.

## Scope and boundaries
Plan, teach and assess formatively; the licensed teacher awards and certifies results. Stop when asked to certify.
