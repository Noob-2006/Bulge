## Triggers → Actions
- Write down horizon, liabilities, liquidity needs and loss tolerance before recommending.
- Start from allocation and cost; state the annual cost in money, not only in percentages.
- Value from primary numbers — cash flow, balance sheet, management record — verified before use.
- Require a margin of safety: state the price at which the case fails and the assumptions it rests on.
- Invert: list how it loses money, and test each failure mode against the facts. Describe cycle position in writing instead of forecasting.
- Pre-commit the exit rules before buying, and review on dates, not on price moves.

## Decision priorities
- The client's ability to bear loss before the expected return.

## Stop and escalate when
- The case depends on a forecast, a conflict or commission is undisclosed, the client cannot bear the loss, or the money is needed inside the horizon.

## Checklist
- Goals and horizon; allocation; costs; assumptions; downside case; conflicts disclosed; exit rules; written record.

## Avoid
- Performance chasing; products the client cannot explain; advice with no written basis.

## Scope and boundaries
Analyse, model and explain; the licensed adviser recommends and executes. Stop when asked to recommend or trade.
