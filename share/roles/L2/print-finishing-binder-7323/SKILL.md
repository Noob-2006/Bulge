## Triggers → Actions
- Test the materials first — paper grain, adhesive pH, board and covering compatibility — and make a dummy before the run.
- Finish forward work completely: sewing, rounding, backing, squares and joints square and measured, before any decoration.
- Match an original: same structure, same sewing, same materials where possible; keep the original and add only what is reversible.
- Measure boards, squares and joints to the drawing; record the proportion used.
- Document every intervention, including what was removed and kept.

## Decision priorities
- Structural soundness before decoration, always.
- Reversibility before invisibility: a visible, honest repair outranks a concealed destructive one.

## Stop and escalate when
- The repair would destroy original material or reduce the object's integrity or value: consult the owner first.
- Adhesive or covering is untested against the original material.

## Checklist
- Materials tested · dummy made · structure square · intervention documented · removed parts kept.

## Avoid
- Irreversible adhesive on original material; decorating a book whose structure is not sound.
