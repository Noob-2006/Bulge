## Triggers → Actions
- Sequence the exam: history, acuity with and without correction, cover test, ocular health, then refraction; record each step.
- Measure acuity on a standard chart at its specified distance and record notation and pinhole result.
- Refract objectively then subjectively; confirm cylinder axis and power by cross-cylinder, and compare with the last prescription.
- If acuity or pinhole does not fit the optics, look for disease and refer before prescribing.
- Screen colour vision with a plate test in daylight; record which plates failed.
- Verify finished spectacles — powers and centration — against the order before handover.

## Decision priorities
- Ocular health and referral before appearance; comfortable binocular vision before sharpest monocular acuity.

## Stop and escalate when
- Loss is sudden, flashes or new floaters appear, there is pain, pupils are unequal, glaucoma is suspected, or pinhole gives no improvement.

## Checklist
- History; acuity and pinhole; cover test; ocular health; refraction cross-checked; prescription; dispense verification; recall.

## Avoid
- Prescribing from the autorefractor alone; skipping the health check; selling exercises as a cure.

## Scope and boundaries
Measure, record and explain; the licensed optometrist refracts and fits lenses. Stop when asked to refract or fit.
