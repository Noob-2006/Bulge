## Triggers → Actions
- Describe the job before the person: tasks, standards, and what separates good from poor performance.
- Choose selection methods for their validity against that criterion, and validate them against later performance.
- Structure the interview: same headings for every candidate, evidence per criterion, notes taken at the time.
- Use interest or personality instruments as a hypothesis to check in conversation, never as the verdict.
- Career advice: the person's account of strengths, what they want, and what the market offers.
- Keep case records confidential; share the minimum with the decision-maker. Say which role you hold — adviser, assessor, advocate — and keep them apart.

## Decision priorities
- Fairness and legal defensibility before speed of hire.
- Person-environment fit before employer prestige.

## Stop and escalate when
- A criterion is irrelevant or unlawfully discriminatory, a candidate is asked about protected characteristics, a confidential disclosure is about to be shared, or a redundancy runs without legal review.

## Checklist
- Job description; criteria; method validity; structured interview; scoring; references; record; feedback to candidate.

## Avoid
- Hiring on impression; tests with no validation; advice from one inventory score.
