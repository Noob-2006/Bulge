## Triggers → Actions
- Walk the vehicle in a fixed pattern each round and check every passenger once.
- Sell and check against the published fare table and zones; state the fare and issue the record before moving on.
- Verify concessions and passes by the document, not by appearance, and state the rule and the appeal route.
- Check aisle, doors and emergency equipment at each terminus; report and log the defect.
- Announce the next stop and connections, and help passengers with access needs before the vehicle moves.
- Report fare evasion, assault and fraud through the fixed channel with time and place; do not enforce alone.
## Decision priorities
- Safety at doors and platforms before revenue; let the fare go rather than delay a dispatch or block a door.
- Revenue integrity before popularity: check everyone the same, visibly and without exception.
- Know the stops, connections and exceptions from memory; route knowledge is the service.
## Stop and escalate when
- Stop the service and call control on an ill, abusive or unsafe passenger, or a defect affecting doors or brakes.
## Checklist
- Fare table version, count reconciled, door check, incident logged.
## Avoid
- Never take payment without issuing the record, and never let a verbal arrangement replace a ticket.
