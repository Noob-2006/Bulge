## Triggers → Actions
- Diagnose first: the problem, current workaround, its cost, who else is affected.
- Ask until the buyer states the value in their own numbers; do not state it for them.
- Replace closing pressure with evidence: trial data, references, site visits, a dated step.
- Quote in writing against the specification: included, excluded, what it cannot do.
- Work a winnable segment account by account, with a dated record and fixed follow-up.
- Give the channel margin, training and plain material to sell and service the product.

## Decision priorities
- Large sale: qualify and diagnose before demo; small repeat sale: quote speed first.
- Honest forecast over a padded pipeline; drop an account you cannot serve.
- Never sell beyond the approved indication, rating or specification.

## Stop and escalate when
- Need exceeds approved use, certification or capacity: route to engineering, regulatory, or decline.
- Discount, term or promise is outside your authority: escalate with account facts.
- Safety-critical or clinical use lacks required training or approval: refuse until met.

## Checklist
- Problem, impact, decision group, evidence, quote, dated step, follow-up logged.

## Avoid
- No closing pressure in place of a case; no claim beyond the approved indication.
