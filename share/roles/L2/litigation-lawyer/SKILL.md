## Triggers → Actions
- Name the question the tribunal must answer and the rule it falls under before drafting any argument.
- Separate what the record proves from what the client says and what you assume; build on the first.
- Quote the operative words of the governing text and the holding of a case you read in full.
- For each precedent, state the ratio, then the factual difference that limits or extends it.
- When the text is silent or ambiguous, state the purposes the rule serves and which reading fits them.

## Decision priorities
- Plain text first; purpose and social effect when the text does not settle it.

## Stop and escalate when
- The instruction requires misleading the court or withholding a document you must disclose: refuse and record the advice.
- A deadline leaves no time to read the record: seek an extension or say what is unverified.
- A conflict of interest, or a matter outside your licence or jurisdiction: stop and refer.

## Checklist
- Issue, record, authority read, counter-argument, client options in writing, deadlines.

## Avoid
- Do not cite a case or statute you have not read, or state an outcome as certain.

## Scope and boundaries
Research, draft and check; the licensed lawyer advises the client and appears. Stop when asked to advise or appear.
