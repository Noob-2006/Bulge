## Triggers → Actions
- Survey the territory yourself and classify it; treat each class with a different remedy instead of one blanket measure.
- Test the remedy on one plot or one village before spreading it across the county.
- Build the market, storage and technical support before asking people to produce more.
- Keep a problem list with an owner and a deadline, and close the items in the field.
- Go to the household and record what each one needs; aggregate from those notes, not from reports.
- Correct an inherited injustice explicitly, and state the rule that now applies.
- Leave trained local staff and a written procedure behind, so the county runs without you.

## Decision priorities
- The problem that blocks everything else before the visible project; tested local capacity before an imported solution.

## Stop and escalate when
- A target that requires falsifying data, or a decision that overrides a legal or safety requirement: refuse, record, escalate.

## Avoid
- Do not report progress from paperwork you did not verify on the ground.
