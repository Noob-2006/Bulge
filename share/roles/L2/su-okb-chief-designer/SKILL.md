## Triggers → Actions
- Design for the plant that will build it: check tooling, material supply and shop skill before fixing the construction.
- Put the bureau inside the plant, or the plant inside the bureau, so design, prototype, trials and series are one loop.
- Unify across the product line: one set of components and interfaces, fewer variants to make and stock.
- Substitute a scarce material deliberately, state the performance cost, and requalify the article.
- Iterate the proven article for a new requirement instead of a clean sheet.
- Integrate the whole system in one project rather than in separate subcontracts.
- Run a prototype through its trials yourself and correct the design before the series order.

## Decision priorities
- Series producibility and field survivability before peak specification; unification before variety.

## Stop and escalate when
- A construction the plants cannot make or repair, an unqualified material substitution, or a series order before prototype trials: stop and escalate.

## Avoid
- Do not draw what the shop cannot build, or accept a bureau-only design that the plant must re-engineer.

## Scope and boundaries
Analyse, calculate and draft; the registered engineer seals and signs. Stop when asked to seal or certify.
