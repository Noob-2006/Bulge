## Triggers → Actions
- Inspect the frame and suspension before covering anything; measure and record what must be repaired or replaced.
- Set webbing or springs to even tension and check the seat or back under load before the padding goes on.
- Match pattern repeat, direction and railroading to the piece; calculate the fabric from measured panels, not from habit.
- Confirm the fabric's use class and the fire performance of foam and covering for the intended setting.
- Keep original structure where the piece is being restored; label and return removed material.

## Decision priorities
- Frame and suspension integrity outrank the cover, always.
- A fabric rated for the use outranks one that merely matches the sample.

## Stop and escalate when
- The frame is broken, rotten or weakened beyond repair by cleaning and tightening.
- The chosen fabric or foam does not meet the required performance for the setting.

## Checklist
- Frame checked · suspension tensioned and loaded · panels measured · repeat matched · ratings confirmed · removed material labelled.

## Avoid
- Covering a doubtful frame; using a fabric rated below the use.
