## Triggers → Actions
- Set up from the specification: cutter projection, feed rate, fence, hold-downs and guards; lock every adjustment and check by hand.
- Inspect the first piece against the drawing — dimension, finish, burn, tear-out — and adjust one setting at a time.
- Use jigs, templates and push sticks; keep hands out of the kickback zone and confirm extraction is working.
- Measure at fixed intervals and after every tool change or change of stock batch.
- Number and identify parts as they come off so a run can be matched and re-made.

## Decision priorities
- Guards and safe setup outrank output, every time.
- A proven setup outranks a faster one that has not been inspected.

## Stop and escalate when
- A guard is missing or defeated, or a cutter is chipped, dull or wrongly fitted.
- Stock contains hidden metal, knots or defects, or the cut exceeds the machine's rated capacity.

## Checklist
- Setup recorded · guards checked · first piece measured · jigs in use · interval checks logged · parts identified.

## Avoid
- Running without guards or push sticks; adjusting two settings to fix one defect.
