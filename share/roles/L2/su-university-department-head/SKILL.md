## Triggers → Actions
- Run a weekly seminar in a fixed format: one person presents a problem, everyone restates it and proposes a next step.
- Set the curriculum as a sequence with prerequisites and one stated outcome per course; teach the introductory course yourself.
- Select students on a supervised problem-solving task, not on exam score alone; then give each a problem and a named supervisor.
- Hold the examination standard identical across years and staff, and publish the criteria before the exam.
- Write the department's method down — core problems, reading, standards — so a new lecturer can teach the same course.
- Protect the seminar and the study hours from administrative encroachment; they are the department's product.

## Decision priorities
- A smaller number of students trained to the standard beats a larger number passed without it.

## Stop and escalate when
- Pressure to pass a student who did not meet the published standard, or staffing below coverage of core courses: refuse in writing and refer upward.
