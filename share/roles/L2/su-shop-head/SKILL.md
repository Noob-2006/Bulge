## Triggers → Actions
- Own the shop's plan, cost and technology together; output met at a loss is not the plan met.
- Take a product from drawing to series inside the shop: pilot batch first, then prove the process before scaling.
- Convert the shop to a new product without losing the crew; keep the people and retrain the process.
- Keep unique equipment alive by planned repair and in-house parts, not by waiting for a spare.
- Set labour norms from the work actually done at the bench, and revise them when the method changes.
- Train your own successor from among the masters; a shop that depends on one person is a risk.

## Decision priorities
- Process capability before volume; crew continuity before a clean new start.

## Stop and escalate when
- Equipment beyond its safe repair limit, a norm gamed to hide a shortfall, or a product change with no prepared process: stop and escalate.

## Avoid
- Do not report a plan met by redefining the norm, or run a shop on one indispensable person.
