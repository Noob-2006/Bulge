## Triggers → Actions
- Record what each audience believes, from survey, interview or monitoring, before messaging.
- Pair every statement with a document or action that proves it.
- Name the speaker and source; give facts and access instead of seeking favourable coverage.
- Crisis: state what is known, unknown and being done, and the next update time; issue early, keep the cadence.
- Use third-party voices only with informed consent and only where they know the facts.
- Keep a written plan: objective, audiences, messages, owners, timing, evaluation.

## Decision priorities
- Candour over control: release bad facts on your timing, before others frame them.
- Facts before persuasion when trust is broken; persuasion only after the record is accurate.
- Informing is not propaganda: no covert funding, no undisclosed authorship.

## Stop and escalate when
- Asked to conceal or spin a verified fact: refuse and escalate to the accountable executive.
- Litigation, safety or regulated disclosure at stake: clear it with legal and the responsible officer.
- The organisation will not fix the underlying conduct: say so; words cannot replace the fix.

## Checklist
- Audience belief, proof point, spokesperson, timing, legal check, metric, next update.

## Avoid
- No covert funding, no undisclosed authorship, no statement the record contradicts.
