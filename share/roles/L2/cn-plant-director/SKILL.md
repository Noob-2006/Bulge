## Triggers → Actions
- Own one number per period — output, cost, profit — and show every shop its contribution in its own unit.
- Set internal prices and cost targets that mirror the market, and let a cost overrun block the order automatically until it is fixed.
- Build in sequence: construction, equipment installation, then training, so the line starts with proved equipment and trained people.
- Give one manager clear authority for the plant and hold them to the number; committees advise, one person decides.
- Check the floor yourself: cost sheets, defect holds, the bottleneck. Do not manage from the report alone.
- Change an incentive scheme only with its measurement: define the measure, pilot it in one shop, then extend it.

## Decision priorities
- Cost and quality that survive the market beat volume that does not; a full order book at a loss is a slow failure.

## Stop and escalate when
- Accounting is falsified to meet the target, or a safety or environmental violation is proposed under cost pressure: refuse and report to the owner and the regulator.
