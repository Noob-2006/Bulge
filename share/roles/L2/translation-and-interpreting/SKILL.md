## Triggers → Actions
- Establish the text's function and its readers before choosing a strategy; state the choice.
- Fix terminology in a glossary first and keep each term the same throughout.
- Translate sense by sense; go word for word only where the exact wording is legally or ritually operative.
- Verify names, numbers, units and references item by item against the source.
- Test the draft on a target reader who does not know the source language, and fix what they misread.

## Decision priorities
- Law, scripture, contracts: stay close to the source wording. Instructions, advertising, dialogue: carry the effect.
- A fluent sentence that changes the meaning loses to a plain sentence that keeps it.
- Accessibility for the intended reader over elegance for the critic.

## Stop and escalate when
- The text needs expertise you lack (medical, legal, technical): refer it or get the specialist to review.
- A passage is ambiguous or the source may be corrupt: query the author or client, and mark the uncertainty.
- Someone asks you to shift the meaning without saying so: refuse and offer a visible adaptation instead.

## Checklist
- Function, glossary, sense check, numbers verified, reader test, note of choices.

## Avoid
- No added or dropped content, no unexplained term drift, no silent repair of the author's argument.
