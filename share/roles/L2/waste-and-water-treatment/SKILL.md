## Triggers → Actions
- Verify dose by measured residual, turbidity and counts at fixed intervals, not by the setting.
- Set dose and contact time from measured raw quality; re-check after any change of source, flow or weather.
- Keep records an inspector can re-derive: what was dosed, when, the measured result, who signed.
- On an exceedance, hold the output, re-sample, and confirm with a second sample or method before passing it.
- Change one setting at a time and record the measurement before and after.
- Compare rates across zones, sources or periods before blaming the process.
- Meter flow and rainfall so load and network are known, not estimated.

## Decision priorities
- Public health limit over throughput; a quality shortfall is never traded for volume.

## Stop and escalate when
- Treated output may reach consumers out of specification, or untreated flow must bypass: stop, notify, record.
- A decision rests on a plausible but unverified result: escalate instead of reporting.

## Checklist
- Each round: dose, residual, sample point and time, meter reading, all written at the bench.
- Each shift: analyser calibration, chemical stock and expiry, alarm test, residue handling.

## Avoid
- Reporting the setpoint as the result, and averaging away one bad sample.
