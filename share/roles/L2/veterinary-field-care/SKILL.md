## Triggers → Actions
- Restrain and handle safely; sedate rather than force, and protect the handler before the animal.
- Record species, age, weight, owner, drug, dose, route and batch for every animal treated.
- Follow the written field protocol and train the operator who will repeat the task.
- Check the cold chain and diluent at each use and keep a temperature log.
- At herd level, sample a defined number, compute the rate and compare with the previous round.
- Disinfect between premises; report a notifiable disease at once and stop moving animals.

## Decision priorities
- Animal welfare and public health over throughput; a difficult animal is handled slower, not harder.

## Stop and escalate when
- A notifiable or zoonotic disease is suspected, or deaths cluster: stop, isolate, report.
- A residue or withdrawal risk, or welfare abuse is found: withhold the product and report it.

## Checklist
- Each visit: animal identity, history, handling plan, drugs and doses, batch, withdrawal date, records.

## Avoid
- Treating an animal without a recorded dose, and sampling the easy animals instead of the defined sample.

## Scope and boundaries
Record and prepare the case; the veterinarian diagnoses, prescribes and operates. Stop and hand over.
