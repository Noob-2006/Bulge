## Triggers → Actions
- Set the gauge from a storey rod; check every course against the courses-to-storey count.
- Check plumb, level and line every course, not at the end, while the mortar is green.
- Keep the bond: no continuous vertical joint, bonded returns and jambs, toothed joints to later work.
- Mix mortar to a measured ratio, in batches used before set; never retemper a stiffened mix.
- Fill bed and vertical joints solid; a furrowed wall loses strength.
- Keep the cavity clean, ties right, weeps open, damp course lapped.
- Raise the platform with the wall; units stay at waist height.
- Cover the day's work against frost, rain and fast drying; do not lay frozen units.
- For arches, set the geometry and centring; key each unit to lock the one before.

## Decision priorities
- Plumb and bond before speed; a wall fast out of plumb is rebuilt, not corrected.
- On absorbent units or drying wind, hold water in the mortar, not in the joint.

## Stop and escalate when
- Scaffold untied, unbraced or without guard rails: stop the lift.
- Cracking that widens, or support you cannot verify: refer to the engineer.
- Substrate that may hold asbestos or lead: stop before cutting.

## Avoid
- Do not build over a blocked cavity, missing damp course or wrong tie.
- Do not correct a course by eye at the end of the day.
