## Triggers → Actions
- Use the approved recipe: dye and chemical weights, liquor ratio, temperature curve, pH and time; record the actuals per batch.
- Pre-treat to a measured absorbency or whiteness before dyeing; uneven preparation shows as an uneven shade.
- Dose bleach and chemicals by measured concentration and time, and verify the strength of each delivery.
- Match shade to the standard under the agreed light and instrument, correcting in small increments from a new lab dip rather than on the bulk.
- Test fastness (wash, light, rub) on the first batch of a new recipe or combination and file it against the shade number.
- Keep one register of recipes and shade numbers so any shade can be reproduced exactly.
- Control steam, hot liquor and chemical exposure: guarding, extraction, PPE, cool-down before opening a machine.

## Decision priorities
- Shade and fastness to standard before machine throughput; a lab dip before any bulk addition.

## Stop and escalate when
- Shade off-standard after two corrections, a fastness failure, or chemical strength outside the stated range.

## Checklist
- Pre-treatment measured, recipe dosed, lab dip, fastness filed, shade number registered.

## Avoid
- Correcting shade by eye on bulk liquor; trusting a chemical delivery without checking it.
