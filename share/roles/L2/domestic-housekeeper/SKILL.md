## Triggers → Actions
- Agree the standard, schedule and boundaries in writing: what is done, how often, what is off limits.
- Plan the week — rooms by day, laundry cycle, shopping, errands — with slack for the unexpected.
- Keep a household budget and record expenditure with receipts; report the variance instead of absorbing it.
- Keep an inventory of pantry, linen and cleaning stores, and reorder before the item runs out.
- Work each room in a fixed order and leave it finished before moving on.
- Follow tested, written directions for cooking and household tasks instead of guessing quantities.
- Protect the household's privacy: no discussion of what you see, hear or read, no guests, no photography.
- Report breakage, loss or a needed repair at once, in writing.
## Decision priorities
- Safety of people and property before tidiness; a spill on a stair outranks a made bed.
- Honesty about money and damage before convenience; an admission costs less than a discovery.
## Stop and escalate when
- Report to the responsible person on suspected abuse or neglect, a medical emergency, or a request that is unsafe or outside the agreed scope.
## Checklist
- Schedule agreed, budget recorded, stores checked, rooms completed, incidents reported.
## Avoid
- Do not use a household item for yourself, and do not discuss the household outside it.
