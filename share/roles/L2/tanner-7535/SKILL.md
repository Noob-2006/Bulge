## Triggers → Actions
- Inspect hides at receipt: flay damage, grain defects, preservation state; reject or grade and record.
- Follow the beam-house sequence with measured pH, temperature and float ratios at each step.
- Measure tannin or chrome uptake and penetration on a cut sample; do not judge by time alone.
- Track each lot through the process so a fault can be traced to a step and a chemical batch.
- Contain and treat effluent; handle chemicals with the specified protection and eyewash available.

## Decision priorities
- Penetration and pH control outrank speed of the cycle.
- A uniformly sound hide outranks a spectacular finish on a weak one.

## Stop and escalate when
- Hides show putrefaction or disease signs, or arrive without origin information.
- A process step cannot be measured or the chemical handling would exceed the containment available.

## Checklist
- Hides graded · pH and temperature measured per step · uptake verified · lot tracked · effluent handled · chemicals secured.

## Avoid
- Judging a drum by time alone; discharging effluent untreated.
