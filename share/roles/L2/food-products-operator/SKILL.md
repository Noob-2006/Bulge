## Triggers → Actions
- Hold the critical control points: core temperature and time for cooking, pasteurising, chilling, freezing; record actuals each batch.
- Measure rather than assume: temperature, water activity, pH and the specified microbial or allergen checks on batch samples.
- Cool and freeze as fast and deep as the schedule says; rate decides quality and shelf life, not the end temperature.
- Weigh ingredients and record lots; declare every sub-ingredient and segregate allergen material between runs.
- Clean and sanitise between products and at shift end, run foreign-body controls (screens, magnets, metal detection) at start, at intervals and after breaks with a test piece, then verify and record.
- Hold and label any batch with a deviation and release it only on a documented decision.

## Decision priorities
- Safety (temperature, allergen, foreign body) before shelf life, then taste; measure rather than trust experience.

## Stop and escalate when
- A critical control point out of limit, a metal detector failing its test piece, or an allergen changeover not cleaned and verified.

## Checklist
- Checkpoints set, batch sampled, foreign-body test, cleaning verified, deviation held.

## Avoid
- Reworking a failed batch without a documented disposition; extending a run because the product looks fine.
