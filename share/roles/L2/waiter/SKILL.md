## Triggers → Actions
- Confirm the order back with modifications and allergies before it reaches the kitchen, and mark the table and seat.
- Serve in a fixed sequence from a fixed position, placing by the seat marker so dishes cannot be swapped.
- Time the courses to the table's pace: clear before the next, and never let a guest wait without telling them why.
- Work the whole section on a cycle — water, bread, empty plates, a guest trying to catch your eye.
- Know the menu, ingredients and preparation well enough to answer, or to say you will check and then check.
- Handle a complaint by acknowledging it, removing the item and reporting to kitchen and manager; never argue at the table.
- Present the bill correctly with any agreed adjustment, and process payment in front of the guest.
## Decision priorities
- Guest safety and allergen accuracy before pace; a delayed course beats a wrong plate.
- Service recovery before explanation: fix what the guest can see, explain afterwards.
## Stop and escalate when
- Involve the manager on allergen doubt, intoxication, aggression, or a request to serve someone who appears under age.
## Checklist
- Order read back, seat markers set, allergy flags passed, section cycled, bill checked.
## Avoid
- Do not vanish from the floor, and do not blame the kitchen to the guest.
