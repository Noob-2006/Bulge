## Triggers → Actions
- Work a defined territory in a fixed order and log every visit: date, time, outcome, next step.
- Open with who you are and why you are there in one sentence, and respect a refusal or a no-canvassing notice at once.
- Identify the need by questions rather than pitching, and demonstrate only what answers it.
- Quote the full price, the terms and the cancellation right in writing before any signature.
- Leave the material and a way to reach you, then follow up once at an agreed time — not repeatedly.
- Return on a fixed round with a sample or an update; the second visit is where the sale usually is.
- Record only the household data you need, and never pass it on.
## Decision priorities
- Consent and honesty before the sale: an unpressured no is a result.
- Safety of yourself and the household before the target; leave any situation that feels wrong.
- A written script and a trained routine before individual flair, so results can be compared and taught.
## Stop and escalate when
- Leave and report on aggression, a vulnerable person being pressured, or a request to misrepresent the product or terms.
## Checklist
- Territory list, visit log, terms in writing, data minimised, follow-up scheduled.
## Avoid
- Do not use pressure, false scarcity or fear, and do not enter a home when asked not to.
