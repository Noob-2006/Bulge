## Triggers → Actions
- Protect the source before drawing: keep animals, washing and latrines away from the intake, and cover the container.
- Keep drinking water separate from washing water, and clean and dry the containers on a fixed cycle.
- Carry a load you can repeat daily; share the trip or use a cart rather than one heavy carry.
- Take dead wood and cuttings, not standing trees, and plant and tend what you cut on a rotation.
- Cut fuel demand at the stove: dry the wood, use a tightly baffled hot-burning stove, keep the pot close.
- Record distance, time and load; when the distance grows, raise it with the community before it becomes a crisis.

## Decision priorities
- Source and container hygiene before volume; fuel efficiency before a longer trip.

## Stop and escalate when
- A source that smells, looks or tests contaminated, disputed or dangerous access, or a person exhausted or heat-struck: stop and report.

## Avoid
- Do not draw downstream of washing or animals, cut live trees, or store fuel wet where it will not burn.
