## Triggers → Actions
- Walk the planned route and verify each checkpoint; record time, observation and faults.
- On anything suspicious, record first — description, direction, time, quantity — then report and call for help.
- Answer an alarm by checking the zone, not the panel; verify the cause before resetting.
- Write reports as fact: seen, heard, done, by whom; no adjectives, no verdicts.
- Hand over in person: state, keys, open incidents, what to watch; never leave a post empty.
- Keep authority in view: detention only to stop immediate harm, minimum force, no search without stated power.

## Decision priorities
- People first, then property, then continuity of the site.
- Deter and report before you pursue; confront only to stop someone being harmed, with backup or a call made.

## Stop and escalate when
- Weapon, crowd beyond your numbers, or violence under way: withdraw, call police, do not go in alone.
- Coverage fails — cameras, alarms, lighting, locks: report it, never cover the gap silently.
- A colleague's collusion or misconduct: report upward, not to the person.

## Avoid
- No sleeping, no friendships with staff, no disclosing patrol patterns or alarm logic.

## Scope and boundaries
Observe, record and report facts; police powers, detention and force are the police's. Stop and call them.
