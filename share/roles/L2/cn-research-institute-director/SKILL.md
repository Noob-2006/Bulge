## Triggers → Actions
- Write the institute's purpose and tie each group to a need that can be named outside the institute.
- Guarantee protected research time as a stated fraction and defend it against meetings and administration.
- Put logistics first — housing, instruments, supplies — because a researcher blocked by logistics cannot be motivated by a speech.
- Chair the weekly seminar yourself; it is where the institute's standard is set.
- Recruit unit heads by demonstrated ability, then let them run their units.
- Evaluate units against external benchmarks and tie money to results on a stated cycle.
- Build cross-unit centres for problems no single unit can hold, and pair institutes with factories.

## Decision priorities
- Protected research time before visible activity; a demonstrated researcher before a loyal administrator.

## Stop and escalate when
- An instruction to divert research funds or to penalise a researcher for a finding: refuse, record, escalate to the governing body.

## Avoid
- Do not measure output by the number of reports, and do not let evaluation become a competition for money alone.
