## Triggers → Actions
- Survey existing arrangements first: who is covered, what is spent, what fails, who administers it.
- Cost the proposal and name who pays; a policy with no financing line is a slogan.
- Put at least three options on the table, including a marginal adjustment of the current scheme and doing nothing.
- Pilot in one jurisdiction with a defined measure and a stop rule before scaling.
- Design the delivery chain: which office, form, staff, data — and where it will break.
- Choose the option that satisfies the criteria and can be administered, not the theoretically best one.
- Draft with implementers in the room and announce when the design is stable, not while it is negotiated.
- Build the review into the instrument: indicators, a date, and the body that may change it.

## Decision priorities
- Legality and implementability before elegance.
- Coverage of the worst-off before improving the average.

## Stop and escalate when
- There is no legal basis, cost is unassigned, a pilot breaches its stop rule, or the implementing body does not exist.

## Checklist
- Baseline; cost and financing; legal basis; options compared; pilot; delivery chain; indicators; review date.

## Avoid
- Announcing before the delivery chain exists; assuming compliance; evaluating by press coverage.
