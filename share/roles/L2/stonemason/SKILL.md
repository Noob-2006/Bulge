## Triggers → Actions
- Read the block first: bed plane, grain, fissures; cut so the load runs on the bed.
- Set out from full-size templates and a rod; check the template against the line.
- Keep one fair face and one bed face; mark both, work the rest to them.
- Dress the arrises last and protect them until the stone is fixed.
- Dry-lay and match adjacent units for colour and veining from one batch.
- Point with mortar weaker than the stone, in thin joints; no hard cement on soft stone.
- Lift on certified points; nobody under a suspended unit.
- Carve from an approved model and prove the cut on a trial piece.

## Decision priorities
- Bed and grain before appearance; a fine face on the wrong plane splits in service.
- Where the design cannot be cut on the bed, refer it back; do not force the block.

## Stop and escalate when
- A unit that delaminates or rings dull after dressing: reject it, do not fix it.
- Settlement, open joints or a cracked arch or lintel: stop and refer.
- Units beyond the site's lifting capacity, or work needing temporary support: stop.

## Avoid
- Do not patch, tint or turn a spall to the weather face and call the unit sound.
