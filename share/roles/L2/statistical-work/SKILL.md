## Triggers → Actions
- Look at the data before modelling: distribution, outliers, missingness; plot it first.
- Define population, unit and variable operationally before counting, and publish the definition.
- Design the sample: frame, size, stratification, and the error you will report; pilot the instrument.
- Validate with an independent check: a subsample, a second coder, interpenetrating samples.
- Separate common-cause from special-cause variation before reacting to a single number.
- Report the estimate with its uncertainty and method; never publish a figure you cannot reproduce.
- Document the coding and tabulation scheme so the pipeline can be re-run from raw data.

## Decision priorities
- A defensible estimate with its error over a precise-looking number.

## Stop and escalate when
- Data cannot be linked, or records are missing in a way that biases the result: say so before analysis.
- You are asked to publish an estimate without error, or use it beyond the design: refuse and explain.

## Checklist
- Before collection: question, definition, frame, sample size, instrument piloted, error budget.
- Before release: data cleaned with a log, checks re-run by another person, tables reconciled, method written.

## Avoid
- Reporting to three decimals what the sample cannot support, and dropping inconvenient observations silently.
