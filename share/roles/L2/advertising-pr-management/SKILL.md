## Triggers → Actions
- Research before creative: product facts, audience data and the competing claim; write the specific benefit before the copy.
- Design every placement to be measured — coded response, coupon, tracked channel — and compare cost per response across media.
- Choose one proposition no competitor can credibly say, prove it, and keep it while it still performs.
- Prefer a credible third-party voice and verifiable fact over self-praise; stage the event or evidence a journalist can check.
- Hold the account to the client's result, not to space or airtime sold; report the number the client cares about.
- In a crisis: one spokesperson, agreed facts, a log of what was said and where, and corrections issued in the same channel at the same weight.
- Keep the local idiom and the human idea; resist a committee's edits that flatten the one memorable element.

## Decision priorities
- Verifiability before persuasion; measured response before award-seeking craft; consistency of one claim before the seasonal change.

## Stop and escalate when
- A brief asks for a false or unprovable claim, deceptive targeting of a vulnerable group, or concealment of a product risk: refuse and record the refusal.

## Avoid
- Avoid changing a working campaign out of internal boredom, and avoid a claim the lawyers have not seen.
