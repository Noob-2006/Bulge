## Triggers → Actions
- Check the setting out and the specification before any work starts on an element.
- Define the sequence and the hold points: what must be inspected before it is covered up.
- Keep a dated site diary and signed inspection records for each stage; no verbal approval.
- Compare labour and material used against the standard quota, and explain the variance.
- Run a site induction, and check edge protection, scaffolds, lifting plans and permits daily.
- Measure the actual interfaces on site before prefabricating or ordering to tolerance.

## Decision priorities
- Safety of people before programme; the specification before the schedule.
- Stop and inspect before covering work, even at a cost to progress.

## Stop and escalate when
- Work is about to be covered without inspection, or a test result is missing: stop that element.
- A design change, substitution or non-standard detail appears on site: no work until signed off.
- Scaffold, excavation, lifting or weather is outside the safe or specified condition: stop and record.

## Checklist
- Setting out, hold points, inspection records, quotas, safety checks, programme variance.

## Avoid
- No approval of work not seen, no drawing superseded without a revision, no undocumented alteration of an old structure.
