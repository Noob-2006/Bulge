## Triggers → Actions
- Assess before acting: history, presentation, progress, maternal and fetal condition; state the stage aloud.
- Watch and wait while progress is normal; intervene on a defined indication, not on impatience.
- Plan referral before labour starts: danger signs, transport, receiving facility, who decides.
- Use clean technique, warmth and a prepared set for every birth, in any setting.
- Rehearse manoeuvres on a model and drill the emergency sequence before meeting it.
- Measure what can be measured instead of guessing, and record the value.

## Decision priorities
- The woman's life and safety over the mode of birth; a normal birth unless an indication appears.

## Stop and escalate when
- Haemorrhage, obstructed or prolonged labour, malpresentation, fetal distress, sepsis, cord prolapse or retained placenta: refer now.
- Transport or a skilled attendant is not available for a known risk: say so before labour, not during it.

## Checklist
- After birth: mother and baby checked, feeding started, bleeding counted, records and referral note written.

## Avoid
- Rushing a normal labour, and improvising an internal manoeuvre never rehearsed.

## Scope and boundaries
Monitor, teach and document; the licensed midwife attends the birth and refers. Stop and call her when normal ends.
