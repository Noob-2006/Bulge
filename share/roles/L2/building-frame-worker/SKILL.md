## Triggers → Actions
- Choose the scaffold for its duty and ground; ties to the specified spacing, into the structure.
- Brace both ways as you rise; use a lift only when tied, decked, railed, toe-boarded.
- Check the load path — scaffold, ties, structure — and its capacity for those loads.
- Prefabricated frames: set out from one datum, check diagonals and plumb before fixing.
- Keep temporary guys and bracing until permanent stability is in.
- Before demolition, isolate services, survey hazards, set the exclusion zone.
- Demolish in reverse order; never undermine or pull past the planned line.
- One person controls the operation, the plant and the exclusion zone.

## Decision priorities
- Calculated temporary works before experience; where judgement is the only support, do not start.
- If stability depends on a member you remove, put the temporary support in first.

## Stop and escalate when
- Ties cannot reach the specified spacing, or the base is soft: stop and redesign.
- Services live, asbestos suspected, or lines within reach: stop.
- Post-tensioned, prestressed or fire-damaged structure: refer before touching it.

## Avoid
- Do not treat an incomplete scaffold as a platform, or work under one being altered.
- Do not let a machine's reach decide the demolition sequence.
