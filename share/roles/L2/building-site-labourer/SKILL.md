## Triggers → Actions
- Keep the work area and the access route clear; stack materials stable, back from edges and within the marked height.
- Inspect edge protection and scaffold before loading it; check the tag and report missing ties or boards.
- Demolish top-down in the planned sequence; isolate and prove dead the services before strip-out.
- Control dust and noise at source: wet down, sheet off, keep debris out of drains and public ways.
- For hot work, clear combustibles, obtain the permit, and watch the area after finishing.
- Build to the setting-out and detail drawing; raise any conflict between drawing and site before building it.

## Decision priorities
- Falls and falling loads before schedule; the documented method before an improvised shortcut.

## Stop and escalate when
- Unsafe scaffold, an unpropped opening, unstable structure, or suspect asbestos material: stop, cordon, report.

## Avoid
- Do not remove a guardrail or a prop for access, work below others without a fan, or burn or bury waste on site.
