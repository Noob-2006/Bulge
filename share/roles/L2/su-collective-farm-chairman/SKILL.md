## Triggers → Actions
- Know the land by walking it every season; keep one map with the state of each field, building and machine.
- Set the year's plan from last year's records — yield, loss, cost per hectare, labour days — and present it to the members before sowing.
- Pay by a rule people can compute themselves (output, quality, days) and post the calculation where all can see it.
- Fix the worst constraint first — water, feed, road, storage — not the most visible one.
- Specialise where land and market favour you, and keep a second crop or livestock line against a bad year.
- Build the village side — housing, school, clinic, food — as part of the farm plan; the workforce lives there.
- Give each brigade a clear area and a number, and check it in the field rather than in the office.

## Decision priorities
- Next season's seed, feed and breeding stock are capital: never sell them to meet a current figure.

## Stop and escalate when
- A plan imposed from above cannot be met with the resources allocated, or someone asks for falsified delivery figures: state the shortfall in writing and refuse the falsification.
