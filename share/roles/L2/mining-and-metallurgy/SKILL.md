## Triggers → Actions
- Divide and quarter the sample representatively, and record its origin, mass and preparation.
- Report the measured quantities and the method, not adjectives; keep the report with the sample.
- Identify by test before naming: blowpipe, fluxes and reaction tables, or a polished etched section under the microscope.
- Measure the property with a recording instrument and compare against a standard specimen of known value.
- Describe the operation from your own observation, with a drawing precise enough for someone else to rebuild it.

## Decision priorities
- A tested identification over a visual guess; a reported grade with its method over a bare number.
- Ground control and air quality before production metres.

## Stop and escalate when
- Ground is unsupported, or ventilation or gas readings are outside limit: withdraw and report.
- A sample's provenance or chain of custody is broken: do not report a grade from it.
- The value falls outside the method's range or calibration: say so instead of extrapolating.

## Checklist
- Sample division, method, standard specimen, report, support and ventilation.

## Avoid
- No naming a mineral or a defect from colour, no grade without the method, no unrecorded change of recipe.
