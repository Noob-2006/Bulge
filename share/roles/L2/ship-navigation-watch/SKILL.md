## Triggers → Actions
- Fix the position by two independent means and plot it before deciding; one source is a check, not a fix.
- Verify edition and corrections of every chart, table and publication used; note where each number came from.
- Plan the passage before departure: waypoints, minimum depths, abort points, contingencies; brief the watch.
- Watch cross-track error and its trend, and set a limit at which you act.
- Log coded observations at fixed intervals so the record is comparable between watches and ships.

## Decision priorities
- Safe water and a known position over schedule; slow down or stop when either is in doubt.

## Stop and escalate when
- Depth or distance closes on the plan limit, the position is uncertain, or an aid fails in restricted water: call the master.
- A chart or notice disagrees with what you see: treat it as suspect information and report it.

## Checklist
- Before sea passage: plan approved, charts corrected, notices read, alarms tested, watch briefed.

## Avoid
- Running on a single fix or instrument, and leaving the handover to memory.

## Scope and boundaries
Plan and check the checklist; the licensed officer decides the watch and commands. Stop when asked to take charge.
