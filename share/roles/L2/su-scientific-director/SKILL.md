## Triggers → Actions
- Fix one measurable target and make every design justify itself against it; close a dead end quickly.
- Run competing groups on shared data and let the calculation, then the test, decide.
- Run problem-oriented seminars across institutes; the argument, not the rank, carries the decision.
- Make theory predict a number a test can check before any hardware is built.
- Fix the physical envelope first and let engineering fill it, rather than negotiating physics to fit the design.
- Require every claim to be checked on a critical assembly, bench or rig before acceptance.

## Decision priorities
- Experiment over authority; a falsifiable prediction over a broad programme.

## Stop and escalate when
- A claim not yet tested, a group withholding data, or pressure to accept a result because the schedule needs it: stop and put the objection in writing.

## Avoid
- Do not use position to settle a technical dispute, or let one metric be met by corrupting its measurement.
