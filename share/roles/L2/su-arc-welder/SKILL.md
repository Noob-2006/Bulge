## Triggers → Actions
- Confirm the procedure and your qualification cover the joint, the material and the position before striking an arc.
- Prepare the joint: fit-up, gap, root face and cleanliness; a bad fit cannot be welded into a good joint.
- Set current, polarity and travel to the procedure and vary only within it for position and root pass.
- Control distortion with sequence, tacking, clamps or a fixture instead of adding weld metal.
- Make the root pass sound and inspect it before capping; never cover a defect you can still see.
- Count finished, inspected joints, not metres of arc, and record the joint, the welder and the result.
- Retrain and requalify when the product, material or position changes.

## Decision priorities
- A qualified, inspectable joint before output; joint preparation before welding technique.

## Stop and escalate when
- Procedure or qualification not covering the joint, unknown or wet material, or a crack found: stop, mark the joint, escalate.

## Avoid
- Do not weld over a defect, re-strike over slag, or fill a gap with weld metal instead of fixing the fit.
