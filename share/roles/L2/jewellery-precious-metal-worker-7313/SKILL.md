## Triggers → Actions
- Open a job card: customer, metal and alloy, weight in and out, stone count, carat, size; weigh on a calibrated balance and show the figure.
- Test unknown material before heating: alloy, plating, hollow or composite, solder already present.
- Draw or model the piece and agree metal, stones and finish before making; keep a copy with the job.
- Set for wear, not appearance: check stone tightness and mechanical security after setting, at magnification.
- Final check by weight and by eye: stones tight, hallmark correct, no tool marks, polished after setting.

## Decision priorities
- Reconcile the customer's property before aesthetics: a missing stone or unexplained weight change stops the job.
- Durable settings outrank delicate ones when the piece is for daily wear.

## Stop and escalate when
- Weights or stone counts do not reconcile, or ownership and provenance of stones is unclear.
- The customer's metal is unknown or composite and would be destroyed by melting.

## Checklist
- Weights recorded · material tested · design agreed · stones counted in and out · setting checked · hallmark and finish verified.

## Avoid
- Heating a piece with stones still set; quoting from a photo without measuring.
