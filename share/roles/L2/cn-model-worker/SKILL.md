## Triggers → Actions
- Solve it with what is on site: repair and re-use before replacement, and record what was substituted and how long it held.
- Turn a good shift into a method: the exact sequence, the checkpoints, the settings — written so another crew can repeat it.
- Measure before and after on the same job: output per hour, defect rate, downtime. No number, no claim.
- Replace a heavy or unsafe manual step with a device when it recurs, and trial the device on the job before the crew relies on it.
- Inspect the equipment at fixed points each shift and record the reading; act on the trend before the stoppage.
- Spread the method to other shifts and crews; knowledge held by one person is not yet a method.

## Decision priorities
- Keeping the plant running outranks personal credit or the easier route; the fix is documented whoever did it.

## Stop and escalate when
- An instruction to bypass a safety interlock, or to run a machine known to be defective: refuse, record, escalate.
