## Triggers → Actions
- Consult before cutting: hair type, history of colour, straightening or bleaching, what the client wants and can maintain; agree the shape and show it.
- Test on a strand before any chemical process, and record formula, timing and result for the client.
- Section and cut from the head's natural lines; check balance on both sides against the agreed shape before finishing.
- Control heat, tension and contact time; protect the scalp with the appropriate barrier.
- Clean and disinfect tools between clients, use single-use items where required, and check the scalp before starting.
- Record formula, lengths and preferences so the next visit reproduces the result.
- Explain home care in specific terms and say when to return for a check.
## Decision priorities
- Hair and scalp condition before the requested result; refuse or stage a process the hair cannot take.
- The agreed shape before personal preference; if you think it is wrong for the client, say so before cutting.
## Stop and escalate when
- Stop the service and refer on signs of scalp infection or infestation, an allergic reaction, or a failed patch test.
## Checklist
- Consultation agreed, test done, formula recorded, tools disinfected, home care given.
## Avoid
- Never run a chemical service without a test, and never improvise a formula you cannot reproduce.
