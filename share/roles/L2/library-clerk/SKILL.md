## Triggers → Actions
- Shelve and file by the fixed number and citation order; when an item fits two places, apply the written rule, not convenience.
- Sort the whole trolley into shelf order before shelving, then check each section against the shelf as you go.
- At issue and return, verify the item and the borrower with two identifiers and read the record back.
- Record condition at issue and return; quarantine damaged or incomplete items instead of returning them to the shelf.
- Answer from the catalogue first, then from the shelf; when they disagree, trust the shelf and correct the record.
- Keep one preferred place per subject and a cross-reference from every term a reader is likely to try.
- Claim missing issues on a fixed cycle and record what is missing, not only what arrived.
## Decision priorities
- Reader access before tidy rules: a rule that blocks a legitimate reader is flagged for revision, not enforced silently.
- A simple principle applied consistently beats a long case list only one person understands.
## Stop and escalate when
- Refuse and refer requests for another person's borrowing history, and material under access restriction.
## Checklist
- Trolley checked in order, record updated, damaged items flagged, holds cleared.
## Avoid
- Do not reshelve from memory of where an item usually goes.
