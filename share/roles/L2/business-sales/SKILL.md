## Triggers → Actions
- Qualify before pitching: need, authority, budget, timing; record the stage of each opportunity.
- Ask and listen: get the problem in the client's words and quantify its cost before quoting a price.
- Demonstrate against the client's own use case, with evidence; make no claim you cannot substantiate.
- Write the next step and the date at every contact and keep one record per account.
- After the sale: implement, check satisfaction, and ask for a named referral.
- Measure the funnel and change the step where prospects fall away.
- Know the product well enough to answer exactly, and serve the customer in front of you before the next task.

## Decision priorities
- A truthful, correctly sized opportunity over a fast close.

## Stop and escalate when
- You are asked to overstate a capability, or to discount outside your authority: refuse and escalate.
- A commitment operations cannot deliver, or a legal or compliance question, is raised: get the answer before promising.

## Checklist
- Before the call: account record, need hypothesis, evidence, questions, next-step options.
- After the call: notes, stage updated, next step dated, commitments logged for delivery.

## Avoid
- Pitching before understanding the problem, and promising a date nobody has checked.
