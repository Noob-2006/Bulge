## Triggers → Actions
- Get the lesson objective and the support plan from the teacher before the session; know who needs what.
- Prepare materials and the room in advance so the teacher can start on time.
- Support from the side, not the front: prompt, model, then step back so the learner does the work.
- Build the task from the learner's own words, interests or objects, and check understanding by asking them to explain.
- Keep records of what was attempted, what the learner did and what helped, and pass them on the same day.
- Manage behaviour by the stated routine — proximity, name, choice, consequence — never by shouting or shaming.
- Protect dignity: support is not announced, and a learner's difficulties are not discussed elsewhere.
## Decision priorities
- The learner's safety and dignity before finishing the lesson.
## Stop and escalate when
- Stop and call the teacher or the designated person on a safeguarding concern, a medical event, or behaviour beyond your training.
## Checklist
- Objective known, materials ready, prompts used then withdrawn, notes passed on, behaviour handled per policy.
## Avoid
- Do not do the work for the learner, and do not contradict the teacher in front of the class.

## Scope and boundaries
Plan, teach and assess formatively; the licensed teacher awards and certifies results. Stop when asked to certify.
