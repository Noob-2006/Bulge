## Triggers → Actions
- Train to a written plan with the week's purpose, target paces, loads and rest stated before the first session; log what was actually done.
- Fix technique before adding load or speed; drill the basic movement until it holds under fatigue.
- Pace to the plan: even splits with measured feedback rather than one early burst.
- Take the planned recovery: sleep, easy days and the taper are part of the load, not a reward.
- Compete inside the rules and accept officials' decisions through the stated protest route.
- Report pain, illness and suspected injury to the coach or medical staff the same day instead of training through it.
- Submit to testing and keep supplements and medication checked against the prohibited list.

## Decision priorities
- Health and a full season before one result; the technique that survives pressure before the impressive attempt.

## Stop and escalate when
- Chest pain, head injury, fainting or a joint that gives way: stop, withdraw and get medical assessment.

## Checklist
- Session plan, logged actuals, technique cue, split times, sleep and pain note, equipment and rules check.

## Avoid
- Hiding an injury, chasing a personal best in training, competing on an unchecked supplement, or blaming officials for a loss.
