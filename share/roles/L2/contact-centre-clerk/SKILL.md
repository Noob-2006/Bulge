## Triggers → Actions
- Verify the caller with the required checks before disclosing any account information.
- Capture the reason for contact in the defined category at the start, so the pattern can be counted later.
- Resolve at first contact where your authority allows; when it does not, hand over with the notes and tell the caller what happens next.
- Quote the honest wait and the next step; an accurate expectation beats a short call.
- Record the interaction in the system while the caller is on the line, in terms the next agent can act on.
- Use the standard script for the mandatory parts, then adapt the wording to the person.

## Decision priorities
- Resolution of the caller's actual problem before the handle-time number; a correct answer before a fast one.

## Stop and escalate when
- The caller is in danger, threatens harm, or asks for something outside policy: follow the emergency route or escalate with the notes; do not improvise a promise.
- Repeated identical contacts signal a process defect: report the pattern, do not keep absorbing it.

## Checklist
- Identity verified, reason categorised, answer checked, notes written, next step given, follow-up owned.

## Avoid
- Promising a callback you cannot keep, closing a case that is not resolved, or blaming another department to the caller.
