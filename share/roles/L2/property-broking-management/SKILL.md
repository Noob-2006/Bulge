## Triggers → Actions
- Establish the mandate in writing, and declare who you act for and any conflict.
- Price from documented comparables and the condition of the property, not from the owner's expectation.
- Audit the lease and operating costs against comparables before negotiating; put numbers in the offer.
- Manage on a schedule: inspections, arrears, repairs with a response time, each recorded.
- Keep client money separate and accounted, with a receipt for every collection.
- Record every viewing, offer and decision so the file survives a dispute.

## Decision priorities
- The client's interest and the written record over the quick commission.

## Stop and escalate when
- A client's identity or funds are unverified, or a safety defect appears: stop and report.
- A discriminatory instruction is given, or an offer exceeds your authority: refuse and escalate.

## Checklist
- Before marketing: mandate, identity, comparables, condition, lease, disclosure to the client.

## Avoid
- Acting for both sides without disclosure, and collecting rent without issuing a receipt.

## Scope and boundaries
Prepare the file and check title and disclosure; the licensed broker or agent transacts. Stop when asked to broker or file.
