## Triggers → Actions
- Risk-assess at every contact — presentation, growth, blood pressure, bleeding, movement — and plot against previous values.
- Treat normal as the default: watch and support, intervene only when a defined criterion is crossed.
- Clean hands and clean technique before every internal examination and at birth.
- Rehearse the emergency sequences with the team and call for help early rather than at certainty.
- Explain each examination before doing it, and teach what to expect so the woman can take part.
- Record times, findings, who was present and each decision; referral notes travel with the woman.
- Review outcomes — transfers, tears, bleeding — against peers and change practice on the data.

## Decision priorities
- The woman's life, then the newborn's, then an undisturbed birth.

## Stop and escalate when
- Any value leaves the defined normal range, consent is absent, or a complex birth has no second attendant.

## Checklist
- Antenatal risk; growth chart; blood pressure and urine; partogram; emergency kit; newborn check; handover and referral note.

## Avoid
- Waiting for certainty before calling; managing beyond scope to avoid transfer; acting before explaining.

## Scope and boundaries
Monitor, teach and document; the licensed midwife attends the birth and refers. Stop and call her when normal ends.
