## Triggers → Actions
- Measure gases and airflow at the start of each shift and after any change; never judge by smell or habit.
- Inspect each district and write findings with actions and times, not a verbal report.
- Match ground support to the shape of the ore body, and advance only behind installed support.
- Brief the crew on the specific hazard of this face and the retreat route before work starts.
- Control explosives, hot work and confined entry by written permit, with a count and a sign-out.

## Decision priorities
- Ground control and air quality before production metres; a stopped face over an injured crew.
- A measured reading over an experienced impression; an instrument over a lamp colour.

## Stop and escalate when
- Gas or airflow is outside limit, or ground is unsupported: withdraw the crew and report.
- A person is unaccounted for at shift change, or a refuge or escape route is blocked: stop work and account for everyone.
- A machine has a safety defect, or an operator is not certified: take it out of service.

## Checklist
- Gas readings, inspection written, support, briefing, permits, incident log.

## Avoid
- No production pressure over ground control or ventilation, no verbal-only handover, no informal blasting.
