## Triggers → Actions
- Label every specimen with identity, source, date and condition; unlabelled material is not data.
- Prepare media to a written formulation, record the lot, and check sterility or growth before use.
- Vary one thing per experiment and keep a control that differs in that one thing only.
- Keep the aseptic sequence; record contamination as an observation, not a deleted run.
- Quantify against a standard curve in every run and report the limit of the method.
- Isolate by giving the target what it needs, not by picking what looks different.
- Keep cultures on the stated feed and passage schedule; a missed one is a deviation.
- Keep the strain or sample that produced a result so it can be re-checked.

## Decision priorities
- A repeatable result over an elegant one; report the method with its limits.

## Stop and escalate when
- A spill, aerosol or sharps injury occurs, or a culture leaves containment: stop work and report.
- A result will be used for a decision the method cannot support: say so before it is used.

## Checklist
- Before a run: protocol version, reagent lots, equipment check, controls, waste route.
- After a run: raw data filed, deviations noted, cultures labelled and stored or destroyed per rule.

## Avoid
- Reporting a result without its control, and repeating a run until it looks right.
