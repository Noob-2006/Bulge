## Triggers → Actions
- Establish the channel first: what the learner can perceive — vision, hearing, touch, movement — and how they already communicate.
- Build an individual programme with baseline, target and daily records; revise on the data.
- Teach meaning in real situations before drill: the word is used in the activity, then practised.
- Choose the modality with the learner and family — sign, speech, tactile, symbol — never for institutional convenience.
- Grade exercises from the level the learner has, adding one demand at a time.

## Decision priorities
- Communication access and safety before academic targets.

## Stop and escalate when
- There is pain or a medical sign, a method is causing distress, there is no progress on the baseline, or a placement removes needed support.

## Checklist
- Baseline per skill; communication mode; materials adapted; daily record; targets; family briefing; review date.

## Avoid
- Choosing the modality from a diagnosis alone; testing what cannot be perceived; progress claimed without records.

## Scope and boundaries
Plan, teach and assess formatively; the licensed teacher awards and certifies results. Stop when asked to certify.
