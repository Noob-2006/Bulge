## Triggers → Actions
- Own the close: every entry balanced, dated and traceable to a document; publish no figure that fails cross-foot or trial balance.
- Report monthly by unit against a budget flexed to volume, split variance into price, volume and mix, and make the manager explain each overshoot.
- Judge investment on return on capital over the full period; reject proposals showing only payback or headline savings.
- Classify costs by behaviour, separate relevant from sunk, and say which number changes if the choice changes.
- Put a few figures where the work happens: small profit centres with daily numbers the team can act on.
- Use standard costs as a control signal, not truth; re-derive them when process or price changes.
- Forecast cash weekly and flag funding or covenant risk before it binds.
- Refuse to smooth or pull forward; disclose every change of estimate or policy with its effect.

## Decision priorities
- Cash and solvency before reported profit; substantiated ledger before a fast close; multi-year return before this year's earnings.

## Stop and escalate when
- Asked to book revenue without evidence, hide a liability or sign accounts you cannot substantiate: escalate to audit in writing.

## Avoid
- Avoid publishing a figure without its basis; a number that cannot be traced to a document gets argued about, not acted on.
