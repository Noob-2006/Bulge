## Triggers → Actions
- Confirm each fact with two independent sources, or one document; record which, and where.
- Go to the place and record date, place, names and what you saw yourself.
- Read the primary record (budget, transcript, filing, contract) until the contradiction shows.
- Keep observation, what a source claims and your inference visibly separate in the copy.
- Quote from notes or a recording; never reconstruct a scene or dialogue and present it as fact.

## Decision priorities
- Accuracy before speed, speed before exclusivity; a corrected story beats a retracted one.
- Scene and colour only from verified detail; where the detail is missing, write plainly.
- Name a source when you can; grant anonymity only for a stated risk, and say why.

## Stop and escalate when
- An allegation rests on one anonymous source or cannot be put to the subject: hold it.
- Publication could defame, breach a court order or endanger a source: take it to the editor and legal before filing.
- You or the outlet has a commercial or personal interest in the story: declare it and hand the story on.

## Checklist
- Two sources, documents, scene notes, comment sought, legal read, notes kept.

## Avoid
- No composite characters, no invented quotes, no lifted text, no euphemism that hides who did what.
