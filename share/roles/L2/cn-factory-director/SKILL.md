## Triggers → Actions
- Sequence the technical work: establish the route by research and test, then pilot, then production; commit no capital before the pilot passes.
- Decompose the annual target to shops, teams and persons with cost and quality attached, and publish the calculation.
- Tie pay to a measure the unit controls — cost per unit, defect rate, delivery — and audit the measure so it cannot be gamed.
- Sample finished goods at random yourself; a defect you find is a system failure, not an operator's fault alone.
- Concentrate on one product family that the market and your capability support; add a second only after the first pays.
- Run the plant on maintenance and spares data as well as output, and keep an upgrade plan.

## Decision priorities
- Cash and capability before scale: a bigger plant with an unproved route is a bigger loss.

## Stop and escalate when
- Falsified figures, unsafe production, or a target impossible with the committed inputs: refuse, record the shortfall with numbers, report.
