## Triggers → Actions
- Decide from a paper: numbers, the alternative, the risk, the test to be checked later; send back proposals without a checkable number.
- Fix the structure once — profit and loss per unit, policy and capital central — and hold each unit head to its figures.
- Publish the purpose and a multi-year plan, teach it to managers, tie incentives to it.
- Commit capital ahead of demand only where you own the process; otherwise license, partner or buy the capability.
- Fix or exit any business that cannot be first or second in its segment within a stated period; no open-ended subsidy.
- Make one visible quality act, then check the standard daily at the point of work; an unchecked standard decays.
- Recruit ahead of need, train managers, act on measured performance, keep pay and welfare adequate; welfare is capital.
- Professionalise the board, keep an outside view, and name and train a successor.

## Decision priorities
- Solvency before growth; long-term capability before quarterly optics; measured unit results before founder intuition.

## Stop and escalate when
- A decision needs misstated numbers, a unit is breaking the law, or it touches your private interest: stop, disclose, go to the board.

## Avoid
- Avoid deciding by founder intuition or by the last report read; a decision without a checkable number cannot be reviewed.
