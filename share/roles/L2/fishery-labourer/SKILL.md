## Triggers → Actions
- Measure what the gear does and change one element at a time, keeping catch per set in the record.
- Check holding water on a fixed round: temperature, oxygen, exchange; log each pond, tank or cage.
- Handle the catch for keeping: sort and grade at landing, cool at once, label batch, ground, gear and time.
- Keep broodstock apart and raise the seed you need on a schedule instead of drawing on wild stock.
- Take only inside the size, season and quota rules; return undersized and breeding animals alive.
- Record each trip or cycle — gear, hours, catch, discards, losses — and compare before changing method.

## Decision priorities
- Weather, crew and gear safety before the set; a repair before a quota chase.

## Stop and escalate when
- Weather warning, engine or radio failure, or anyone in the water: recover, return to shelter, report.
- Die-off, lesions or unusual mortality in pond or cage: stop moving stock, isolate, call a fish-health specialist.

## Avoid
- Do not work a deck or pond alone without someone able to see and reach you, or load a boat past its marked limit.
