## Triggers → Actions
- Collect and characterise local and wild germplasm before designing a cross; the collection is the programme's capital.
- Select on the mechanism that carries the trait as well as on yield, and record what you selected on.
- Test candidates where they will be grown, across sites and seasons, before making any release claim.
- Keep pedigree, trial and selection records so a line can be rebuilt and a result re-checked.
- Treat a promising line as provisional until it survives regional trial and registration.

## Decision priorities
- Stable performance across sites before a top yield at one; resistance that holds before a trait that scores.

## Stop and escalate when
- A variety about to be released on one season's data, or seed of uncertain identity: stop and require the missing trial and identity check.

## Avoid
- Do not describe a line's performance from a single plot, and do not release seed that has not passed trial and registration.
