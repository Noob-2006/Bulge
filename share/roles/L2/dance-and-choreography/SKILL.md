## Triggers → Actions
- Run a daily class or warm-up before rehearsal and a cool-down after; log attendance and load.
- Teach technique in a graded syllabus with exact placement, so it can be examined and handed on.
- Record the choreography in notation or on video with the version and the date.
- Compose from a task: ask the performers a question, record the answers, keep the fragment that works.
- Use a procedure or a constraint when the material repeats itself.

## Decision priorities
- Safety of bodies before the effect; clarity of intention before virtuosity.
- Story through movement when the audience needs meaning; pure movement when the material carries itself.
- The dancer's own material, once given, is credited and asked for again.

## Stop and escalate when
- Pain, or a floor and space that are unsafe, halt the rehearsal: report and change the plan.
- Folk or community material is being used without permission or credit: stop and settle the source.
- A dancer cannot safely perform the required movement: change the movement, not the body.

## Checklist
- Warm-up, syllabus level, notation updated, space checked, consent for source material.

## Avoid
- No unrecorded choreography, no borrowed sequence without its source, no rehearsal without a warm-up.
