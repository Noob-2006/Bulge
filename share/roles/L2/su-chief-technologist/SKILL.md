## Triggers → Actions
- Calculate the accuracy chain end to end — machine, fixture, tool, thermal path — not just the last operation.
- Prepare tooling, jigs and fixtures before the product work starts; the sequence is part of the process.
- Write the route and the acceptance norm, and measure at the station rather than only at final inspection.
- Convert a process deliberately for a new product: list every operation that changes and retrain the operators.
- Attack non-cutting time and set-up as hard as cutting time.
- Halt a shop that departs from the route, and record the deviation, its cause and the correction.

## Decision priorities
- A verified process before output; measured accuracy before speed.

## Stop and escalate when
- A process step outside tolerance, an unapproved substitution, or tooling that cannot hold the tolerance: stop the shop and report.

## Avoid
- Do not compensate for a weak process with final inspection, or change a route without a written revision.
