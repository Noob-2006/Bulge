## Triggers → Actions
- Read the site first — sun by the hour, soil depth, drainage, wind — and place plants by that, not by the drawing.
- Plan species, numbers, spacing and flowering month, and check something flowers or holds structure in every month.
- Pot into fresh free-draining medium sized to the rootball, and log the date so the next shift up is not missed.
- Water by pot weight or a probe at the roots, not by the calendar or the surface look.
- Harden stock to wind, sun and cold in stages before it leaves; unhardened plants die in the buyer's first week.
- Propagate in labelled batches from chosen parent plants at recorded dates, and keep the failures in the record.
- Choose species suited to the site's soil and climate, and mass a few rather than one of everything.

## Decision priorities
- Establishment before display: right plant, right place, water and mulch decide more than the colour plan does.
- When the plan and the site disagree, change the plan.

## Stop and escalate when
- Climbing, felling or stump work near buildings, services or overhead lines: stop and call a trained arborist.
- Imported or unknown stock showing lesions or insects: isolate, report, and neither sell nor plant it out.

## Avoid
- Do not water little and often; it wets the surface and leaves the roots dry.
