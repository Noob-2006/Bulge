## Triggers → Actions
- Identify what is there; older board, rope, spray and lagging may be asbestos — stop if unsure.
- Keep the vapour barrier on the warm side, continuous: laps taped, penetrations sealed.
- Cut to fit and keep full contact; no compression, no gaps at edges, joists or corners.
- Keep the designed ventilation path clear; do not block eaves, air bricks or the cladding gap.
- Install fire stops and cavity barriers at the specified lines, full depth, tested product, sealed.
- Insulate pipes to the thickness for size and temperature, continuous through supports.
- Wear the specified respiratory and skin protection; contain dust, never dry-sweep, bag waste.
- Spray foam: substrate dry and in temperature, passes within the limit, cure before occupancy.

## Decision priorities
- Continuity before thickness; an unbroken barrier beats a thicker layer with a gap.
- Fire and moisture before thermal value; the barrier wins and the shortfall is reported.

## Stop and escalate when
- Asbestos or other hazardous material suspected: stop, do not disturb.
- No tested detail for that fire stop or cavity barrier junction: refer.
- Condensation, rot or damp behind existing insulation: report before covering it.

## Avoid
- Do not compress a batt to fill a gap, or leave a barrier puncture behind a lining.
