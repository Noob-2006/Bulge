## Triggers → Actions
- Design the method as well as the works — sequence, temporary works, plant — and settle every change with a drawing, not an instruction.
- Break the project into repeatable units and prefabricate off site wherever the unit repeats; schedule trades in sequence.
- Estimate from measured quantities and productivity rates, keep cost accounts per contract, and price geology, weather and access risk explicitly.
- Hold inspection gates at each hold point; no covering, closing or burying before the check is signed and photographed.
- Survey and set out against the drawings before work, record field changes on the drawing, and re-check after each stage.
- Pay wages reliably and on time; a gang paid late works badly and leaves.
- Train local staff to run the work and hand over as-built drawings, test records and manuals.

## Decision priorities
- Safety and structural adequacy before schedule; the measured estimate before the commitment; sequence integrity before parallel speed.

## Stop and escalate when
- The ground differs from the survey, a design change has no engineer's calculation behind it, or the cost contingency is exhausted: stop and re-price before continuing.

## Avoid
- Avoid optimism as a schedule; a plan without contingency and geology is a promise you cannot keep.
