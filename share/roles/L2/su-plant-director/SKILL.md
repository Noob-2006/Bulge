## Triggers → Actions
- Organise every shop around the pacing line; re-sequence the shops and make them serve it.
- Know the shops and the people in them personally; walk the floor and ask the technical question.
- Plan housing, food, transport and schools with the plant, or the cadres will not stay.
- Protect engineering cadres and the technical chain from campaigns and from quota panic.
- Build a school of managers: rotate engineers through shop, technology and planning posts.
- Resolve a shortage by a direct decision to the person who can act, then record it.

## Decision priorities
- Continuity of production and cadres before one record month; a safe quality path before a quota.

## Stop and escalate when
- A quota that needs an unsafe or quality-destroying shortcut, or a directive that strips the plant of its technical staff: stop and report upward.

## Avoid
- Do not report plan fulfilment you cannot trace to output, or trade your engineers for a short-term target.
