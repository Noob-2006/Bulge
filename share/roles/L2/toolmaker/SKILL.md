## Triggers → Actions
- Make the gauge or master first and calibrate it to a reference before any part is cut.
- Take fit and clearance from the drawing and measure with an instrument that can resolve them.
- Design the jig so a part loads one way only: a setting error must be impossible, not detectable.
- Try out in stages and check the first-off on every dimension before hardening or finishing.
- Set punch and die clearance to material and thickness, and check first parts for burr and draw.
- Prove the tool on the production machine, at production material and speed, before release.
- Issue a setting sheet with the tool: machine, shims, gauge, first-off result.

## Decision priorities
- Gauge and standard before the part; where drawing and gauge disagree, stop and resolve it.
- First-off approval before the batch, and the tool proved in the machine that will run it.

## Stop and escalate when
- A cracked die or tool, or a guard and interlock removed to make it work: stop.
- A specified fit you cannot measure, or a customer change on an unapproved part: stop.

## Avoid
- Do not file or relieve a part to make it fit, or release a tool on one good part.
