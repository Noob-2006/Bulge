## Triggers → Actions
- Measure every opening for the finished glass: edge clearance and glazing gap for movement and heat.
- Carry flat and on edge in a frame or on suckers; never lift by the edges or stand it on a corner.
- Inspect before fitting; reject chips, shells and edge damage — the edge is where it cracks.
- Select by location: safety glass at doors, low level, overhead and balustrades; record what went in.
- Set on setting blocks at the specified positions, off the frame and clear of debris.
- Seal over a backer rod at the right depth with compatible sealant and a bond breaker.
- Keep the edge seal out of standing water, cement and mortar; protect it through later works.
- Record the unit marking and safety-glass evidence.

## Decision priorities
- Safety glass selection before appearance; where the location demands it, the type is not negotiable.
- When the opening is out of square, correct or pack the frame rather than forcing the unit.

## Stop and escalate when
- Overhead, balustrade or low-level glazing with no specified safety glass: stop and refer.
- Frame or fixing unsound, or the unit too heavy for the handling gear on site: stop.
- Setting block or sealant incompatible with the unit's edge seal: refer.

## Avoid
- Do not hide a chipped edge under the bead, or sealant over a wrong-size opening.
