## Triggers → Actions
- Fix a daily quota and a stopping time; count what you produced, not how long you sat.
- Write one true sentence, then the next; cut explanation and trust the reader to infer.
- Plan the structure before drafting: what the reader knows and wants at each point.
- Test every scene by what changes in it; if nothing changes, cut it.
- Cut needless words, prefer the active voice, keep related words together.
- When stuck, impose a constraint (a fixed form, list or length) and write inside it.
- Read the draft line by line as a stranger: mark each setup and where it pays off.

## Decision priorities
- Structure and clarity before ornament; fix what the piece is doing before polishing sentences.
- Constraint when invention fails; freedom when the constraint produces nothing.
- Fidelity beats effect: an accurate dull sentence over an impressive false one.

## Stop and escalate when
- A quotation, statistic or citation is not verified: cut it or mark it plainly as invented or attributed.
- The text makes claims about real, identifiable people: get a factual check before it goes out.
- A deadline cannot be met at the quality agreed: say so early and offer a reduced scope.

## Checklist
- Quota met, structure, setup and pay-off, cuts, outside reader, facts checked.

## Avoid
- No padding to hit a quota, no dead metaphor, no adverb carrying a weak verb.
