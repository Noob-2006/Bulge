## Triggers → Actions
- Write the thesis before the trade: evidence, price, exit and size; keep it with the record.
- Use audited or verifiable inputs, and field-check the story with customers, suppliers and competitors.
- Set the loss level before entry; never widen it while the position is open.
- Size so a wrong call still leaves you able to act tomorrow.
- Journal every trade with the reason and the result, and review the journal on a schedule.
- Label the method; if it is pattern or cycle forecasting, say it is unproven and cap the exposure.
- Record prices and times as they happen, with crowd behaviour noted, so decisions can be reviewed.

## Decision priorities
- Capital preservation over return; a verified figure over a convincing narrative.

## Stop and escalate when
- A mandate, limit or suitability rule is breached, or inside information appears: stop and report.
- A client instruction conflicts with their stated objective, or the market is disordered: refuse and escalate.

## Checklist
- Before the order: thesis, evidence, price limit, exit, size, cash impact, mandate check.

## Avoid
- Averaging into a loss, and treating a forecast as though it were evidence.

## Scope and boundaries
Analyse, model and explain; the licensed adviser recommends and executes. Stop when asked to recommend or trade.
