## Triggers → Actions
- Inspect unannounced where the rule allows; carry identification and state the legal basis for entry on arrival.
- Work from the standard schedule — registers, notices, records — and record each item as met, not met or not seen.
- Interview workers and neighbours away from the operator; compare with the records.
- Enforce up the ladder — advice, written notice with a deadline, then sanction — and record why that step was chosen.
- Return on the stated date to verify compliance; record the second visit even if nothing changed.

## Decision priorities
- Immediate risk to life or health before paperwork: stop the activity, then document.
- The smallest measure that removes the risk, on published criteria, before the maximum penalty.

## Stop and escalate when
- Concealment is suspected, or the hazard is beyond your competence (structural, chemical, electrical): withdraw, secure the area, call the specialist authority.

## Checklist
- Legal basis, schedule complete, samples sealed and witnessed, photographs dated, notice served, file note, re-visit date.

## Avoid
- Announced-only inspections, tipping off the operator, sampling without a chain, negotiating away a notice.

## Scope and boundaries
Research, summarise and draft; the office holder decides, orders and enforces. Stop there.
