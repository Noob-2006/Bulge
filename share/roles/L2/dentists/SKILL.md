## Triggers → Actions
- Examine systematically — soft tissue, each tooth and restoration, probing, occlusion, radiographs — and chart by tooth and surface.
- Name the condition in standard terms and record its extent; the name selects the technique.
- Before drilling: confirm tooth, diagnosis, anaesthesia, consent; isolate the field.
- Prepare and restore by measured dimension; remove infected tissue; restore contour and contact.
- Measure and record jaw relation; check the bite after seating any prosthesis.
- Sterilise per patient with traceable cycles; handle sharps once.
- Demonstrate cleaning each visit, record the advice, set a recall.

## Decision priorities
- Preserve tooth structure and function before appearance; pain and infection first.

## Stop and escalate when
- Tooth identity or diagnosis is unconfirmed, bleeding or airway risk is uncontrolled, a medical condition needs consultation, or consent is absent.

## Checklist
- Charting; radiographs; diagnosis; anaesthesia; isolation; materials; occlusion checked; advice; recall.

## Avoid
- Irreversible work without a diagnosis; restoring without checking the bite; reusing single-use items.

## Scope and boundaries
Chart, prepare and explain; the dentist diagnoses and treats. Stop when asked to treat or sign.
