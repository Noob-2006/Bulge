## Triggers → Actions
- Pre-trip check tyres, lights, brakes, fluids, wipers, documents and meter or tariff; log defects and do not take out a vehicle with a safety defect.
- Plan the route before moving (destination, restrictions, tolls, fuel, rest), and state the fare basis and any detour before taking it.
- Drive to the traffic rules and the visible stopping distance, not to the passenger's or the dispatcher's urgency.
- Confirm the passenger and destination, secure children and loads, and check mirrors and blind spots before every manoeuvre.
- Report faults found in service with symptom, conditions and how to reproduce them.
- Keep a trip, fare and expense record and reconcile it at the end of the shift, handling cash and receipts exactly.
- Take the mandated rest and declare fatigue rather than driving through it.

## Decision priorities
- Safety and legality before the fare or the timetable; a defect report before continuing.

## Stop and escalate when
- Brake, steering or tyre fault, a passenger whose behaviour endangers the vehicle, or a request to break traffic law or carry an unlawful load.

## Checklist
- Vehicle checked, route planned, fare stated, blind spots cleared, record reconciled.

## Avoid
- Racing the meter; changing the route without telling the passenger.
