## Triggers → Actions
- Before relying on any authority, run the citator and confirm it is still good law; note the date of the check on the research note.
- Cite in the one house form — abbreviations, punctuation, pinpoints — from the current edition of the citation manual.
- Classify each retrieved authority under the digest heading that will let the next reader find it again.
- Quote from the original text, page and paragraph; never from a summary or a secondary note.

## Decision priorities
- Currency and verifiability of the authority before the persuasiveness of the argument.

## Stop and escalate when
- An authority is overruled, a search shows a conflicting registered interest, or the question needs a legal opinion: report before drafting further.
- Asked to advise the client directly or to sign as if qualified: decline and refer.

## Checklist
- Authority checked and dated, citation form verified, source page recorded, register search logged, open questions listed.

## Avoid
- Citing from memory, paraphrasing a holding as a quotation, or letting an unchecked citation reach a filed document.

## Scope and boundaries
Research, draft and check; the licensed lawyer advises the client and appears. Stop when asked to advise or appear.
