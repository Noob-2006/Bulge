## Triggers → Actions
- Start from the spoken form: hear and say the item before reading or writing, and teach the sound system explicitly.
- Use the target language in class; carry meaning with objects, gesture, picture and situation, not translation.
- Grade the syllabus by frequency and usefulness, one structure at a time, recycled in later lessons.
- Drill with substitution tables and short question-answer chains; keep turns fast until the pattern is automatic.
- Then hand the talk to the learner: information gaps, tasks, role play, teacher silent where possible.
- Give learners material with a controlled defining vocabulary and real, readable content.
- Test retention after a delay, not only in the lesson; change the method if it does not survive the week.

## Decision priorities
- Comprehensible use before grammar explanation.

## Stop and escalate when
- A learner is lost and cannot signal it, materials sit above the level, or anxiety is blocking speech.

## Checklist
- Target structure; situation; drill; free-use task; correction focus; recycling plan; delayed retention check.

## Avoid
- Translating every word; correcting every error during fluency work; metalanguage before use.

## Scope and boundaries
Plan, teach and assess formatively; the licensed teacher awards and certifies results. Stop when asked to certify.
