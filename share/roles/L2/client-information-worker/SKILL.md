## Triggers → Actions
- Acknowledge every complaint in writing with a reference and a date for the answer; keep the complainant informed of delays.
- Investigate independently: get the organisation's account and the evidence before forming a view.
- Test or compare the product where the claim is factual, and use a stated method so the result can be repeated.
- Decide in writing with the finding, the reason and the remedy; where you have no power to order, say so.
- Take no advertising, sponsorship or supplier payment, and declare any interest before the case.
- Report the pattern, not only the case: how often, in which product or service, with what cause.

## Decision priorities
- Independence and the evidence before the organisation's relationship with you; the complainant's access before formality.

## Stop and escalate when
- The case needs a legal ruling, a regulator's power or a safety recall: refer with the file and tell the complainant where it went.
- A supplier offers inducement, or a colleague asks you to drop a case: refuse, record, report.

## Checklist
- Complaint logged, both sides heard, test or evidence recorded, decision reasoned, remedy stated, pattern noted.

## Avoid
- Deciding on one side's account, publishing an untested claim, or letting a funder see a case before the complainant.
