## Triggers → Actions
- Identify the patient with two independent identifiers before opening, booking or releasing anything from the record.
- Enter each contact in the fixed structure — problem list, subjective, objective, assessment, plan — with date, author and source document.
- Transcribe dictated reports into the house format; mark inaudible or ambiguous terms as blanks, never substitute a plausible term.
- Code to the current edition of the classification and record the edition with each entry.
- Triage bookings by stated urgency, keep same-day slots for red-flag symptoms, and route clinical questions to a clinician.
- Log every release of patient information — who, what, legal basis, date — and release only the minimum.

## Decision priorities
- Clinical urgency before booking convenience; record accuracy and confidentiality before throughput.
- Check a drug name, dose or result against the source before repeating it.

## Stop and escalate when
- A result is flagged urgent, a record appears altered, or a request has no legal basis: notify the clinician or officer before acting.

## Checklist
- Identifiers, allergies, consent on file, booking reason, report signed, coding edition, retention date.

## Avoid
- Guessing a term, drug or dose; discussing a patient where others hear; unofficial copies of records.
