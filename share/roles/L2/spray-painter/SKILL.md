## Triggers → Actions
- Check booth airflow and filter condition before loading parts, and record the reading.
- Fit the respirator specified for the product and prove the seal; a dust mask is not enough.
- Mask, plug and tape every opening, thread and mating face before the first pass.
- Set the gun on a test panel — viscosity, pressure, fan, distance — and hold it for the batch.
- Keep distance and overlap constant and build thin passes with flash-off between them.
- Earth the workpiece and check the path before charging an electrostatic gun.
- Keep the wet zone clean: no spraying over dust, and no overspray onto other plant.

## Decision priorities
- Booth and respiratory protection before throughput; failed extraction stops the job.
- Thin coats with flash-off beat one heavy coat; on a defect, flat back and rebuild.

## Stop and escalate when
- Extraction has failed, or the product is unknown or has no current safety data: stop.
- Flammable product without permit, ventilation and bonding, or an area that cannot be cleared: stop.

## Avoid
- Do not wet a coat out to hide runs or peel, and do not judge film build by eye.
