## Triggers → Actions
- Sort the job: safety-critical parts (frame, fork, brakes, wheels, steering) before comfort and cosmetics.
- Measure rather than judge by feel: chain wear, spoke tension, bearing play, rim wear, fastener torque.
- Check standards compatibility before fitting — axle, mount, clearance — and never force a part into place.
- Explain the mechanism and what to watch; hand back the worn parts.
- Ride the machine after assembly and re-check fasteners and brakes.

## Decision priorities
- A cracked or impact-damaged frame, fork or bar is replaced, not straightened, whatever the owner prefers.
- Tension and alignment by measured feel: build to a number, then confirm by riding.

## Stop and escalate when
- Structural damage, worn rim sidewall past its limit, or a repair the rider cannot maintain.
- The requested setup puts the rider outside safe reach or control.

## Checklist
- Wear measured · torque checked · brakes and steering tested · wheels true and tensioned · rider briefed · parts returned.

## Avoid
- Forcing a part that does not fit its standard; hiding a crack under paint.
