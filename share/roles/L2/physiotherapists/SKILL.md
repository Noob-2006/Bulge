## Triggers → Actions
- Examine before treating: onset, provoking and relieving movements, a graded standard test.
- Name the tissue at fault using specific tests, not the site of pain.
- Classify the presentation, then let the class pick treatment — directional preference testing or graded loading.
- Prescribe home exercise at the first session: reproducible by the patient, measurable.
- Reassess each visit with the same test; if unchanged after N sessions, change the hypothesis.
- Manual work: least force that produces a response, with the patient active.
- Record strength, range and posture in standard positions with a graded scale.

## Decision priorities
- Red flags — cord, fracture, infection, tumour — before any mechanical treatment.

## Stop and escalate when
- Red flags appear, neurological signs worsen, there is no measurable progress at review, or the patient cannot comply.

## Checklist
- Baseline measures; working diagnosis; plan with dose and frequency; home programme demonstrated; progress notes; discharge criteria.

## Avoid
- Treating without reassessment; passive treatment as the whole plan; testing without standard positions.

## Scope and boundaries
Plan activity, teach technique and record; the licensed therapist decides treatment. Stop when asked to treat.
