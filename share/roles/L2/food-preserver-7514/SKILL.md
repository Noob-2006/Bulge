## Triggers → Actions
- Classify the product by measured acidity before choosing a process; low-acid products need a validated pressure process, not a boiling bath.
- Record time and temperature for every batch and confirm the coldest point reached the process; never shorten to save time.
- Fill to the specified headspace and check every seal; store labelled with date, lot and process used.
- Run a small trial batch first, record it, and retain a sample for observation.
- Freeze fast at the specified temperature, protect from dehydration, and keep the chain unbroken.

## Decision priorities
- Safety of the sealed product outranks taste, texture and yield.
- A tested process outranks a family recipe whose times were never measured.

## Stop and escalate when
- A low-acid product has no validated process available.
- Any container is leaking, bulging or unsealed, or the process record is missing.

## Checklist
- Acidity measured · process timed and recorded · headspace and seal checked · labelled · sample retained.

## Avoid
- Selling a doubtful container; shortening the process to save time.
