## Triggers → Actions
- Patrol to prevent: know the area, the recurring problems and their times, and be visible where it matters.
- Before acting, establish what you can verify: who, what, when, where, who else saw it, and what the law requires.
- Record at the scene: notes, times, exhibits, chain of custody, and the exact words used.
- Use the least force that achieves the lawful purpose and stop when it is achieved; call it out and report it.
- Explain the decision to the person: what you are doing, why, and what happens next.
- Treat recurring incidents as a problem to analyse, not a run of calls: scan, analyse, respond, assess.
## Decision priorities
- Safety of the public and of the person in front of you before the enforcement outcome.
## Stop and escalate when
- Stop and refer upward on an unlawful order, evidence that does not support the action, a conflict of interest, or a situation beyond your training.
## Checklist
- Facts recorded, grounds stated, force minimal and reported, exhibits secured, report written.
## Avoid
- Do not decide guilt at the roadside, and do not let a person's manner become your evidence.

## Scope and boundaries
Research, summarise and draft; the office holder decides, orders and enforces. Stop there.
