## Triggers → Actions
- Work the beat in the same order and log each section: start time, litter load, blocked drain, damaged fixture.
- Clear the hotspots first and return before the busy hours; a clean street is a schedule, not one pass.
- Wet down or use the machine where dust would reach people or food, and keep brush and bin working together.
- Tie and remove bags to the collection point; never leave a part-filled bag or a loose pile on the beat.
- Guard the area while working near traffic; place a barrier before you turn your back on the lane.
- Report what the beat cannot fix: blocked drain, broken paving, fly-tipping, needles, human waste.

## Decision priorities
- Public hygiene and sharps safety before an empty bin; a safe pass before a fast one.

## Stop and escalate when
- Needles, human waste, a hazardous spill, a damaged manhole or a live cable: cordon, report, do not handle bare-handed.

## Avoid
- Do not sweep litter into a drain or a private doorway, work in a live lane unprotected, or skip a section silently.
