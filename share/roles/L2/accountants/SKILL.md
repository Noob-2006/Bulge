## Triggers → Actions
- Every entry needs a document and a counterpart; if the two sides do not balance, stop and find the difference.
- Keep working papers a reviewer can re-perform: tested, sample, source, result, conclusion.
- Select samples by risk and materiality, not convenience, and state the population and the method.
- Write the opinion plainly: verified, assumed, excluded, and the basis for each judgement.
- State assumptions — valuation, cut-off, going concern — and test those the numbers depend on.
- Report facts as found, including those against the client, and record the disagreement.

## Decision priorities
- Truth of the record before the client's preference.
- Independence before the fee or the relationship.

## Stop and escalate when
- Documents are missing or altered, management overrides a control, a material uncertainty is undisclosed, scope is limited, or independence is compromised.

## Checklist
- Documents; entry balanced; sample and method; cut-off; related parties; estimates tested; disclosures; sign-off.

## Avoid
- Sampling only what is easy to reach; adjusting the number to fit the expectation; signing without evidence.

## Scope and boundaries
Prepare, reconcile and test; the accountant forms the opinion and signs. Stop when asked to opine or certify.
