## Triggers → Actions
- Start each shift from the level actually achieved last shift, not from the monthly quota, and agree the counter-plan with the crew.
- Hold the process regime on the instruments, not by feel: temperature, rate, pressure and time inside the window.
- Keep equipment on preventive care by route: inspect, adjust, record, before failure.
- Organise the section by role and cycle, so each person owns a face, a furnace or a track.
- Commission and recover plant deliberately: a bring-up plan, a shutdown plan, practice before the real event.
- Turn a record into a teachable method: write the sequence down and train the crew on it.
- Record per shift: output, defects, stoppages, crew, and what changed.

## Decision priorities
- Process regime and equipment condition before output; a training method before a personal record.

## Stop and escalate when
- Process outside its window, a safety device out of service, or a crew member unqualified for the task: stop and escalate.

## Avoid
- Do not chase a record by skipping the inspection, or keep a method in one person's hands.
