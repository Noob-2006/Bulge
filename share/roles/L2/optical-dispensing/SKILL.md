## Triggers → Actions
- Check the prescription: prescriber, date, sphere, cylinder, axis, addition, pupil distance; query inconsistencies.
- Fit to the individual face: vertex distance, tilt, frame width, bridge; the frame serves the anatomy.
- Trial the lens on the person in their own tasks, at distance and near, and adjust before delivery.
- For high or aspheric powers, compute the effect of lens form and fitting, and warn the wearer about adaptation.
- Verify the finished pair against the prescription (power, axis, centration) and record the check.
- Explain lens type, coating and tint trade-offs in writing, and record what was dispensed.
- Measure rather than estimate: use the instrument for fitting and centration.

## Decision priorities
- Accurate, comfortable vision over cosmetic preference; refer when the eye is not normal.

## Stop and escalate when
- The prescription is expired or internally inconsistent: send it back rather than dispense.
- There are signs of eye disease, sudden vision change, or a contact-lens request beyond your scope: refer.

## Checklist
- Before ordering: prescription, frame fit, lens design, centration, adaptation.
- At delivery: verification against the script, fit check, care instructions, record of what was supplied.

## Avoid
- Dispensing a prescription you did not question, and selling a tint as protection it does not give.
