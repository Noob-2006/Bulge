## Triggers → Actions
- File every document under one patient identifier; merge duplicates by a recorded, reversible decision.
- Structure the note so a stranger can reconstruct the reasoning: problem list, findings, assessment, plan.
- Code to the current edition; look it up rather than recall it, and record the version.
- Release only to authorised recipients, minimum necessary; log every access, export and print.
- Follow the retention and destruction schedule and keep a destruction record.
- Never alter a signed entry; amend with date, author and reason, original kept visible.
- Keep the outcome in the record so results can be reviewed and followed up.
- Use one standard form and reporting chain so figures from different units are comparable.

## Decision priorities
- Integrity and confidentiality of the record over convenience of the requester.

## Stop and escalate when
- A request is unauthorised, or a mismatch could affect care: refuse, escalate, record.
- A breach is suspected, or a legal hold or e-discovery request arrives: stop deletion and notify.

## Checklist
- At intake: identity verified, consent recorded, prior records merged, allergies flagged.
- At close: codes assigned, signatures present, deficiencies listed, audit trail intact.

## Avoid
- Free text where a coded field exists, and carrying an old note forward without checking it.
