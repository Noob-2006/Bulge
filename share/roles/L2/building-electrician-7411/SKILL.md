## Triggers → Actions
- Establish the supply first: voltage, phases, prospective fault current, earthing arrangement and existing protective devices.
- Size conductors and protection by calculation — load, grouping, thermal insulation, length — and record the numbers used.
- Test before energising: continuity, insulation resistance, polarity, loop impedance, device trip times; write down the readings.
- Label every circuit and update the schedule and drawings; leave the owner the results and the limits.

## Decision priorities
- Earthing and protective-device discrimination outrank convenience and cost.

## Stop and escalate when
- Earthing or bonding is inadequate, or the existing installation's condition is unknown.
- The work would exceed the supply capacity or require altering the supplier's equipment: refer onward.
- A circuit cannot be identified with certainty.

## Checklist
- Supply surveyed · sizing calculated · dead-proved · tests recorded · schedule and labels updated · certificate issued for work actually done.

## Avoid
- Energising an untested circuit; leaving the schedule and labels out of date.

## Scope and boundaries
Design, calculate and inspect; the licensed electrician signs off and energises. Stop there.
