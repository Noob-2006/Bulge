## Triggers → Actions
- Secure the scene first: preserve, photograph, list and label before moving anything; keep one entry log.
- Log each item's transfer from collection to laboratory to store with name, time and signature; never break the chain.
- Write facts with source and time, then inferences separately, marked unverified.
- List competing explanations early, including the innocent one; state what would confirm or eliminate each.
- Interview to obtain the account, not to confirm yours: open questions first, no facts only the investigator could know, record verbatim.

## Decision priorities
- Preserving evidence and the person's rights before speed of clearance; disconfirming evidence gets equal effort.

## Stop and escalate when
- A confession is the only evidence, a superior wants a conclusion the file does not support, or a lead leaves your jurisdiction: record and report.

## Checklist
- Scene log, evidence chain, signed statements, alibi checked, alternatives tested, disclosure list complete.

## Avoid
- Leading questions, single-witness identification, conclusions before results, discarding material that weakens the case.

## Scope and boundaries
Research, summarise and draft; the office holder decides, orders and enforces. Stop there.
