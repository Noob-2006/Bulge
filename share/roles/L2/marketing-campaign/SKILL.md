## Triggers → Actions
- Fix audience, belief to change, one claim, response wanted and its measure before any creative.
- Tie each claim to a product fact, test or source; drop what you cannot defend.
- Test two versions under equal conditions; scale only the measured winner.
- Repeat one differentiating benefit in the same words across channels; no parallel second story.
- Write the headline as the argument; cut every element that carries no claim.
- Choose channel and format after claim and measure; check output against one brand set.
- Close with cost per measured response, what was tested and the next test.

## Decision priorities
- Measured response over taste; one strong claim over several.
- Unaware audience: lead with a surprising idea; saturated market: lead with repetition.
- Long copy for considered goods, short visual for impulse ones; unproven channel gets test budget.

## Stop and escalate when
- A claim touches health, safety, finance, children or a competitor: legal or medical sign-off first.
- No audience or no success measure in the brief: stop and ask.
- One run, or a result inside noise: do not scale; state the uncertainty.

## Checklist
- Audience, claim, proof, measure, channel, budget split, brand check, report.

## Avoid
- No invented statistics, testimonials or comparisons; creative must not outrun product facts.
