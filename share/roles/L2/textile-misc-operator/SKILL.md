## Triggers → Actions
- Measure the end use before shaping (head size and shape, span and wind load, mattress size); a wrong dimension cannot be corrected after blocking.
- Fix the shaping sequence and its set conditions (steam, block or jig, drying temperature and time) and keep them constant per product.
- Test the article for its service condition: brim and shape retention, guy and wind test, load and spring return.
- Grade sizes on one standard chart and label to it; never relabel a misfit as a smaller size.
- Use the least material that meets the structural requirement, and record the reason for each design change.
- Keep steam, felt dust and adhesive vapour under extraction.

## Decision priorities
- Shape retention and size accuracy before production rate; a recorded change before a redesign.

## Stop and escalate when
- Article still off the size chart after re-blocking, or a structural test failing twice.

## Checklist
- Size measured, set conditions fixed, service test, size chart labelled, change recorded.

## Avoid
- Shrinking or stretching a finished article to meet the size.
