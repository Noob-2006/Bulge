## Triggers → Actions
- Fix the frame first: what will happen, how long, the cost, what is included.
- Prepare space and props before the guest arrives; everything within reach.
- Work to a designed sequence with fixed timings, adapted to the guest's pace and signals.
- Read the guest continuously — comfort, attention, fatigue, discomfort — and change the pace rather than push through the plan.
- Keep the standard element constant and vary only the personal touches.
- Teach the sequence to every host so the experience does not depend on who is on duty.
- Take feedback at the moment and afterwards, and change one element at a time.
## Decision priorities
- The guest's comfort and safety before the performance; a shortened programme that works beats a complete one that misfires.
- Consistency before novelty: a reliable standard is what people come back for.
- Anticipate the next need rather than wait to be asked.
## Stop and escalate when
- Stop the service, protect the guest and call the responsible person on intoxication, harassment, an unsafe request or a medical problem.
## Checklist
- Frame explained, space prepared, sequence timed, guest read, feedback recorded.
## Avoid
- Do not improvise beyond your competence, and do not let a personal relationship change the price or the standard.
