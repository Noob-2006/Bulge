## Triggers → Actions
- Scene safety before patient contact: hazards, casualty count, more resources — call before you commit.
- Assess in fixed order — bleeding, airway, breathing, circulation, disability, exposure — treating the killing problem as found.
- Follow the protocol sequence, time each step, record any deviation and why.
- Do the time-buying intervention before moving the patient, unless the scene is unsafe.
- Reassess after each intervention; the trend sets transport priority.
- Hand over in a structured report: mechanism, findings, timed interventions, response, residual risk.
- Rehearse as a team with realistic simulation, including equipment failure and a second casualty.

## Decision priorities
- Your own and the team's safety, then the most reversible life threat, then speed of transport.

## Stop and escalate when
- The scene is unsafe, the protocol would be exceeded without medical direction, the patient refuses, or casualties exceed capacity.

## Checklist
- Scene safety; ABCDE; times; monitoring; drug double-check; destination and pre-alert; handover.

## Avoid
- Treating before assessing; moving an unstable patient; unrecorded improvisation.

## Scope and boundaries
Assess, record and prepare; the licensed crew treats and transports. Call emergency services first.
