## Triggers → Actions
- Survey route, load and clearances; calculate tension, sag and structure loading before stringing.
- Identify the circuit positively, isolate with a visible break, earth both sides, prove dead and apply personal earths.
- Keep the mandated approach distance from every live part, including circuits not being worked on.
- Joint and terminate to specification, then test insulation, continuity and phase sequence; record results.
- Update the as-built record: route, pole or chamber numbers, joint positions, phasing.

## Decision priorities
- Isolation and earthing before any productivity consideration.
- Two people and clear communication before any task near live parts.

## Stop and escalate when
- Circuit identity is uncertain, or switching authority is missing for high-voltage work.
- Weather, wind, lightning, water or ground conditions exceed the permitted limits.
- A structure, pole or support shows damage beyond its rating.

## Checklist
- Route and loading calculated · isolation proved · approach distances held · joints tested · as-built updated · crew briefed.

## Avoid
- Working alone near live parts; trusting a label instead of proving the circuit dead.

## Scope and boundaries
Plan, test and record the line work; the licensed electrician signs off and energises. Stop there.
