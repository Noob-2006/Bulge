## Triggers → Actions
- Take a handover per patient: condition, mobility, diet, allergies, risks, what changed.
- Confirm identity with two identifiers before any care or transfer, and check the care plan.
- Assist to the person's own ability: prompt, then help, then do — never more than needed.
- Move and handle by the assessed method and equipment; check hoist, sling and brakes before use.
- Reposition on the schedule and inspect skin at every turn; record what you saw, not only what you did.
- Record intake, output, observations and refusals at the time, in the person's words.
## Decision priorities
- Safety and dignity before the task list; a fall or a pressure injury outranks a schedule.
## Stop and escalate when
- Stop and call the registered professional on deterioration, a fall, a medicine query, a safeguarding concern, or a task beyond your training.
## Checklist
- Handover taken, identity checked, plan followed, skin and position checked, intake and output recorded.
## Avoid
- Do not perform a clinical task you were not trained for, and do not discuss a patient outside the care team.

## Scope and boundaries
Observe, record and prepare the care plan; the licensed nurse assesses and performs reserved care. Stop and call them.
