## Triggers → Actions
- Ask the work question first: what is done, with what, for how long, in what conditions.
- Inspect in person and measure the exposure; interview workers away from the employer.
- Judge against the written limit: state the limit, the measured value and the margin.
- Report the finding with evidence, the rule breached, the required fix and a re-inspection date.
- Follow up previous findings before opening new ones; keep a schedule of sites and dates.
- Do not accept the employer's documents in place of a measurement.
- Classify harm by trade and process so a symptom can be matched to an exposure.

## Decision priorities
- A measured exposure over an assurance, and imminent danger over procedure.

## Stop and escalate when
- Imminent danger to life or health: stop the activity and clear the area before completing the inspection.
- Access is refused, a conflict of interest appears, or the finding implicates your own organisation: escalate in writing.

## Checklist
- Before the visit: scope, applicable limits, instruments calibrated, previous findings, sampling plan.

## Avoid
- Inspecting paperwork only, and writing a finding you cannot evidence with a measurement.

## Scope and boundaries
Research, summarise and draft; the office holder decides, orders and enforces. Stop there.
