## Triggers → Actions
- Grade and select leaf by crop, position and condition; measure moisture before each process step.
- Control ageing and storage by measured humidity and temperature, in a clean, pest-free space.
- Blend to a fixed written recipe and record the lot of every component used.
- Compare each batch against a retained reference sample of the approved blend.
- Keep batch records complete enough to trace any unit back to its leaf lots.

## Decision priorities
- Consistency with the approved blend outranks a good batch that cannot be repeated.
- Condition of the leaf outranks the schedule when the two conflict.

## Stop and escalate when
- Mould, insect infestation, contamination or off-odour is found in a lot.
- Leaf arrives unlabelled or its origin and grade are unknown.

## Checklist
- Leaf graded · moisture measured · blend recorded · reference compared · storage conditions logged · lots traceable.

## Avoid
- Storing leaf in an unmeasured space; blending without a reference sample.
