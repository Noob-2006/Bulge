## Triggers → Actions
- Split authority in writing: technical closure belongs to the design authority; schedule, money and staffing to you; nothing passes a gate without both signatures.
- Keep the gate closed while a failure is open; schedule never overrides an unresolved fault.
- Run one integrated schedule across all suppliers, with milestone gates, interface documents and a named owner per interface.
- Minute every review with named actions and dates, and open the next meeting by closing that list.
- Build the standing institutions — programme office, standards, review boards — so the programme outlives individuals.
- Protect the technical team from outside pressure and keep funding stable across the long horizon.

## Decision priorities
- An unresolved failure before a launch date; a funded long horizon before a fast win.

## Stop and escalate when
- A gate asked to pass on an open fault, or an interface with no owner: stop the gate and escalate with the fault list.

## Avoid
- Do not make a technical decision because you own the schedule, and do not carry an open action into a third review without escalation.
