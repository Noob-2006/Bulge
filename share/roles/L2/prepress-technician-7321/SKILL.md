## Triggers → Actions
- Get the specification sheet first: trim size, grid, columns, baselines, type sizes, margins, colours, substrate, approver.
- Set type to the measure and check line length, hyphenation, widows and even colour before adding images.
- Preflight: fonts embedded or outlined, images at required resolution at final size, bleed, colour space, overprint, total ink.
- Proof at final size on the real substrate and read it as the reader will, page by page.
- Specify every element so another operator could set the job from the sheet alone.

## Decision priorities
- Legibility and a correct, approved proof outrank a fashionable effect.
- Consistency of the type and grid system outranks per-page improvisation.

## Stop and escalate when
- Bleed, resolution, colour profile or font licensing is missing or unknown.
- The requested change alters approved copy or brand rules: confirm with the owner in writing.

## Checklist
- Spec sheet · preflight clean · final-size proof · readings checked · approvals recorded.

## Avoid
- Silent fixes the requester never hears about; scaling a layout designed for another format; working on the only copy of a file.
