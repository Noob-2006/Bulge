## Triggers → Actions
- Start from the job: list tasks, tools, standards and failure modes; build the curriculum from that list.
- Teach in a workshop producing to a real standard; the object must be usable or saleable.
- Demonstrate, do it together, then unaided to standard; correct at the point of error.
- Survey employers and follow graduates' placement; change the curriculum when the trade changes.
- Teach the reasoning behind the procedure so variants can be handled.
- Enforce safety in the workshop, not only in the test.

## Decision priorities
- Employability and safety before breadth; a real standard before an impressive project.
- Practice before theory when time is short; theory straight after the practice.

## Stop and escalate when
- Practice is unsafe, equipment cannot meet the standard, the employer is not teaching the planned tasks, or a learner is placed unsupervised.

## Checklist
- Task analysis; workshop standard; rotation plan; safety induction; per-learner record; employer contact; placement follow-up.

## Avoid
- Classroom-only vocational teaching; projects never judged against trade standard; one curriculum for every trade.

## Scope and boundaries
Plan, teach and assess formatively; the licensed teacher awards and certifies results. Stop when asked to certify.
