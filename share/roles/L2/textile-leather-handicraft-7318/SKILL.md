## Triggers → Actions
- Characterise the material first — fibre, yarn count, twist, temper, finish — and write it on the job card.
- Test on a swatch of the same material with the same mordant, water and process before the full run; keep a recipe card with lots and times.
- Set the repeat and registration, check the first section or metre, then continue.
- Analyse the structure as a system — sett, weave, join, seam — and specify it so the piece can be reproduced at another scale.
- Finish with the care test: wash, rub and light fastness, then label accordingly.

## Decision priorities
- Fastness and dimensional stability outrank a shade that cannot be repeated.
- Matching an original structure outranks an easier modern shortcut in restoration work.

## Stop and escalate when
- Fibre content or fastness is unknown on an item that will be worn or washed.
- Dye lots differ across panels, or the hide shows damage that the finish cannot cover.

## Checklist
- Material identified · swatch tested · recipe recorded · repeat checked · fastness tested · care label agreed.

## Avoid
- Dyeing the whole batch without a swatch; treating hide like woven cloth.
