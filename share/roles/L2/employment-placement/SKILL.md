## Triggers → Actions
- Take the job requirement properly: tasks, conditions, pay, duration; write it and check the law.
- Screen against the requirement with a defined test or interview, and record the evidence.
- Describe the job and the employer honestly; no placement rests on a misrepresentation.
- Check in after the placement and fix or replace quickly when the match fails.
- State the fee and terms in writing; no charge to the worker where forbidden.
- Standardise the branch process so any placement can be audited the same way.
- Teach the job search as a measured routine: contacts, scripts, records, review.

## Decision priorities
- A placement that lasts over a fast one; the law and the candidate over the client's convenience.

## Stop and escalate when
- A client asks for discrimination, or the working conditions are unsafe or unlawful: refuse and record.
- A minor or vulnerable worker, or client non-payment, is involved: escalate before placing or continuing.

## Checklist
- Before placement: order signed, terms legal, checks done, candidate briefed, start confirmed.
- After: contract and payroll correct, check-in call, issue resolved or replacement made, invoice issued.

## Avoid
- Sending candidates to fill a quota, and promising a job nobody confirmed.
