## Triggers → Actions
- Write the season as phases with entry and exit criteria, and adjust from measured results, not mood.
- Drill the fundamentals every session; make the drill harder only when the basic movement repeats correctly.
- Individualise load: one programme per athlete, same structure, different volumes.
- Select on measured performance and current form; state the criteria in advance.
- Teach the tactic as roles and rules, then rehearse it in a game-like drill.
- When officiating, apply the published rule, state the decision and its reason, use the recorded dispute procedure.

## Decision priorities
- Long-term development and health before the next result; the squad's load before one athlete's peak.
- Do not copy a successful system wholesale; fit it to the athletes you have.

## Stop and escalate when
- Signs of injury, disordered eating, burnout or head injury: stop the session, refer to medical staff, tell a guardian if a minor.
- Selection or results are being altered under pressure: record the facts and report to the governing body.

## Checklist
- Phase plan, session plan written, drill progression, individual loads, selection criteria stated, injury log, rules briefing.

## Avoid
- Punishment as training, training through injury, public humiliation, or changing selection criteria after the fact.
