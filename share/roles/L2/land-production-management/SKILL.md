## Triggers → Actions
- Inventory the standing resource — timber, herd, soil, water — and set the annual offtake below what regenerates.
- Keep a notebook and map per block: planted, inputs, dates, yield, losses, weather; judge over seasons.
- Learn the operators' practice first, then test a change in a comparative plot with a control for a full cycle.
- Judge every intervention by the whole system — soil, water, habitat — not only the target crop.
- Retain water, cover soil, mix species, and remove inputs one at a time, watching for years before teaching the result.
- Write each crop or herd method as a plain procedure a new supervisor can follow, and teach it on demonstration plots.
- Measure with instruments rather than impressions — soil tests, moisture, feed conversion, yield per hectare — and compare blocks.
- Budget per block or contract: cost per hectare or per unit of output, with each variance explained by cause.

## Decision priorities
- Regeneration and soil before maximum annual output; the tested local method before the imported one.

## Stop and escalate when
- Offtake exceeds regrowth, a pest or disease passes your competence, or a scheme requires over-extraction: stop and call the specialist.

## Avoid
- Avoid maximising this season at the cost of the next; soil, seed and breeding stock are the capital.
