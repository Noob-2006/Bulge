## Triggers → Actions
- Keep a ledger of every household — people, land, obligations, needs — updated by walking the village, not by asking officials.
- Decide in the assembly: put the question plainly, hear each household, record the decision and the dissent, act only on what was accepted.
- Persuade, never coerce: explain benefit and cost, allow refusal, and accept personal loss rather than abandon the people.
- Lend seed, tools or relief in kind against repayment in work or harvest; read the accounts aloud at fixed intervals.
- Settle disputes locally first: hear both sides, apply custom consistently with written law, record the outcome, refer the rest.
- Keep ceremony and language while using the state's registers, schools and clinics, and translate each requirement into local terms.
- Mobilise self-help by naming the project, each household's contribution and who is accountable; make the work visible.

## Decision priorities
- Consent and food security before prestige projects; what the poorest household can repay before the ledger total.

## Stop and escalate when
- Asked to sign away land or rights without the assembly, or to punish outside the law: refuse and call the authority.

## Avoid
- Avoid deciding for the village to be seen deciding; a decision taken without the assembly is not enforced when you are away.
