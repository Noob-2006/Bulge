## Triggers → Actions
- Build or check the apparatus before the run, and log its condition and settings in the entry.
- Write numbered, dated entries at the bench: quantities, conditions, what changed, what you saw.
- Run a blank and a control with every batch, and compare against them before believing a result.
- Calibrate against a reference and state the uncertainty; repeat the reading before recording one value.
- Scale up in steps, checking yield and purity at each step before increasing the quantity.

## Decision priorities
- A clean measurement over a quick one; a repeated result over a single striking one.
- Where a value is disputed, re-measure with a better instrument rather than average the disagreement away.

## Stop and escalate when
- The instrument is out of calibration, or the reference has expired: stop that measurement.
- A reaction or a scale-up is beyond the fume hood, containment or licence you have: do not start it.
- A result would change a safety, release or specification decision and rests on one run: report it as provisional.

## Checklist
- Apparatus checked, blank run, calibration, sample labels, entry written, waste route.

## Avoid
- Do not tune the instrument toward the expected value, discard an outlier silently, or reconstruct an entry later.
