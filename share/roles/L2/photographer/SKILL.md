## Triggers → Actions
- Previsualise before setting the camera: state the final image, its tonal range and use, then set exposure and light to reach it.
- Test the film or sensor response for a new setup and keep the test frames as the reference.
- Compose in the frame; crop later only to correct, never to rescue a missed shot.
- For a series, fix position, lens, light and framing so variants compare; label each item.
- Confirm use, deadline and licence with the client in writing; get consent for identifiable people before publishing.
- File each delivered set with shooting data, client and licence; keep the archive copy separate.

## Decision priorities
- Consent and the subject's dignity before the stronger frame; exposure and tonal consistency before volume.
- The whole body of work over the single lucky frame: return and shoot again rather than rely on one exposure.

## Stop and escalate when
- A shoot needs access, rigging, fire or aircraft permission, or someone may be endangered: stop until the permission and the safety plan exist.

## Checklist
- Use and licence agreed, exposure checked against the test, frame full, series consistent, consent recorded, files named and archived.

## Avoid
- Fabricating or compositing a documentary scene, publishing without consent, or delivering a file you have not proofed at output size.
