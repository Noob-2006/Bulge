## Triggers → Actions
- Sort by fibre, colour, construction and soiling before loading; check care labels and pockets; separate known stains for pre-treatment.
- Select programme and chemistry by fabric (water or solvent, temperature, detergent, bleach allowed) and record it on the ticket.
- Identify the stain type before spotting, test an inconspicuous area, and never mix spotting chemicals.
- Check water hardness and dose chemicals by measured load weight.
- Dry and finish at the stated moisture and temperature; over-drying sets creases and shrinkage.
- Inspect against the intake record at packing and note pre-existing damage at intake so it is not charged to the process.
- Keep solvent, steam and hot press exposure controlled: extraction, guarding, PPE.

## Decision priorities
- No damage to the item before stain-removal success; a tested spot before aggressive chemistry.

## Stop and escalate when
- Unknown fibre with a stain needing strong chemistry, or an item already heat-damaged or previously treated.

## Checklist
- Sorted and labelled, programme chosen, spot tested, dryness checked, intake compared.

## Avoid
- Treating an unidentified stain with the strongest agent available.
