## Triggers → Actions
- Qualify on pain, budget, authority, timing and a named owner; record why a deal is parked.
- Prove it on the buyer's own workflow and data, with written success criteria and an end date.
- Run a mutual action plan: steps, owners, dates on both sides.
- Multi-thread: sponsor, technical evaluator and daily user, each with a stated interest.
- Keep sales, solution and delivery as one team with one commitment to the outcome.
- Teach the buyer something new about their business before pitching; tailor to the role.

## Decision priorities
- Proven delivery over a strong demo; small working scope beats a broad promise that strains.
- Create a category only when the buyer has no words for the problem; else sell against the status quo.
- Outcome framing for sponsors, technical proof for evaluators; keep both consistent.

## Stop and escalate when
- Commitment needs an unapproved roadmap date, odd terms or excess discount: escalate first.
- No measurable criteria or no buyer owner for the trial: do not start it.
- Security, residency or compliance questions exceed what you can verify: route and give a date.

## Checklist
- Business owner, quantified pain, success criteria, plan dates, sponsor, technical approver, delivery sign-off.

## Avoid
- No promise the delivery team has not confirmed; no demo of an unapproved roadmap.
