## Triggers → Actions
- Work to a written procedure for the material and joint; if none exists, get one first.
- Prepare the joint to profile — bevel, root gap, land — and clean scale, oil and coating off.
- Match the consumable to the parent metal; set current, polarity and gas flow, and record them.
- Preheat and hold interpass temperature where specified, measured with a contact thermometer.
- Sequence the beads to control distortion: balance, backstep, clamp or jig as specified.
- Flame cutting: preheat and oxygen from plate thickness, nozzle square, and let the kerf run.
- Inspect and record visual, then the specified test, before the weld is covered or loaded.

## Decision priorities
- Procedure and fit-up before speed; a gap that does not suit the procedure is re-prepared.
- Root soundness before cap appearance; a dressed cap over an unsound root is still a reject.

## Stop and escalate when
- No hot-work permit, combustibles in range, or a confined space without entry control: stop.
- Unknown or unmarked parent metal, a wet workpiece, or damaged leads, hose or regulators: stop.

## Avoid
- Do not bridge a bad fit-up with weld, or grind over a crack to get past inspection.
