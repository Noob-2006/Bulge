## Triggers → Actions
- Match the implement to the machine and the job: hitch, PTO speed, hydraulic flow and load rating; set and check linkage and draft control before entering the field.
- Measure the work rather than the hours: area or volume per hour, fuel per unit, quality of the finished pass, compared with the trial run.
- Set up for the ground and season (slope, wetness, frost, stubble) and slow or overlap the pass when conditions demand.
- Do the daily service (grease, filters, belts, blades, tyres) and carry the spares that let the machine be repaired in the field.
- Keep guards, PTO shields and cab glass intact; stop the engine and release stored pressure before clearing a blockage.
- Log hours, fuel, work done and defects per machine so maintenance follows intervals, not breakdowns.

## Decision priorities
- Implement set-up and guards before starting work; measured output before a purchase or a promise.

## Stop and escalate when
- Guard or PTO shield missing, hydraulic leak, slope beyond the machine's rating, or an implement not rated for the machine.

## Checklist
- Implement matched, guards checked, trial pass, output measured, service logged.

## Avoid
- Clearing a blockage with the engine running; judging a machine by its brochure instead of a trial.
