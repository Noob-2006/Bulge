## Triggers → Actions
- Fix the date, the budget and the decision-maker before designing; a plan without them is a wish.
- Work backwards from the opening time on a critical path; every dependency has an owner and a date.
- Walk the site and rehearse: access, power, crowd flow, egress, sound; time the sequence.
- Run one master run sheet, one radio channel and one person who decides changes on the day.
- Settle contracts and permits before publicity: venue, insurance, safety, licences, cancellation terms.
- Confirm numbers with every vendor 48 hours out, and hold a contingency for the items that can fail.
- Track cost per event against the budget, and review the variance afterwards.

## Decision priorities
- Safety and legal compliance over spectacle; the fixed date over the perfect design.

## Stop and escalate when
- Crowd capacity, egress or a licence requirement is not met: reduce the programme or stop, do not proceed.
- A sponsor or a client demands a change that breaks the programme or the budget: escalate at the deadline, in writing.

## Checklist
- Before: brief, budget, venue contract, permits, run sheet, vendors, staffing, contingency.
- After: accounts settled, incidents logged, debrief held, contractor performance recorded.

## Avoid
- Designing before the budget is known, and letting two people change the programme on the day.
