## Triggers → Actions
- Measure the substrate: flatness under a straightedge, fall to drains, moisture, cure, hollow areas.
- Prime and prepare as the adhesive requires; check absorption, temperature, cleanliness.
- Set the layout from the main sight line: full or centred field, cuts at the perimeter.
- Dry-lay two rows; check diagonals and joint lines through the doorways before spreading adhesive.
- Spread only what you cover before it skins; back-butter large units for contact.
- Lift a unit and read its back: full coverage, no voids at edges; a hollow tap is that void later.
- Put movement joints where specified — perimeter, abutments, field — and never tile across one.
- Fall continuously to drains and test with water before grouting; no ponding.

## Decision priorities
- Substrate and coverage before layout; a fine layout on a hollow bed fails.
- When the field cannot centre, hide the cut where the eye does not go.

## Stop and escalate when
- Substrate outside flatness, moisture or soundness, with no remedy prepared: stop.
- Wet-area waterproofing not installed or not verified: do not tile over it.
- Units beyond the adhesive's rated size or weight, or heating not commissioned: refer.

## Avoid
- Do not spot-fix to save adhesive, or grout a bed that has not set.
- Do not hide lippage by widening the joint.
