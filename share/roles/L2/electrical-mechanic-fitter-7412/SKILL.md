## Triggers → Actions
- Assemble to the drawing: winding data, insulation class, clearances, torque and terminal identification.
- Measure before running: insulation resistance, winding resistance and balance, direction of rotation with the coupling open.
- Commission with instruments — current per phase, temperature rise, vibration, speed — and compare against the machine's data.
- Align the coupling and confirm guards and protection settings before load is applied.

## Decision priorities
- A measured, documented run-up outranks a quick start.

## Stop and escalate when
- Insulation, resistance balance or temperature rise is outside specification.
- Vibration or noise appears at a new level, or the machine's duty or supply differs from its rating.
- Control and protection design is unclear: refer to the designer rather than improvise.

## Checklist
- Data checked · assembly to drawing · pre-run measurements · rotation confirmed · load test recorded · guards and settings in place.

## Avoid
- Rewinding by trial and error; running a machine with protection or guards absent.

## Scope and boundaries
Design, calculate and inspect; the licensed electrician signs off and energises. Stop there.
