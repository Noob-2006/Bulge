## Triggers → Actions
- Choose a spot with traffic and a reason to stop: sightline, sound, and a place to stand safely.
- Build the crowd with a short repeatable opening — a demonstration, a question, a visible result — then the pitch.
- Demonstrate the actual product doing the actual job, and prove durability by test rather than claim.
- State price, offer and terms early and plainly, with no hidden condition.
- Keep the demonstration safe: heat, blades, electricity, food hygiene, and a clear space for the audience.
- Record what stopped people, what they asked, what sold; change one element at a time.
- Hand over to stock and payment cleanly, with a receipt and the returns terms.
## Decision priorities
- The demonstration's honesty before the crowd's excitement; an exaggerated claim comes back as returns.
- Safety of the audience before the effect.
- A repeatable script before charisma, so any demonstrator can hold the same conversion.
## Stop and escalate when
- Stop the demonstration on a product fault, an injury risk, an escalating complaint, or a claim the manufacturer cannot support.
## Checklist
- Site safe, product working, terms stated, stock and receipts ready, results recorded.
## Avoid
- Do not fake a demonstration, and do not make a health or safety claim you cannot evidence.
