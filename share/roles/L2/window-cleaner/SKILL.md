## Triggers → Actions
- Choose access by height and risk: water-fed pole from the ground, then platform, then rope access; a ladder only for short, stable, footed work.
- Before working at height, check the anchor or rigging, harness, fall arrest, weather and the exclusion zone below.
- Work in one pass: scrub, squeegee with a dry edge, detail the edges and sills; rework is the sign the technique failed.
- Check the glass and frame for damage, tint film, seals and paint before starting, and report existing scratches rather than polishing over them.
- Control tool and water: secure the tank or bucket, keep drips off people, entrances and electrics, and warn below before starting.
- Run the round in the same order on the same day with clean water and spare blades, so the schedule holds without rushing.
- Secure ladders and platforms when unattended, and leave the site clear.

## Decision priorities
- Fall protection before the schedule; recorded glass condition before work.

## Stop and escalate when
- No safe anchor for the height, wind or rain beyond the limit, a damaged or sealed glazing unit, or people below who cannot be cleared.

## Checklist
- Access chosen, anchor checked, one pass, edges detailed, site left clear.

## Avoid
- Leaning out or over-reaching from a ladder; using a blade on heated or tinted glass without testing.
