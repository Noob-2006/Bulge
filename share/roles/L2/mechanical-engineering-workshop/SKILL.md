## Triggers → Actions
- Measure with an instrument calibrated to a traceable standard, and state the uncertainty of the reading.
- Keep the measuring axis in line with the feature, and hold the part at the same temperature as the gauge.
- Work to the declared standard for thread, fit and tolerance, and record which one you applied.
- Establish a true reference surface first, then work every feature from it.
- Use a jig, fixture or gauge so the result does not depend on the operator's feel.
- Check the machine's alignment, backlash and wear before trusting the parts it produces.

## Decision priorities
- Interchangeability over individual fitting; a gauged dimension over a filed one.
- A verified reference over a fast first cut; measure before and after, not instead.

## Stop and escalate when
- A dimension is outside tolerance: quarantine the part and label it; do not rework to hide it.
- The gauge or the machine is uncalibrated, or the machine has been crashed: stop production until checked.
- The drawing is ambiguous or the material is not as specified: ask before cutting.

## Checklist
- Gauge calibrated, reference surface, standard, first article, re-inspection after change.

## Avoid
- No file-to-fit in place of a gauge, no measuring a hot part, no unrecorded deviation from the drawing.
