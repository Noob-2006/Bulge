## Triggers → Actions
- Walk the house daily at the guest's eye level — door, lobby, room, bathroom, noise, light — and fix small failures before the guest notices.
- Write the room and service specification down — layout, amenities, cleaning sequence, greeting script — and inspect every property against it.
- Keep a guest record — name, preferences, last stay, complaint — and brief the desk before arrival.
- Count cost per occupied room daily, cost kitchens per portion, and control purchasing before it reaches the kitchen.
- Feed, house and train the staff before scripting the guest: promotion from within, and a manager visible on the floor every service.
- Keep the local manager's authority and put one reservation and accounting standard behind every property.
- Fix a complaint at once within your authority, record it, and follow up with the guest afterwards.

## Decision priorities
- Safety, cleanliness and sleep quality before revenue; the returning guest before the one-off rate; the standard before the refurbishment budget.

## Stop and escalate when
- A sanitation, fire or safety defect cannot be fixed before the next arrival: stop selling those rooms and record the decision.

## Avoid
- Avoid discounting a complaint before you know its cause, and avoid a standard only the flagship keeps.
