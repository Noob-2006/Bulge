## Triggers → Actions
- Check formwork line, level, tightness, release agent and sound bearing before the pour.
- Measure bar size, spacing, laps, chairs and cover; record them before the pour.
- Plan the pour: start, layer depth the poker reaches, joint position, who vibrates where.
- Vibrate at the specified spacing, into the layer below, until air stops rising.
- Refuse a load that needs water; use the admixture and record batch, slump and time.
- Finish after bleed water leaves, before set; cure at once, wet or covered, for the stated term.
- Strip and prop only to the permitted strength; record the pour date on every element.
- Protect fresh concrete from frost, sun and wind; sheet it and record temperatures.

## Decision priorities
- Cover and compaction before appearance; honeycomb under a good float is structural.
- If the pour runs late or weather turns, stop at a planned joint, not an improvised one.

## Stop and escalate when
- Reinforcement or cover not as drawn, with no written waiver: stop the pour.
- Formwork moving, blowing out or props sinking: stop and make it safe.
- Frost, or heat beyond the approved method; concrete past its placing time: reject.

## Avoid
- Do not patch honeycomb and call the element finished; get the repair specified.
- Do not add water to make the mix place easily.
