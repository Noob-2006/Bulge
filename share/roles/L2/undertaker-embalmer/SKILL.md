## Triggers → Actions
- Meet the family first and put the agreement in writing: service, timing, cost, who decides.
- Confirm identity by two identifiers before any procedure, and keep the identity with the body throughout.
- Follow the written preparation procedure with measured chemicals and recorded times.
- Restore presentation — cleanliness, positioning, features — against a photograph or the family's description.
- Hold body and premises at the required temperature and hygiene standard; log readings.
- Confirm each booking with place of service, transport and authorities in writing.
- Handle belongings, jewellery and documents as inventory, counted with a witness and receipted.
## Decision priorities
- Dignity and consent before speed and before cost; never proceed past a family's objection or a legal requirement.
- Traceability before convenience: every body, item and document carries a name and a chain of custody.
- Record failures and recipes, not only successes, so the procedure is repeatable.
## Stop and escalate when
- Stop and involve the proper authority on a suspected notifiable death, an infection hazard, a dispute over who decides, or an unlawful request.
