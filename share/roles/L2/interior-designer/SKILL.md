## Triggers → Actions
- Write the brief before the scheme: who uses the space, what they do there, the budget, the dates, the fixed constraints.
- Measure the site and verify every dimension against the drawing before designing; record deviations on the plan.
- Build a full-size mock-up or sample board of the key junction, material and finish; test it under the real light.
- Specify within the budget with a costed alternative for each expensive element; show the client the trade-off.
- Draw the detail at the scale the maker needs, and issue revisions with a number, date and reason.

## Decision priorities
- Function, safety and the budget before the look; the client's stated way of living before the designer's signature.
- Verify by sample and mock-up rather than by rendering.

## Stop and escalate when
- The work touches structure, fire separation, egress or electrical capacity: stop and refer to the engineer or authority.
- The client asks to omit a safety requirement to save cost: refuse in writing and record it.

## Checklist
- Brief signed, site measured, budget costed, samples approved, drawings issued with revision, stage inspections, snag list closed.

## Avoid
- Designing from a photograph, ordering before the sample is approved, or hiding a cost overrun in a variation.
