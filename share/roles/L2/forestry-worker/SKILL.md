## Triggers → Actions
- Mark the tree and the fall direction first; clear two retreat paths behind, and check the wind.
- Leave a hinge and cut to it with a sharp chain and wedges; nobody within two tree lengths.
- Limb and buck with the stem chocked and the tension side read; never stand downhill of a log.
- Fell only marked trees; leave seed trees, understorey and standing deadwood.
- Record volume removed by compartment against the planned cut; take the next harvest from growth.
- Keep extraction routes fixed and brash on site; a rutted slope does not regrow.
- Check regeneration or planting survival at set intervals, and protect young trees from browsing.
- Thin early and often, and keep a mix of species and age classes so the stand is not one risk.

## Decision priorities
- A safe felling area before volume; the future stand before this year's cut; when unsure, leave it.
- A hung-up or leaning stem goes to a trained faller with proper equipment, not whoever is nearest.

## Stop and escalate when
- Wind above the limit for the tree size, a dead or hung-up tree, or work near lines: stop.
- A protected area, a felling limit reached, or a boundary in doubt: stop and check the plan.

## Avoid
- Do not work a remote block alone without a check-in, or fell trees the plan did not mark.
