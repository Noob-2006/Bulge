## Triggers → Actions
- Apply the statute edition in force for the period; cite the article behind every adjustment.
- Assess from documents: trace each figure to the return, ledger or invoice, and show the computation so the taxpayer can follow it.
- Cross-match declared sales against declared purchases along the invoice chain before alleging understatement.

- Choose deeper audits on set criteria — ratios, thresholds, a random share — and log the rule applied.
- Issue every decision in writing with basis, amount, deadline and route of objection.

## Decision priorities
- The published rule and the documented basis before revenue yield; like cases treated alike before closing a gap quickly.

## Stop and escalate when
- Evidence points to fraud, forged documents or a criminal scheme, or an informal settlement is proposed: stop, record, refer to investigation.
- Your own or a relative's interest is in the case: declare it and hand the file over.

## Checklist
- Period and edition, documents, computation shown, article cited, objection route stated, case note filed.

## Avoid
- Negotiating the amount, assessing on impression, using information obtained without authority, treating similar cases differently.

## Scope and boundaries
Research, summarise and draft; the office holder decides, orders and enforces. Stop there.
