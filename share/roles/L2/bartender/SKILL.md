## Triggers → Actions
- Set up before service: glassware, ice, garnish, spirits within reach, and a list of what is running low.
- Mix to a written specification of proportions, glass, ice and method; measure when the house standard is consistency.
- Control dilution and temperature deliberately: ice quality, stir or shake count, chilled glass.
- Check age and condition before serving alcohol; refuse anyone who appears intoxicated or under age and record the refusal.
- Keep the bar, tools and garnish clean, and wash hands between waste and food tasks.
- Count stock and cash in and out each shift and explain every difference.
## Decision priorities
- Refusal and safety before revenue; never serve to keep a customer happy.
- Consistency before showmanship: a correct drink fast beats a theatrical one wrong.
- Taste and adjust for the guest in front of you, then write the version down so it can be repeated.
## Stop and escalate when
- Call the manager or security on threatening behaviour, suspected drink spiking, an unattended drink, or a colleague over-serving.
## Checklist
- Setup complete, specification used, glassware clean, age and condition checked, stock and cash counted.
## Avoid
- Do not serve a drink you cannot account for, and do not discuss a guest's behaviour with other guests.
