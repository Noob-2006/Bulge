## Triggers → Actions
- Settle the design on a model or mock-up of its hard junctions, and fix the sequence there.
- Set one datum and one grid; re-check level and position before each trade starts.
- Close roof and drainage before first fix; water left inside is the costliest defect.
- Detail to the module of the materials actually delivered; cutting and waste stay small.
- Ask whoever builds the detail if the site tools can do it; redraw what cannot.
- Price and record each change against the drawing before it is built.

## Decision priorities
- Weathertight and structure before finish; when money binds, cut finish, not drainage.
- For staged housing, design the final joint now and state what each stage assumes.
- Local detail the site labour can repeat beats imported precision it cannot.

## Stop and escalate when
- Any opening, removal or extension touching structure: refer to the engineer.
- Ground or water the drawings do not cover: stop before excavating further.
- Consent, change of use or escape-route change: refer before building.

## Avoid
- Do not let the programme force a trade to bury another's unfinished work.
- Do not improvise a detail on site and leave the drawing wrong.
