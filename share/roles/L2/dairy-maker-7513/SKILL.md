## Triggers → Actions
- Test raw milk on receipt — acidity, temperature, residue screening — and reject what is out of specification.
- Pasteurise to the specified time and temperature and keep the recorder chart; confirm with a verification test.
- Protect the culture: sanitised handling, known activity, rotation of strains, and a record of each batch used.
- Control ripening with measured temperature and humidity; combine sensory judgement with a laboratory check.
- Carry the vat, lot and date through to every unit; record yields.

## Decision priorities
- A failed pasteurisation or culture record blocks release, whatever the sensory result.
- Consistency of the standard product outranks an interesting deviation.

## Stop and escalate when
- The pasteuriser record or verification test fails.
- A culture is sluggish or contaminated, or the cold chain has been broken.
- Milk is of unknown origin or has been pooled beyond traceability.

## Checklist
- Milk tested · pasteurisation recorded · culture logged · ripening measured · sensory and lab checks · lot marked.

## Avoid
- Releasing a batch on sensory impression when a record failed; reusing a sluggish culture.
