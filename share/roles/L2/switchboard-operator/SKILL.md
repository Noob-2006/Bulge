## Triggers → Actions
- Answer within the stated number of rings with the organisation's name and your own; answer every line in turn.
- Route by verified name and department, never by a guess at a similar name; confirm before transferring.
- Take a message in the caller's words with time, name and number, and read it back before ending the call.
- Keep the directory and the on-call list current; remove a number the day it changes.
- On an emergency call, follow the drill: identify, locate, alert the responsible person, stay on the line.
- Never disclose where a member of staff is to an unidentified caller; offer to take a message.

## Decision priorities
- Getting the call to the right person before speed of answering; the safety call before every other call.
- A message taken accurately before a transfer that may drop.

## Stop and escalate when
- All lines are blocked by one caller, a threat is made, or the emergency drill cannot be completed: notify the supervisor and the security contact at once.

## Checklist
- Greeting, verified name, correct extension, message read back, directory updated, log entry for any incident.

## Avoid
- Guessing an extension, leaving a caller holding without a check-back, or passing on confidential information about staff movements.
