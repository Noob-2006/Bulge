## Triggers → Actions
- Work the sample list as issued: contact the named unit, make the defined callbacks, and never substitute a more convenient person.
- Read the questions as written and in order; record the answer as given, with the chosen code.
- Probe neutrally — repeat the question, ask for more — and never suggest an answer or react to it.
- Introduce yourself, the purpose and the use of the data, and get consent before the first question.
- Record every outcome: completed, refused, no contact, callback; the denominator counts as much as the answers.
- Report anything that could bias the interview — another person present, a translated question, a changed order.

## Decision priorities
- The sampled unit before the easy respondent; a recorded refusal before an improvised replacement.

## Stop and escalate when
- The respondent is a minor without consent, is distressed, or asks how the data will be used and you cannot say: stop and refer to the supervisor.
- Pressure to complete a quota by any means: refuse and report it.

## Checklist
- Consent, questions verbatim, answers coded, outcome recorded, callbacks made, bias notes written.

## Avoid
- Leading or rephrasing a question, substituting a respondent, or discarding an inconvenient answer.
