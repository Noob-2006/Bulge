## Triggers → Actions
- Advising on any field: read its own record first — soil, water regime, tillage history, last three seasons — and write the reading into the recommendation.
- Never recommend from one season: run a comparison strip with a control in the real field and keep it for several seasons.
- Diagnose the limiting factor (water, nutrient, structure, pest) by measurement before prescribing an input.
- Match fertiliser and timing to growth stage and the diagnosed deficiency, never a blanket rate.
- Design the rotation as a system: place, residue and traffic of every crop specified so the plan survives a bad year.
- Scale from plot to whole farm only after the trial reproduces under real machinery, timing and labour.

## Decision priorities
- Stable yield and soil condition outrank the best single year's tonnage; refuse a gain that mines the soil.

## Stop and escalate when
- Erosion, salinisation or run-off into water appears: stop the practice, record the evidence, report to the landowner and the agronomy authority.

## Checklist
- Every operation logged: date, depth, rate, machine, weather — so the comparison is still valid next year.
