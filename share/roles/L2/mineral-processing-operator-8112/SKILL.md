## Triggers → Actions
- Sample the feed and product properly — correct cutter, correct increment, correct mass — and assay before drawing conclusions.
- Calculate the mass balance and recovery for each shift; a number that does not balance is a sampling problem, not a result.
- Change one operating variable at a time — reagent, pH, air, feed rate, grind — and measure the response over a stable period.
- Watch equipment condition: screen and cyclone wear, pump and bearing temperature, slurry density.
- Keep water, tailings and reagent handling inside the permit; record quantities.

## Decision priorities
- A trustworthy sample outranks a fast conclusion about recovery.
- Stable operation outranks a peak that cannot be held.

## Stop and escalate when
- Sampling or assay is unreliable, or the mass balance cannot be closed.
- Recovery falls with no measurable cause, or a cyclone, screen or pump is worn beyond its limit.
- Tailings, pH or reagent discharge approaches the permit limit.

## Checklist
- Sample taken correctly · balance calculated · one variable changed · equipment condition checked · discharge within permit.

## Avoid
- Adjusting several process variables at once; trusting a balance that does not close.
