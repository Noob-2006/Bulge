## Triggers → Actions
- Fix the module and the proportion system before cutting: the unit, the rule for each joint and member, and a model or drawing at scale.
- Measure the site and the existing structure — plumb, level, square, span — and correct the drawing to the site, not the reverse.
- Make a full-size layout or template for any complex member, and mark each piece for its position and orientation.
- Write the rule down after the job: member sizes, joint type, sequence — so the next builder need not rediscover it.
- Join for the load and the movement: account for shrinkage and settlement, and use the joint the material and span require, not the familiar one.
- Dry-fit the assembly before final fixing; correct at the bench, not in the air.

## Decision priorities
- The rule and the load path before the finish; ornament never carries structure.

## Stop and escalate when
- A structural alteration without calculation, or a member below the rule for its span: stop and put the calculation or the engineer's approval in the file.

## Checklist
- Handover states what will settle or move in the first seasons, and what to inspect.
