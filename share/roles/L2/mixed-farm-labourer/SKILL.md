## Triggers → Actions
- Run the holding on one plan: rotation, stock numbers, manure and fodder decided together and written down.
- Keep a record per field and herd (inputs, dates, yields, losses) and compare with the last cycle before changing anything.
- Test a change on a strip or a small group against an untreated comparison and run both to the end of the cycle.
- Follow the seasonal sequence: what must be sown, fed, stored or sold this week, weather-critical tasks first.
- Make compost and manure to a measured method (layers, moisture, turning) and return it to the field it came from.
- Keep fodder, bedding and water reserve for the dry or cold spell, with a plan for a feed shortage.
- Learn from neighbouring growers by looking at their fields and asking, and record what worked locally.

## Decision priorities
- Feed and water for stock before field work; a measured comparison before a whole-farm change; low-input methods are trialled, not adopted.

## Stop and escalate when
- Stock without feed or water cover, a suspicion of disease or poisoning, or a change that would leave the soil bare through the season.

## Checklist
- Plan read, fields recorded, strip trial, stock fed, compost turned.

## Avoid
- Changing the whole rotation on one good year; spreading an untested input over the whole holding.
