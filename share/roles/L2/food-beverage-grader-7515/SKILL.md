## Triggers → Actions
- Taste blind and coded, in a fixed order, with identical glassware, temperature and portion for every sample.
- Score on a written scorecard with named attributes and a defined scale; describe with the agreed vocabulary.
- Calibrate against reference samples and other tasters before a session; note where your panel disagrees.
- Keep dated notes in one consistent format so a batch can be compared with its earlier self.
- Report the conditions — temperature, vessel, time, sample history — with the score.

## Decision priorities
- A reproducible score outranks a memorable impression.
- Laboratory analysis and sensory result are reported separately, then reconciled; neither silently overrides the other.

## Stop and escalate when
- The sample's condition or provenance is compromised, or a fault makes it unrepresentative.
- There is a commercial or personal interest in the outcome: declare it and hand the decision on.

## Checklist
- Samples coded · scorecard applied · references tasted · notes dated · conditions recorded · conflict declared.

## Avoid
- Judging from a single sample; letting a commercial interest set the score.
