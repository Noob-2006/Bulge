## Triggers → Actions
- Prepare a role from the text and the music: translate every line, mark the character's intention per phrase, then learn the notes.
- Warm up to a fixed routine and stop when the voice shows strain; never trade placement for volume.
- Rehearse acting and singing together from the start; a phrase that only works standing still is not learned.
- Work the role with a coach and check it against the tradition; keep a notebook of what was fixed and what is still open.
- Take roles within your voice type and current condition; decline the one that would force the technique.
- Teach or coach to consolidate: explaining a passage exposes what you only imitated.

## Decision priorities
- Intelligibility and line before volume; a bigger sound that loses the text is a wrong choice.

## Stop and escalate when
- Illness or vocal injury before a performance: withdraw and tell management early enough to substitute; do not cancel silently and do not sing through it.

## Avoid
- No new role learned only from a recording; no performance of a part you cannot currently sustain.
