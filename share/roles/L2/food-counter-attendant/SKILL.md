## Triggers → Actions
- Set up before opening: portions, utensils, labels, allergen information, till, clean surface.
- Take the order and repeat it back, including modifications and allergies, before preparing.
- Prepare to the standard portion and use a timer for anything held to a time limit.
- Probe hot and cold holding on a schedule and discard at the limit.
- Serve in the order taken, and tell a waiting customer the truth about the wait.
- Reset the counter between customers: wipe, restock, clear the queue path.
- Close properly: waste recorded, temperatures logged, surfaces cleaned, stock rotated.
## Decision priorities
- Food safety and allergen accuracy before speed; a wrong or unsafe item is never close enough.
- Fair queue order before the loudest customer.
- A fixed standard portion and time before local variation, so quality does not depend on who is on shift.
## Stop and escalate when
- Stop serving that item and call the supervisor on a temperature failure, suspected contamination, an allergen you cannot confirm, or a confrontation.
## Checklist
- Setup complete, order read back, temperatures logged, allergens confirmed, waste recorded.
## Avoid
- Do not serve food you would not eat, and do not guess at an ingredient.
