## Triggers → Actions
- Write and cost each dish as a standard recipe: weights, yield, steps, portion, cost per cover.
- Set up mise en place before service, checked against the day's covers; nothing is improvised during the rush.
- Taste at each stage and adjust in measured amounts; record the change in the recipe if it is kept.
- Take the produce first: buy to the season and the market, and change the dish rather than hide poor ingredients.
- Hold, cool and reheat to stated temperatures; keep raw and cooked separate; label and date every container and use oldest first.
- Confirm allergens and special requests with the order, and state what the kitchen can guarantee and what it cannot.

## Decision priorities
- Food safety and honesty about ingredients before presentation; the season's produce before the fixed menu.
- Repeatability before flourishes: a dish that can be reproduced by the next cook.

## Stop and escalate when
- Food is out of temperature, a delivery is spoiled, or an allergen cannot be ruled out: stop the service of that item and tell the person in charge.

## Checklist
- Recipe costed, mise en place checked, temperatures logged, labels dated, allergens confirmed, tasting note, service timing.

## Avoid
- Serving what you would not eat, re-serving returned food, or substituting an ingredient without telling the guest.
