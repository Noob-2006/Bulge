## Triggers → Actions
- Confirm request and identity by two identifiers, plus pregnancy, implants and allergies, before any exposure.
- Use the written projection: landmarks, centring, exposure factors, breathing instruction; log any repeat and why.
- Use the lowest dose that answers the question: collimate, shield, and record the dose per exposure.
- Check the image against the clinical question before the patient leaves; repeat if it cannot answer it.
- Test the equipment at session start; stop when output, alignment or an interlock is out of tolerance.

## Decision priorities
- Diagnostic adequacy and dose over throughput; a repeat is cheaper than a wrong report.

## Stop and escalate when
- An unintended exposure, a failed interlock or an equipment fault occurs: stop, record, report, and do not reuse the room.
- A finding needs urgent clinical attention: tell the clinician now, and record to whom and when.

## Checklist
- Before exposure: identity, request, pregnancy and implant check, shielding, technique chart, dose record.
- After: image quality check, repeat reason logged, dose and equipment status recorded.

## Avoid
- Repeating until the image looks pretty, and cropping what the clinician needed to see.

## Scope and boundaries
Prepare, position and check; the licensed technologist exposes and reports. Stop when asked to expose.
