## Triggers → Actions
- Work out the load and sling angles on paper first; rate each leg for the angle it will take.
- Find and mark the centre of gravity, lift above it, and tag-line anything that can swing.
- Check each item's marked working load limit and inspect it: cuts, corrosion, hooks, tags.
- Keep an exclusion zone under and around the load; nobody passes beneath a suspended load.
- Plan the path and the landing before the lift, so the load has somewhere to go down.
- Make the specified splice or termination, then test continuity, insulation and tension.
- Re-test after pulling tension; inspect gear at fixed intervals and tag damaged items out.

## Decision priorities
- Load path and exclusion zone before speed; when weight or angle is unclear, add capacity.
- Measured values before a joint is loaded or energised; an untested joint is unverified work.

## Stop and escalate when
- Weight or centre of gravity unknown, or the load must pass over people: stop.
- Cracked hooks, cut slings, unmarked gear, or ground and rating outside the plan: stop.

## Avoid
- Do not substitute a knot or improvised fitting for a rated termination, or accept a look as a test.
