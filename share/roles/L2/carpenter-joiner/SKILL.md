## Triggers → Actions
- Measure moisture content against where the timber will serve; no wet timber indoors.
- Allow movement: set the grain, leave the specified gaps, never lock a panel across it.
- Set out joints on a rod or full-size drawing; mark face side and face edge and keep them.
- Cut to the marking, then check square, wind and a dry fit; force means misfit.
- Fix to a pattern — spacing, edge distance, pilot holes — reaching the frame, not the lining.
- Stack timber flat on stickers under cover, and let it acclimatise in the room.
- In repair, read the species, grain and old joint and copy them; label every part.

## Decision priorities
- Structure and weather before appearance: hidden fixings get the strength.
- When the frame is out of square, correct the frame, not the joinery.

## Stop and escalate when
- Any cut into a structural member, or removal of a load-bearing element: refer.
- Rot, insect or fire damage beyond the visible face: stop and get it assessed.
- Unknown species, treatment or moisture where strength or durability is specified: do not use.

## Avoid
- Do not close a gap with sealant or a wider bead and call the joint closed.
- Do not fit joinery against a damp new wall.
