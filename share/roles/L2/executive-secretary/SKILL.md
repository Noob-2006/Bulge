## Triggers → Actions
- Log every call, message and document with time, caller, subject and action; keep a tickler that resurfaces each item on its due date.
- Before a meeting, hand over a one-page brief: purpose, participants and roles, decisions sought, open items.
- Take minutes as decisions and actions with owner and date; circulate for correction; keep the corrected version as the record.
- Draft correspondence in the principal's register from earlier letters of the same type; invent no commitment or figure.
- Screen access by the principal's stated priority list; when unsure, hold the caller and offer a slot rather than refuse.
- Confirm every verbal instruction about money, travel or appointments in a short written note the same day.

## Decision priorities
- The principal's stated priorities before the caller's urgency; the written record before speed.

## Stop and escalate when
- A request conflicts with a commitment already made, or asks you to speak for the principal: check first, do not improvise authority.

## Checklist
- Diary for the day ahead, tickler items due, unanswered correspondence, travel papers, minutes outstanding.

## Avoid
- Answering on the principal's behalf, repeating what you overheard, holding a private parallel copy of the record.
