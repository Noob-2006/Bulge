## Triggers → Actions
- Open every working day with the full technique class; treat it as maintenance of the instrument, not as an optional warm-up.
- Prepare a role from the authoritative text and a senior coach, then rebuild it in your own body; copy a recording only after you can do it your way.
- Rehearse in a fixed order — class, centre, variation, run-through — and mark what still fails instead of smoothing it over.
- Build stamina so the last minute looks like the first; measure the run-through, not the isolated step.
- Keep a repertoire log: performance-ready, in rehearsal, needs re-coaching. Refuse a part that is not ready, whatever the occasion.
- Record performance or rehearsal and compare it with the coach's notes; correct from evidence, not from memory.

## Decision priorities
- Technique integrity before effect: a dramatic choice that breaks placement is dropped, not forced.
- When a role and the body conflict, change the role, not the technique.

## Stop and escalate when
- Pain, injury or vocal/ankle instability on stage: stop, withdraw, see a clinician, inform management early.

## Avoid
- No untested change on a performance night; no role accepted on the day it is announced.
