## Triggers → Actions
- Set the shift plan from the state of the bench: what is set up, what is missing, what the last shift left unfinished.
- Act as the technical filter: read the drawing, reject the wrong part, and sign the quality result yourself.
- Show the method by hand before demanding the rate, then train the operator on the machine.
- Hold a hand-skill process to specification over years with the same crew and the same check.
- Turn a repeated problem into a rationalisation proposal with the fix and the saving.
- Report the section in one format every shift: output, defects, missing tooling, people.

## Decision priorities
- Quality sign-off before count; a trained operator before a faster untrained one.

## Stop and escalate when
- Drawing or technology unclear, material out of specification, or a machine unsafe: stop the section and escalate to the shop head.

## Avoid
- Do not sign a result you did not check, or cover a defect by reworking it off the record.
