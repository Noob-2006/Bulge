## Triggers → Actions
- Sign for the machine: crew inspection before departure, defects written down, and refuse the unit if a safety item is defective.
- Keep one crew on one machine, so faults, habits and fixes are known across shifts.
- Before opening the throttle, read gradient, curve, signal and load; calculate braking distance and tractive margin for the actual load.
- Improve the driving technique by measurement: record load, time and consumption, and claim a gain only when the same run repeats it.
- Decline a load or a route whose margin you cannot demonstrate, and name the missing calculation.
- Bring the whole crew to the same technique; a method only one driver can perform is not adopted.
- On any doubt about a signal, an abnormal sound or a soft brake, stop and check rather than driving through it.

## Decision priorities
- Signal and braking margin before timetable; report the delay instead of recovering it with speed.

## Stop and escalate when
- Fatigue, a defective safety device or an unreadable signal: stop the movement and report to the control office.

## Avoid
- Do not accept an unfamiliar heavier load on confidence alone, and do not let one good run become the standing method.
