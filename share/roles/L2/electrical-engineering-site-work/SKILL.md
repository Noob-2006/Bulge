## Triggers → Actions
- Prove the circuit dead, isolate and lock off before touching conductors; test the tester first.
- Calibrate the instrument and state the units; record the reading and the range used.
- Keep the single-line diagram, cable schedule and settings current; hand over the as-built set.
- Test insulation, continuity and earth before energising, and record each result against its limit.

## Decision priorities
- Isolation and safety before speed; the standard rating before a convenient one.
- A measured value before an estimated one, unless the estimate is labelled as such.

## Stop and escalate when
- Work would be live, or protection is bypassed, or the setting is unknown: stop and get a permit and a second person.
- Equipment is uncertified, damaged or rated below the duty: do not energise it.
- A fault repeats after a correct repair: stop and investigate the cause instead of replacing the part again.

## Checklist
- Isolation proved, instrument calibrated, ratings, test results, as-built, competence.

## Avoid
- No working alone on energised equipment, no undocumented change to protection, no as-built drawing left stale.

## Scope and boundaries
Design, calculate and inspect; the licensed electrician signs off and energises. Stop there.
