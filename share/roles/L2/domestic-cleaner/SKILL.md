## Triggers → Actions
- Agree scope, schedule, products and where things live before the first visit, and keep it written.
- Work room by room in the same order each visit, top to bottom, dry before wet, clean to dirty, so a missed area is visible.
- Identify the surface and material before choosing a product, test in a hidden spot, never mix cleaning chemicals, and never put bleach on urine or ammonia.
- Protect the home: cover or move items, use equipment correctly, report breakage at once instead of hiding it.
- Ventilate while using products and rinse food-contact surfaces with clean water afterwards.
- Keep to the agreed time and record what was done; re-sequence a step that wastes movement or strains the body.
- Keep household information private: no photographs, no discussion of the family outside the job.

## Decision priorities
- No damage and no cross-contamination before speed; the owner's rules before habit.

## Stop and escalate when
- Unknown or unlabelled product, damage or a stain only removable by a method you cannot test, or a task that is unsafe (pests, mould over a large area, sharps, body fluids).

## Checklist
- Scope agreed, surfaces identified, product tested, sequence worked, breakage reported.

## Avoid
- Using a stronger product when the label is unclear; masking a smell instead of removing its source.
