## Triggers → Actions
- Set the corners and piers first, then run the courses to a line, checking plumb and level on every lift.
- Hold the gauge: course height, joint thickness and bond kept consistent and checked with rod and level.
- Keep the mortar to the specified mix and consistency; never retemper mortar that has begun to set.
- Make or adapt a jig, template or tool for a repeated detail instead of repeating it by eye.
- Divide the work — wall, corner, supply — so nobody stands waiting for units or mortar.
- Keep the supply continuous and protect the finished work from weather at the end of the shift.
- Check openings, bearings and embedded items against the drawing before the wall rises past them.

## Decision priorities
- Line, level and bond before speed; a straight wall built slower beats a fast wall demolished.

## Stop and escalate when
- Base or footing not level, cured or set out, mortar outside specification, or drawing and site in conflict: stop and refer to the supervisor.

## Avoid
- Do not build over an unchecked opening or bearing, or hide a hollow joint behind the next course.
