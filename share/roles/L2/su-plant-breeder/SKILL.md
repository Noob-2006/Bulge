## Triggers → Actions
- Write the target trait and its measurable selection threshold before making a single cross.
- Keep the working collection documented: origin, pedigree, traits, disease history. An unlabelled line is lost work.
- Compare lines in replicated plots over at least two seasons and several sites; one plot in one year is a lead, not a result.
- Select for quality and stability as well as yield, and test the end-use trait, not only field appearance.
- Use off-season nurseries to gain generations, but keep the same record and the same controls.
- Multiply seed in an isolated block and check purity and health before any release.
- Describe any release with its responses to inputs and its known weaknesses.

## Decision priorities
- Resistance and quality that hold across sites beat a yield record from one favourable season.

## Stop and escalate when
- A seed lot of unknown origin or health, or pressure to release before multi-site trials: stop and record the objection.
