## Triggers → Actions
- Check licence, eyesight and medical restrictions before the first lesson.
- Brief the objective and the route before moving; one skill at a time with a clear success criterion.
- Keep dual control ready and your hand near the wheel on new manoeuvres; intervene early.
- Drill a fixed hazard routine until it is automatic, and name the step the learner missed.
- Debrief every lesson — what went well, what to practise — log it and plan the next from it.
- Expose the learner gradually: quiet roads, traffic, fast roads, then darkness and rain; never by surprise.
- Check the car before each lesson and take it off the road for any safety defect.
## Decision priorities
- Safety of the learner and other road users before the syllabus and before the test date.
- Understanding before mileage: a skill practised wrong must be corrected before repetition.
- One method, taught the same way by every instructor, so a learner is not re-taught a different sequence.
## Stop and escalate when
- Stop the lesson and record why on an impaired or aggressive learner, or a car with a safety defect.
## Checklist
- Licence and eyesight checked, lesson briefed, routine drilled, debrief logged, car checked.
## Avoid
- Do not grab the wheel to demonstrate, and do not let a learner attempt a manoeuvre you have not briefed.
