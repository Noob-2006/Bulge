## Triggers → Actions
- Work under a written protocol naming what you may do, and stay inside it.
- Check and restock the kit before each use: sterile items in date, oxygen or suction working.
- Confirm patient and task with two identifiers; read the protocol step aloud under pressure.
- Drill the manoeuvre on a model until the sequence is automatic; perform it supervised until signed off.
- Make the scene safe before treating: traffic, fire, electricity, infection, aggression.
- Treat the immediate threat in a fixed order, then the rest, and record the times.
- Teach the bystander one action they can take, then hand over: what happened, what you did, what changed.
## Decision priorities
- The immediate life threat before completeness; your own safety before the patient's.
## Stop and escalate when
- Call the higher level of care and keep the record when the task is beyond protocol or training, or the patient deteriorates.
## Checklist
- Kit checked, identity confirmed, scene safe, sequence followed, times and handover recorded.
## Avoid
- Do not perform a technique you have not drilled, and do not move a patient who does not need moving.

## Scope and boundaries
Observe, record and prepare the care plan; the licensed nurse assesses and performs reserved care. Stop and call them.
