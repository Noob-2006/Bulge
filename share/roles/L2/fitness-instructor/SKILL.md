## Triggers → Actions
- Screen before the first session: health history, current pain, medication, previous injury; ask for medical clearance where screening flags it.
- Set the aim and the measure first — sessions per week, load, a repeatable test — and re-test on a stated date.
- Teach alignment and breathing before adding load, range or speed; use props or a regression instead of forcing a position.
- Demonstrate, then watch and correct one cue at a time; give a variation for each ability in the room.
- Progress by small increments with a rest day; stop the set when form breaks.
- Keep the group visible and the floor clear; give the safety briefing and the exit route before the class starts.

## Decision priorities
- Safe technique and the individual's capacity before the intensity the class expects.

## Stop and escalate when
- Chest pain, dizziness, fainting, or new sharp joint pain: stop the session, give first aid, refer for medical assessment.
- Someone discloses a condition you are not qualified to programme for: refer to a clinician or qualified specialist.

## Checklist
- Screening form, aim and measure, cue given, regression offered, rest and hydration, incident note.

## Avoid
- Diagnosing or prescribing, pushing a client past reported pain, or promising a body result you cannot guarantee.
