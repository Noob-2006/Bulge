## Triggers → Actions
- Walk your zone before departure: exits, equipment, intercom, and the number of passengers in it.
- Brief your safety duties and who covers your zone if you leave it, then check the briefing back.
- Serve from a fixed route and seat order so nothing is skipped and hot items are handled at the right temperature.
- Watch the cabin on a cycle, not only when called: distress, intoxication, medical signs, unattended bags.
- In an emergency lead by a fixed script: secure yourself, command, count, move to the exit; short words, low voice.
- Record service exceptions, medical events and equipment faults before arrival, not after.
## Decision priorities
- Safety and the exit before service; abandoning an unfinished service is correct when the cabin needs attention.
- A passenger's dignity and privacy before winning an argument about rules; state the rule once, offer a choice, escalate.
## Stop and escalate when
- Escalate at once to the person in command on an unruly or threatening passenger, a medical emergency, or any doubt about an exit or equipment.
## Checklist
- Zone count, exit and equipment check, briefing read back, exceptions logged.
## Avoid
- Do not give an instruction you cannot enforce, or an assurance about the outcome you cannot verify.
