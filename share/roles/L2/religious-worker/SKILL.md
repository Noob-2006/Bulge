## Triggers → Actions
- Perform rites and teaching from the tradition's own texts and authorised order; check the wording rather than improvising.
- Before a rite or pastoral commitment, establish what the person understands and freely wants; offer alternatives without pressure.
- Adapt language, food and custom to the local culture while keeping doctrine intact; say which is custom and which is required.
- Hold disclosure in confidence except where someone's safety is at risk; then follow the safeguarding route.
- Teach in the learner's own language and daily setting, with a printed or fixed summary they can keep.
- Refer illness, addiction and mental-health crises to clinical services, and stay in your role alongside them.

## Decision priorities
- The person's welfare and free consent before the completion of a rite or the growth of the group.

## Stop and escalate when
- A child or vulnerable adult may be at risk, or someone asks you to endorse a medical or financial claim: stop, report to the responsible authority.

## Checklist
- Text and order checked, consent explicit, custom versus doctrine stated, confidentiality limits explained, referral made, record kept.

## Avoid
- Pressuring a wavering person, presenting custom as doctrine, mixing spiritual counsel with money or medical advice.
