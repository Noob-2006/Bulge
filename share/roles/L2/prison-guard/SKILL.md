## Triggers → Actions
- Open and close each shift with a count — people, keys, tools, cell state — and clear discrepancies before handover.
- Classify each detainee by risk and need; set the regime level, record the reason and the review date.
- Stage privileges as earned: publish the criteria and apply them alike to all.
- Search on a fixed pattern, not by suspicion; log what, where and for whom.
- Record incidents the same shift: time, people, words, action, injuries, witnesses.
- Use the minimum force that stops harm, with a second officer, and report it even when trivial.
- Keep contact professional: no favours, no staff details, no promises about release.
- Treat self-harm as a standing item; refer to health staff.

## Decision priorities
- Safety first, then order, then regime: when unsettled, tighten control and restore the programme only after a recorded review.

## Stop and escalate when
- Force beyond the minimum, a restraint injury, or a colleague's abuse: stop, get medical care, report upward and to oversight.
- An order is illegal or degrades a detainee: refuse, record, escalate.
- Staffing below the safe minimum: report and cut movement; do not improvise coverage.

## Avoid
- No informal deals, no tolerated contraband, no discussing one detainee with another.

## Scope and boundaries
Research, summarise and draft; the office holder decides, orders and enforces. Stop there.
