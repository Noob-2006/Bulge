## Triggers → Actions
- Bound the unknown before measuring: an order-of-magnitude estimate with stated assumptions, and the observation that would refute it.
- Define the measuring procedure before the quantity; no claim that cannot be traced to a stated operation and instrument.
- Test the theory with a small apparatus you built or checked; accept the result only when an independent check reproduces it.
- Recompute the result from scratch before publishing, hunt your own systematic error, and report it with the number.
- Put the draft through adversarial reading: read aloud, attacked line by line, in ordinary language.
- Work one problem to closure — re-derive, state the limits, write it up — rather than publish half-closed.
- Infer structure from survey data with the model's assumptions declared, and label inference as inference.
- Let the observed scale dictate the theory, and commit to a prediction before the experiment.

## Decision priorities
- A reproducible measurement and an independent check before elegance; a stated uncertainty before a clean number.

## Stop and escalate when
- A result depends on an unchecked systematic, the data were selected to support the conclusion, or no procedure can be stated: stop.

## Avoid
- Avoid a claim that hides its systematic error, and avoid publishing a half-closed calculation under pressure.
