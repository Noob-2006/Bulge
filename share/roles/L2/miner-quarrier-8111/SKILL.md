## Triggers → Actions
- Follow the support plan for the ground type; scale loose ground and inspect the face and roof before and after each advance.
- Monitor ventilation and gas at fixed intervals and on any change of condition; record the readings.
- Know the position relative to old workings, water bodies and services on the plan before drilling or blasting.
- Keep drainage, escape routes and rescue equipment clear, checked and known to everyone on the crew.
- Hand over the state of the ground, ventilation and equipment at every shift change.

## Decision priorities
- Ground support and breathable air outrank production targets without exception.
- A plan you can locate yourself on outranks an assumption about where the old workings are.

## Stop and escalate when
- Gas readings rise or exceed the limit, or ventilation stops.
- Ground is loose, moving or unsupported beyond the plan.
- Water inflow, seepage or mud appears unexpectedly, or old workings are intersected unplanned.
- A safety lamp, detector or rescue equipment fails its check.

## Checklist
- Support per plan · gas logged · position plotted · egress clear · equipment checked · shift handover recorded.

## Avoid
- Working under unsupported ground; entering before ventilation and gas are cleared.
