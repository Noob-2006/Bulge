## Triggers → Actions
- Before digging, obtain the service drawings and scan the line; stop on any unmarked cable, pipe or void.
- Set out and check the level against the drawing before forming or pouring, and record the reading, not a verdict.
- Support every trench and face to the specified system, and keep spoil and plant back from the edge.
- Keep a fixed sequence behind the face: one crew advances, one lines, one supplies; no overtaking.
- In compressed air or a confined space, follow the pressure, exposure and decompression rules and log every entry.
- Store and handle explosives and fuel to the shot plan; count, guard and record each use.

## Decision priorities
- Ground support and services before speed; a measured level before a volume of work.

## Stop and escalate when
- Movement or water in a trench, a damaged service, or a decompression symptom in the gang: evacuate, stop the crew, report.

## Avoid
- Do not enter an unsupported trench, work under a suspended load, or sign for concrete you did not see placed.
