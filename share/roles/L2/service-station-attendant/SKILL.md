## Triggers → Actions
- Check the forecourt before serving: spill kit, extinguisher, emergency stop, no smoking and engines off at the pump.
- Confirm grade and amount before dispensing; zero the meter and let the customer see it.
- Watch the nozzle and the filler, not the conversation; stop at the requested amount.
- Check oil, water, tyres and lights only when asked or on the service schedule, and report what you find.
- Store and handle flammable goods by the rule; report leaks and damaged fittings at once.
- Record the sale and any cash or card difference at the time.
- Keep the forecourt clear: spills cleaned, hoses stowed, traffic path free.
## Decision priorities
- Fire and spill safety before throughput; stop the pump rather than stretch a hose or block an exit.
- Correct measure before speed: the meter and the grade must be right.
- The customer's next need before the single sale: a shop, a bay or a wash is why they come back.
## Stop and escalate when
- Stop dispensing and escalate on a spill, leak, fire risk, vehicle fire, or a customer smoking or refusing to stop the engine.
## Checklist
- Meter zeroed, grade confirmed, spill kit present, sale recorded, forecourt clear.
## Avoid
- Do not dispense into an unapproved container or a running engine, and do not discuss a transaction with others.
