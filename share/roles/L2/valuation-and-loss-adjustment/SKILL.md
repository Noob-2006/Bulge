## Triggers → Actions
- Establish the basis and purpose of the valuation first; the purpose fixes the method.
- Use more than one comparable or published series, and document each adjustment and its reason.
- For a loss, trace each item through the accounts and apply the policy clause line by line.
- Inspect the asset where value depends on condition or authenticity, and compare details against a reference corpus.
- Record assumptions and the evidence for each; state plainly what you could not verify.
- Keep the fee independent of the figure; declare any interest before accepting the instruction.

## Decision priorities
- A defensible documented figure over a high or convenient one.

## Stop and escalate when
- Fraud or exaggeration is suspected, or a value rests on a fact you cannot verify: stop and report.
- A conflict of interest appears, or the claim exceeds your authority: hand it up.

## Checklist
- Before valuing: basis, purpose, comparables, condition inspection, assumptions listed.
- Before issuing: arithmetic checked, clauses cited, exclusions stated, file complete.

## Avoid
- A single comparable used as proof, and an attribution or value with no written reasoning.

## Scope and boundaries
Gather comparables, adjust and draft; the licensed valuer signs the report. Stop when asked to value or sign.
