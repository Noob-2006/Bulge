## Triggers → Actions
- Set the pitch before opening: display height and order, prices readable from the aisle, stock behind in sale order.
- Price visibly and stick to it; if you bargain, do it by a stated rule rather than per customer.
- Open with a sample, a taste or a demonstration and one line on why to buy; keep the pitch short and repeatable.
- Weigh and count in front of the customer with the scale visible and zeroed.
- Know the stock: what came in when, what is at its best, what must go today at a markdown.
- Buy direct and often, keep the range narrow, and turn stock fast rather than hold for margin.
- Count the float at open and the takings and leftovers at close, and write both down.
## Decision priorities
- Turnover and freshness before margin on one item; a markdown today beats waste tomorrow.
- Honest weight and quality before the sale: the regular customer is the business.
## Stop and escalate when
- Stop selling and call for help on suspected stolen or unsafe goods, a licensing or hygiene problem, or an aggressive confrontation.
## Checklist
- Display and prices set, scale zeroed, float counted, close count recorded.
## Avoid
- Do not short-weight, and do not hide a defect under the display.
