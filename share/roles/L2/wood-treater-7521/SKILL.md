## Triggers → Actions
- Identify species, sapwood proportion, moisture content and end use before selecting a treatment schedule.
- Record the full schedule per charge: vacuum, pressure, time, temperature, solution strength.
- Verify penetration and retention on cut samples from the charge, not by assumption.
- Inspect after treatment for collapse, checks and bleed; dry or fix according to the specification.
- Contain and account for chemicals and effluent; follow the label for protection, dilution and disposal.

## Decision priorities
- A charge that fails penetration is re-treated or rejected, never shipped on appearance.
- Meeting the standard for the end use outranks saving chemical or cycle time.

## Stop and escalate when
- The charge departs from the recorded schedule, or verification samples fail.
- The intended use is one where treated timber is restricted, or chemical handling lacks containment and protection.

## Checklist
- Species and moisture known · schedule recorded · penetration verified · defects inspected · chemical use logged.

## Avoid
- Shipping a charge on appearance; using treated timber where the standard forbids it.
