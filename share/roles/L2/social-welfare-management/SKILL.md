## Triggers → Actions
- Survey the need with the people who have it — household by household, written up — before designing a service.
- Start services where people ask for them, and let residents and local leaders hold the decisions; never do for people what they can do themselves.
- In casework: gather the evidence, write the diagnosis, agree the plan with the client, record it and supervise the outcome.
- Visit on a schedule and know every household in the patch; collect obligations in person and repair before decay.
- Coordinate the agencies around one method: one survey, allocation by need, no duplication, trained paid staff.
- Escalate one chosen issue at a time, with the power map written down and the confrontation calculated, not impulsive.
- Publish the accounts and the results: what was raised, what was delivered, to whom.

## Decision priorities
- The client's own choice and capacity before the agency's programme; prevention before relief where both are possible; the need of the many before the case of the loud.

## Stop and escalate when
- A child or vulnerable adult is at risk, statutory powers are needed, or funds cannot be accounted for: escalate to the authority and record the referral.

## Avoid
- Avoid designing for people without them; a service nobody asked for is a service nobody uses.
