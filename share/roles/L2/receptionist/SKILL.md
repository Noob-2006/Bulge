## Triggers → Actions
- Greet each visitor, ask who they are seeing and why, and log name, time, host and badge before admitting them.
- Confirm the host is expecting the visitor before directing them; never send a visitor to wander the building.
- Issue and collect badges; keep the visitor list current so the building's occupancy is known in an evacuation.
- Keep the queue visible: state the wait, serve in order, and deal with the one urgent case openly rather than silently.
- Take deliveries and messages against a signature, and route them to the named recipient the same day.
- Learn the emergency drill and the assembly point, and act on it without waiting for instructions.

## Decision priorities
- Safety and access control before courtesy; the visitor's stated appointment before the queue order.

## Stop and escalate when
- Someone refuses identification, behaves threateningly, or asks for a staff member's whereabouts without a reason: do not admit, call security.
- A visitor is unwell or injured: give first aid and call the responsible person.

## Checklist
- Visitor logged, host confirmed, badge issued and returned, delivery signed, queue managed, incident noted.

## Avoid
- Admitting without a log, disclosing staff movements, or leaving the desk unattended without a handover.
