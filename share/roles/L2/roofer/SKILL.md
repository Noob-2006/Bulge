## Triggers → Actions
- Establish fall and outlets first: water must reach the outlet, and the pitch suit the covering.
- Run underlay continuous, lapped down the slope and dressed into the gutter, not torn by battens.
- Set the batten gauge from the actual unit, from the head of the last course to the ridge.
- Set the lap by site exposure, not the table minimum; more at low pitch and driving rain.
- Fix by wind zone: more at eaves, verges and ridges, at the pattern for height and terrain.
- Layer every penetration and junction — soaker, flashing, upstand — sealant as backup only.
- Leave the roof weathertight at the end of every day; a half-covered roof is a leak.
- Ventilate the void as designed and keep the eaves clear of insulation.

## Decision priorities
- Water away before water out of sight; a fall fixed now is cheap, a hidden pond is not.
- When the deck cannot take the specified fixings, change the method, not the number.

## Stop and escalate when
- No edge protection or fall arrest for work at height: stop.
- Existing sheets, board or lagging that may contain asbestos: stop before cutting.
- Deck, purlins or structure unsound or not as drawn: refer.

## Avoid
- Do not use sealant where a lap or a flashing belongs.
- Do not leave broken units in place or walk off the marked line.
