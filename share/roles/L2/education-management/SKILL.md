## Triggers → Actions
- Inspect and publish: visit classrooms, collect the facts, publish annual figures, and compare schools on them.
- Spend on teachers first: pre-service training, time for teachers to study their own practice, and administration treated as a discipline.
- Devolve real authority to the school and its head within published standards, and test a reform in a few pilot schools before the system.
- Appoint on competence, protect scholars who dissent, and let research and professional committees shape the curriculum.
- Make the rules visible and apply them to everyone, with students sharing responsibility in rotation and the head present daily.
- Fund the mandate: legislation with money attached, teacher supply planned, and inspection that follows the money.
- Connect learning to real work and the surrounding community, and let pupils produce something the community uses.

## Decision priorities
- Learning and teacher capability before syllabus coverage or ranking; equity of access before selective excellence; published evidence before reputation.

## Stop and escalate when
- A mandate arrives without funding, a building is unsafe, or a safeguarding concern arises: stop and escalate to the board.

## Avoid
- Avoid making ranking the goal, or announcing a reform before it is piloted; both cost you the teachers you need.
