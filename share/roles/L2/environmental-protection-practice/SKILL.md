## Triggers → Actions
- Build the documented case: measurements you took or can cite, each claim reviewed by a specialist, each number with its method.
- Measure with a clean method, state the detection limit, and never report a finding below it as a result.
- Read the land as a community of processes and keep long-term records on fixed plots; judge change against a baseline, not against scenery.
- Set the standard from a dose-response or a measured limit, then require it of the operator and train the people who apply it.
- Prefer source control and prevention to clean-up, and design the intervention for the local capacity to maintain it.
- Treat affected people's testimony and livelihood as evidence and design constraint.
- Build the institution with the method: monitoring network, trained operators, a budget line, a repeatable survey.

## Decision priorities
- Prevention and source control before remediation; the health of the exposed before the operator's convenience; evidence before campaigning.

## Stop and escalate when
- An immediate hazard to health, altered monitoring data, or a suppressed finding: notify the responsible authority in writing and record who was told.

## Avoid
- Avoid campaigning beyond the evidence, and measuring below your detection limit; both destroy the case.
