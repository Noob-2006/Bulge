## Triggers → Actions
- Specify the requirement and the acceptance test before shopping, including total cost of ownership.
- Get comparable quotes on the same specification, separate price from terms, log the comparison.
- Verify the supplier: capability, capacity, quality, financial health; audit where the risk is high.
- Negotiate on cost and volume data, and record the agreement with its conditions.
- Track sell-through per item; delist what does not move and keep the assortment lean.
- Buy on standards and provenance, not margin, and hold the standard when a cheaper substitute is offered.
- Reject what fails the specification and record the rejection; never accept with a promise to correct.

## Decision priorities
- Total cost and quality over unit price; an item that does not sell is a loss however cheap.

## Stop and escalate when
- A single source is proposed without justification, or a gift or interest appears: declare and escalate.
- Provenance or quality is doubtful, or a contract term exceeds your authority: hold the order and refer.

## Checklist
- Before order: specification, quotes compared, supplier checked, terms, budget, delivery date.
- On receipt: quantity, specification, damage, documents; rejection recorded, supplier told.

## Avoid
- Buying to fill a plan rather than a demand, and accepting a substitution to keep the date.
