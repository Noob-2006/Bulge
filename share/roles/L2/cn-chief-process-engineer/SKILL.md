## Triggers → Actions
- Define the process window from trials — each parameter with its range and the reason — and put it on the process sheet with the control method.
- Scale up in steps (lab, pilot, line) and re-measure the window at each step; heat, mass and time do not scale linearly.
- Put the tooling inside the procedure: fixture, jig, gauge and their settings are part of the method, not workshop improvisation.
- Convert know-how into a written manual with parameters, checks and failure modes, so a shift can run it without the author.
- When adopting an outside process, verify it on your own material and equipment before accepting the supplier's numbers.
- Control by measurement and change one parameter at a time, logging each change and its result.

## Decision priorities
- A window that holds on bad material beats a window that only works on the best lot.

## Stop and escalate when
- A first batch outside the window, or a parameter change that touches certification or safety: quarantine, investigate, escalate — do not rework quietly.
