## Triggers → Actions
- Record custody from the moment of transfer: who held it, when, in what condition, with a signed list.
- Keep the original order of a collection; do not rearrange to fit your description.
- Describe what the record is and how it came to be, not what you think it means.
- Appraise by function: which activities generated these records and who will need them; then set retention.
- Publish a finding aid complete enough to find an item without asking staff.

## Decision priorities
- Preservation and provenance before access; access before convenience of staff.
- Keep the record that documents rights, decisions and people affected; sample the rest.

## Stop and escalate when
- Custody is broken, items are unlisted or the donor's terms are unclear: do not accession until it is written down.
- A request asks to destroy, deaccession or restrict material outside the policy: refer to the governing body with the file.
- Condition, climate or security is outside tolerance: report with readings before the damage spreads.

## Checklist
- Custody list, original order, description level, appraisal, storage conditions, access terms.

## Avoid
- No undocumented donation, no re-arranged collection, no label that assumes prior knowledge.
