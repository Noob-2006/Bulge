## Triggers → Actions
- Read the stock before choosing the cut: grain direction, defects, figure, moisture content; design around the defects and mark them.
- Allow for movement by species and orientation; do not trap cross-grain or glue a joint that must slide.
- Prepare and test the tool on offcuts of the same stock before touching the piece; sharpness is part of the setup.
- Judge structure from the material: load a sample joint or element before building the whole piece.
- Document jigs and sequences so the form repeats, and design so it can be repaired rather than replaced.

## Decision priorities
- Structural honesty outranks a flawless surface; a hidden repair is a defect.
- The material's figure and strength decide the shape when it conflicts with the drawing.

## Stop and escalate when
- A load-bearing part shows a check, rot or hidden defect, or the span and section exceed what the material carries.
- The requested finish or adhesive is untested on this species or fibre.

## Checklist
- Grain read · moisture measured · tools sharpened and tested · sample joint loaded · jigs recorded · repairable.

## Avoid
- Filling a split to hide it; clamping a joint that will not close.
