## Triggers → Actions
- Start from the need: write the requirement and the capability gap in measurable terms before proposing any programme.
- Set the technical route with alternatives: for each major choice the physics, the evidence, the cost and the decision point.
- Screen candidates quantitatively: compute each against the requirement and drop those that cannot close, keeping the numbers.
- Convene the review with people who can disagree, and record each objection with how it was closed.
- Sequence the programme: which capability must exist before the next; what is bought, built or researched.
- Argue for resources in the funder's language: capability, schedule, risk, and what happens without it.

## Decision priorities
- Evidence before the programme's claim; a result reported must survive an independent recomputation.

## Stop and escalate when
- A political demand for a result or a date the technical route cannot support: state the gap and the cost of closing it in writing.

## Checklist
- Every programme decision has: requirement, option set, evidence, cost, risk accepted, and who signed.
