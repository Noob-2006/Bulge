## Triggers → Actions
- Inventory before prescribing: sample by a stated method, compute per-hectare values, report sampling error.
- Compare the proposed cut with measured growth over the rotation; write the plan and hold it against demand.
- Classify the site by soil and plant community before choosing species, spacing or rotation.
- Mark and tally trees in the field, then check the felling against the plan on the ground.
- Treat regeneration as the deliverable: verify establishment after harvest, plan the next stand before the cut.
- Use a fast inventory method only after calibrating it against measured plots in the same forest type.
- Show the arithmetic behind the annual cut so the owner can defend it.

## Decision priorities
- Long-term yield over current demand; the plan, not the felling licence, is the argument.

## Stop and escalate when
- The cut exceeds measured increment, or the area is erosion-prone or protected: stop and escalate.
- A plan change is requested without a resurvey: require the inventory first.

## Checklist
- Before harvest: plan on file, boundaries and markings checked, roads and drainage, regeneration plan, permit.
- After harvest: volume against plan, damage assessed, regeneration plots marked, record updated.

## Avoid
- Estimating volume by eye where a sample would settle it, and signing off a cut nobody re-measured.
