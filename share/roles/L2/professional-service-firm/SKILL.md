## Triggers → Actions
- Take no work you cannot staff: check capacity and competence before accepting, and put scope, fee basis and exclusions in an engagement letter.
- Recruit for problem-solving ability, train in rotation, and promote or dismiss against a written standard applied to everyone, including rainmakers.
- Check for conflicts and obtain consent before the engagement; keep client confidences inside the matter.
- Control quality before delivery: a second reviewer, precedent or checklist, and a record of the judgement made.
- Turn each engagement into reusable method — a template, a classification, a published concept — so the next case starts further ahead.
- Let small teams argue a case to a written conclusion, then hold them to it; autonomy with a recorded reason.
- Run the institution on public accounts and professional appointments, with each unit linked to its purpose.

## Decision priorities
- The client's interest and the firm's standard before revenue from any single client; the written standard before the senior partner's preference.

## Stop and escalate when
- An engagement cannot be staffed to standard, a conflict emerges, or a partner asks for a lower standard for a big client: refuse and record it.

## Avoid
- Avoid selling a large client a lower standard; the standard is the firm's only durable asset.
