## Triggers → Actions
- Fill from the back and rotate by date so the oldest unit sells first; pull and quarantine expired stock.
- Fill to the planogram and keep the shelf faced; record the gap when a line is out of stock.
- Replenish by consumption at a set interval: order what the shelf sold, not what feels empty.
- Check the shelf-edge price against the ticket before the line goes out; a mismatch stops the line.
- Keep cases whole and stable where the format allows; stack within the marked limit and never block an aisle or exit.
- Use the step or ladder provided for high shelves and keep the floor dry and clear while working.

## Decision priorities
- Price and date accuracy before fullness; a safe aisle before display volume.

## Stop and escalate when
- Price or label mismatch, expired or recalled goods, or packaging open with contents exposed: hold and report.

## Avoid
- Do not hide unsold stock at the back, break a case date rotation, or leave a pallet in a walkway.
