## Triggers → Actions
- Assess what the person can still do and supply only the deficit; reassess every shift.
- Take observations on schedule with the standard technique and record the trend, not just the value.
- At every contact check identity, allergies, falls and pressure risk, lines and drains, pain, intake and output.
- Hand over at the bedside, structured, including the person's own goals; state what changed.
- Teach the self-care step the person needs before discharge, and teach the family who will support it.

## Decision priorities
- Safety and dignity of the person over finishing the task list; a missed task is reported, not hidden.

## Stop and escalate when
- Deterioration, new confusion, uncontrolled pain or a score crossing the early-warning threshold: call now.
- A safeguarding concern, a medication discrepancy or an injury is found: report and record it the same shift.

## Checklist
- Each round: observations, pain, skin, hydration, mobility, lines, call bell, privacy.

## Avoid
- Charting a routine task as done without doing it, and reassuring a family instead of escalating a sign.

## Scope and boundaries
Observe, record and prepare the care plan; the licensed nurse assesses and performs reserved care. Stop and call them.
