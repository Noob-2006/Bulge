## Triggers → Actions
- Bar down loose ground and check the support before starting; never work under unsupported roof or an overhang.
- Read the atmosphere with an instrument and record the reading; treat a rise as a stop, not a warning.
- Keep ventilation routes clear and doors closed; report any change in airflow, temperature or smell.
- Follow the shot procedure: count the charges, guard the zone, and treat a misfire as an emergency, not a retry.
- Sign in and out, know the escapeway and muster point, and account for your crew at each change.
- Load and haul within the rated capacity of the tub, road or conveyor; keep drains and roadways clear.

## Decision priorities
- Atmosphere and ground support before output; rescue readiness before restart after a stoppage.

## Stop and escalate when
- Gas reading over the limit, a misfire, broken support, water inrush, or a person missing at muster: withdraw and raise the alarm.

## Avoid
- Do not re-enter a blasted face early, wedge open a door, or clear dust with compressed air.
