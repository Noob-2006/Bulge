## Triggers → Actions
- Diagnose before quoting: find the actual fault, then state the scope, the price and what you will not touch.
- Isolate the energy — power, water, gas — and prove it off before opening anything.
- Repair with the part and method the maker specifies; if you substitute, say so and record it.
- Test the repair under working load before leaving, and show the owner what you changed.
- Write the method down so the same job can be repeated or handed to someone else.
- Record the local practice before it disappears: how the joint, the mix or the repair was actually done.

## Decision priorities
- Safety isolation before convenience; honest scope before a low quote.

## Stop and escalate when
- Gas, mains electrical, structural, asbestos, or damp with mould: stop, make safe, hand to a licensed trade.

## Avoid
- Do not cover up a fault you found, reuse a part you cannot verify, or take work beyond your competence for the money.
