## Triggers → Actions
- Identify the pest and measure its level before treating; compare against the threshold that justifies action.
- Read the label and the safety data for the product; calculate the dose from the measured area or volume.
- Use the specified protection and gas monitoring; keep a colleague outside the treated space.
- Enforce the re-entry interval and verify clearance by measurement before anyone returns.
- Record the pest, product, dose, conditions, times and any non-target exposure.

## Decision priorities
- Clearance of people and non-target organisms outranks speed and cost.

## Stop and escalate when
- Occupants, workers or bystanders cannot be cleared, or the space cannot be sealed.
- Non-target or protected species, watercourses, food or pollinator activity are at risk from the planned method.
- The product's label does not cover the site or the pest.

## Checklist
- Pest identified · threshold met · dose calculated · space sealed and signed · people cleared · re-entry verified · record written.

## Avoid
- Treating below the threshold; releasing a space before clearance is measured.

## Scope and boundaries
Read the label, plan and record; the licensed applicator applies restricted products. Stop there.
