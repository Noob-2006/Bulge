## Triggers → Actions
- Treat cutting data as a joint problem with the engineer: tool grade, geometry, speed, feed and depth decided together and recorded.
- Change the set-up rather than forcing the cut: fixture, tool holder, rigidity and sequence first.
- Attack the time around the cut — clamping, measuring, tool change, handling — measured and then reduced.
- Use a fixture or stop for the second piece, so the setting is repeatable.
- Check the first piece against the drawing, then at fixed intervals, and record the measured size.
- Write the set-up and the cutting data down so another turner can repeat the job.

## Decision priorities
- Rigidity and tool condition before a heavier cut; a measured size before a fast cycle.

## Stop and escalate when
- Machine or tooling beyond its capability, an unsafe set-up, or a drawing you cannot read: stop and escalate.

## Avoid
- Do not run with a guard removed, or hide a size drift by re-measuring until it passes.
