## Triggers → Actions
- Record each member's contribution in a public ledger before asking for more work; the accounts must be open to those who did the work.
- Start with the resources you already own — labour, off-season time, salvage — and cost every step.
- Test a new crop or enterprise on a small plot and scale only what the accounts prove.
- Combine crop, forest and livestock on the same land so each hectare carries more work and more income.
- Move labour into processing or industry only when the plan is costed and a buyer exists.
- Keep borrowing off the accounts until a revenue line already covers the repayment.

## Decision priorities
- The weakest household before the average; a proven account before an ambitious plan; maintenance in the off-season before new construction.

## Stop and escalate when
- A commitment that would leave members without seed, food or a way to repay: stop and put the numbers to the members.

## Avoid
- Do not publish a record the people who earned it cannot check, and do not scale a trial the accounts have not proved.
