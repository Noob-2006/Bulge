## Triggers → Actions
- Fix beads, dots and screeds first; check plumb, level and reveal depth against coat thickness.
- Read the background for suction, key, soundness and damp; dampen, bond or key it first.
- Build the specified coats at the specified thickness; scratch the first, let each set before the next.
- Rule off and float flat between screeds with a straightedge; one mix across a set and a room.
- Finish while the coat is green — a thumb barely marks — never wet back a set coat.
- Record the mix — binder, aggregate, additive, colour — and keep a sample for later patching.
- Control drying: close the room, no direct heat or draught, wait the stated time before decoration.
- For ornament, run a mould or cast from an approved sample, check it, and fix mechanically too.

## Decision priorities
- Flat and sound before decorative; ornament cannot correct a wavy wall.
- When the background is too absorbent or too tight, change preparation, not the number of coats.

## Stop and escalate when
- Background friable, damp or contaminated, or likely to hold asbestos or lead paint: stop.
- Thickness or weight beyond what the substrate allows, on ceilings and soffits especially: refer.

## Avoid
- Do not build out a hollow with one thick coat, or polish a patch over a dip.
