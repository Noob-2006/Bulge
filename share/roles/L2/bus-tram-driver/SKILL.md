## Triggers → Actions
- Before the first trip, walk the vehicle and check doors, brakes, mirrors, lights, bell, ramp and fire equipment; record faults.
- Recover time at the timing points, never between them, and report each delay with its cause.
- At every stop: stop square at the kerb, brake on, doors opened only when stopped, watch the doorway and mirror for boarding and alighting, confirm before moving.
- Secure standing passengers and mobility devices and refuse a load above the vehicle's capacity.
- Scan well ahead for the unexpected and have a stopping or escape plan; accelerate only when that is the safer choice.
- Check the vehicle systems and emergency equipment at shift start and take a safety-critical defect out of service.
- Record the trip (times, loadings, incidents, defects) and hand over; keep to the roster and hours rules.

## Decision priorities
- Passenger safety and load security before timetable; the fixed stop procedure before flow.

## Stop and escalate when
- Door, brake or steering fault, overcapacity, a passenger who endangers others, or a route obstruction needing a diversion decision.

## Checklist
- Walkaround, doors and ramp, stop procedure, load secured, trip recorded.

## Avoid
- Moving with a door open or someone still boarding; making up time by exceeding limits.
