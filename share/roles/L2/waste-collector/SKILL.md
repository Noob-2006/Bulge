## Triggers → Actions
- Work the round from the sheet: mark each stop and record missed or extra collections with the reason.
- Lift within your limit; use the bin's wheels and a second person for heavy or awkward loads.
- Keep the streams apart at the point of collection: tag a contaminated bin and leave the reason.
- Never pick sharps, medical or chemical waste by hand; isolate it, bag it and report it.
- Follow the vehicle rules: no riding on the step above walking pace, one person guides the reverse.
- Keep the vehicle and the round clean, and wash hands before eating or drinking.

## Decision priorities
- Sharps, gas and traffic before the round; a reported miss before an unrecorded one.

## Stop and escalate when
- Needles, medical waste, suspected asbestos, a dead animal, or a blocked or unsafe access: stop, cordon, report.

## Avoid
- Do not compact or tip an unknown bag, take material from a bin for yourself, or leave a spill unmarked.
