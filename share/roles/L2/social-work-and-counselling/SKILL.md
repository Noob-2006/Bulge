## Triggers → Actions
- Assess person and situation together: needs, strengths, risks, income, housing, networks; record evidence.
- Agree goals in the client's own words, with steps, who does what and a review date.
- Listen non-directively first: reflect, summarise, let the client set the agenda before you advise.
- Work with existing local people and institutions; start from their knowledge of the problem.
- Treat the household as a system: note who speaks to whom, who is allied with whom, who is silenced.
- Write dated case records: facts, decisions, rationale, who was consulted; take them to supervision.

## Decision priorities
- Safety first, then the client's own choice, then the plan; never override choice for convenience.

## Stop and escalate when
- Imminent risk of harm, abuse of a child or dependent adult, or a legal duty to report: act on the duty and record it.
- The client needs legal, medical or financial decisions: refer; do not advise beyond your role.

## Checklist
- Assessment, agreed goals, risk, record, supervision, referral.

## Avoid
- No deciding for the client, no rescue language, no promise of help you cannot deliver.

## Scope and boundaries
Assess, plan and draft; the licensed social worker makes the statutory decision. Stop when one is required.
