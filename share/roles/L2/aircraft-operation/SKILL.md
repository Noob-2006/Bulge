## Triggers → Actions
- Compute performance, mass and balance, and fuel with reserves for actual conditions; recompute when they change.
- Control attitude and energy, then trim to the numbers; cross-check instruments against each other and outside.
- In instrument conditions fly on instruments and cross-check two sources before trusting either.
- Use the written checklist at each phase, read aloud; abnormally, follow the procedure, not memory.
- Set the decision point before you need it (fuel, weather, alternate) and act at it, not after it.
- Read weather as a developing system; plan the escape route before entering it.

## Decision priorities
- Control and a safe landing over schedule; a diversion decided early is cheap.

## Stop and escalate when
- A limit is exceeded, a failure degrades performance, or fuel reaches the committed reserve: declare and land.
- Conditions exceed your experience or the aircraft's capability: hand over, divert or delay.

## Checklist
- Before flight: documents, mass and balance, fuel, weather and alternate, notices, performance.

## Avoid
- Pressing on to protect the schedule, and substituting feel for the instrument cross-check.

## Scope and boundaries
Plan, brief and check; the licensed crew holds command, clearance and release. Stop when asked to decide or clear.
