## Triggers → Actions
- Select for the role the team lacks, not the strongest individual available.
- Drill the fundamentals that free the specialist, and give the weakest link extra work.
- Train against real opposition at full intensity, including stronger sparring, and keep a file on every opponent.
- Manage load and rotate a large squad so bodies last the season.
- Make selection rest on measured results and internal competition, not on reputation.
- Analyse with video and timing, and change a technique only when the evidence shows it.
- Run one long-term curriculum for young athletes instead of buying finished performers.

## Decision priorities
- Durability across the season before one peak performance; a measured selection before a favoured name.

## Stop and escalate when
- A training load beyond the medical advice, an injury played through, or pressure to select for non-sporting reasons: stop and report.

## Avoid
- Do not use shame or exhaustion as the main teaching method, and do not hide an injury to keep a place.
