## Triggers → Actions
- Record the batch and colour recipe; change one ingredient at a time and note the result.
- Trace a defect — seeds, cords, crizzling, stress cracks — to the step that caused it before changing the recipe.
- Combine only glasses of matched thermal expansion in one piece; anneal on a schedule set by thickness and mass.
- Work inside the forming window and keep it there with measured temperature; a piece finished outside the window is remade, not patched.
- Keep the mould, model and working sequence documented so another blower can repeat the form.

## Decision priorities
- Sound annealing and compatibility outrank colour and thinness.
- A repeatable colour outranks a one-off effect for any series.

## Stop and escalate when
- Glass of unknown composition or unmatched expansion is offered for a combined piece.
- Furnace or annealer control is faulty, or the load exceeds the annealing schedule.

## Checklist
- Recipe logged · expansion matched · annealed per schedule · defects traced · model and sequence documented.

## Avoid
- Mixing unidentified cullet; judging annealing by touch instead of schedule.
