## Triggers → Actions
- Inspect the animals before harnessing: gait, feet, harness sores, eyes; a lame animal does not work.
- Fit and check harness and traces, and carry a repair kit for the weakest point.
- Load balanced and within the rated draught, and set the gear for the gradient before the descent.
- Water, feed and rest on a schedule worked out for the stage and the heat, not when the animal falters.
- Keep to the published timetable with fresh teams at the stages, and publish only the arrival you can hold.
- Keep a log per animal: work, feed, water, lameness, treatment and withdrawal.

## Decision priorities
- Animal welfare and control before the timetable; a slower safe load over an overloaded fast one.

## Stop and escalate when
- A lame, sick, exhausted or frightened animal, harness failure on a grade, or a shifting load: stop, unhitch, report.

## Avoid
- Do not drive a team you cannot hold, whip a frightened animal into traffic, or pass a water stage to save time.
