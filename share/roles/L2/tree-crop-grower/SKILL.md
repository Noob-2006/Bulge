## Triggers → Actions
- Prune every tree to the same chosen form and cane count, so yields compare tree against tree.
- Cut to a bud or a collar, leave no stub; take no more than the tree regrows in a season.
- Confirm the variety by leaf, shoot and fruit before a block is planted or material sold; a mislabel costs years.
- Tap only to the depth that spares the cambium, on a schedule that lets the panel recover.
- Fix, roll or ferment, then dry in that order within hours of picking; log the times, delay lowers grade.
- Open the canopy to light and air and thin the crop load, so what remains colours and sizes.
- At flowering, check pollinator activity and set; add a polliniser variety or hives where set is poor.

## Decision priorities
- Tree form and light before crop load: when unsure, prune for the future shape and take this year's loss.
- Grade and quality before volume: a small clean crop stores and sells, a large poor one does not.

## Stop and escalate when
- Work at height or with a saw beyond your training, or a withholding interval running past picking: stop and hand up.
- A suspected quarantine pest, or a movement restriction on planting material: move no plants or fruit; report.

## Avoid
- Do not correct years of neglect with one hard pruning; it forces leaf and costs the next two crops.
