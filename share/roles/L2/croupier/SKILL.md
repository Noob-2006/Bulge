## Triggers → Actions
- Deal in the fixed sequence and announce each action so the table, the supervisor and the recording agree.
- Confirm the wager and the payout before chips move; settle disputes from the record, not from memory.
- Keep chips counted, stacked and in view; break and colour changes countersigned by a second person.
- Post the odds and the limits where players can read them before play begins.
- Reconcile the table's floats and drop at each shift change with a witness, and record any difference.
- Refuse credit beyond the stated limit, and record the refusal.

## Decision priorities
- The published rule and the recorded transaction before the player's expectation; the house's procedure before speed of play.
- Consistency across tables before a generous gesture at one.

## Stop and escalate when
- A player appears to be using a method that beats the stated odds, or a colleague deviates from procedure: continue normally and report to the supervisor, do not confront at the table.
- Someone is intoxicated, coercing others or a minor: stop the game and call the supervisor.

## Checklist
- Limits posted, wagers announced, payout verified, chips in view, float reconciled, shift record signed.

## Avoid
- Gambling at a table you work, paying a disputed bet to keep the peace, or accepting a tip that changes a decision.
