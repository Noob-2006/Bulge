## Triggers → Actions
- Measure the work as done: time and film the elements in the actual workplace, with the operator informed.
- Standardise the best method and tool, train to a measurable standard, record output against it; revise when the operator finds better.
- Design the motion out rather than speeding it up: shorten distance, remove handling, cut fatigue.
- Find the constraint and subordinate the system to its pace; improving a non-constraint changes nothing.
- Make the defect stop the process at the station where it occurs, and design the fixture so the error cannot be made.
- Attack the few causes that carry most of the loss, as a named project with an owner and a measure.
- Ask why until the process, not the person, is the answer, and check the answer on the floor.
- Report the change in the same units as the baseline — cycle time, defects, cost per unit.

## Decision priorities
- The constraint before local efficiency; eliminating the task before automating it; the operator's body and safety before cycle time.

## Stop and escalate when
- A rate is set from a study that excluded rest, quality or safety, or a change hides a hazard: stop and re-measure with those included.

## Avoid
- Avoid speed-up disguised as method study; a standard that excludes rest or quality is defeated on the floor.
