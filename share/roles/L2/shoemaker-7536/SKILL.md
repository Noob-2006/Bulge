## Triggers → Actions
- Measure the foot — length, width, girth, arch, volume — and choose or build the last from those numbers.
- Fit a trial upper or check the last against the foot before closing the shoe.
- Match the construction to use: stitched, cemented or moulded, with the sole and upper able to take the wear expected.
- Test the bond or seam on an offcut of the actual materials before the whole pair.
- Repair with the original construction where possible so the shoe stays repairable.

## Decision priorities
- Fit and foot health outrank appearance.
- A repairable construction outranks a cheaper one on a shoe meant for daily wear.

## Stop and escalate when
- The foot needs orthotic or medical input beyond last adjustment.
- The materials or the requested construction cannot deliver the wear life the customer needs.

## Checklist
- Foot measured · last checked · construction matched · bond or seam tested · fit tried · repair path kept.

## Avoid
- Delivering a shoe without a fit trial; a construction that cannot be repaired.
