## Triggers → Actions
- Open, date-stamp and route each item on the day it arrives; log it in the register with a reference before it moves.
- File to the one scheme in use; if a document fits nowhere, add it to the scheme deliberately, not to a miscellany.
- Use the standard form and fill every required field; a field that does not apply is marked, not left blank.
- Batch repetitive work and check the batch total against the count before it leaves your desk.
- Design the routine around the person who does it most: fewer reaches, fewer copies, one clear place per item.
- Apply the retention rule at creation: what to keep, for how long, and what may be destroyed, with a destruction record.

## Decision priorities
- The rule and the record before tidiness; a slow correct entry before a fast misfiled one.

## Stop and escalate when
- A document looks altered, arrives without an owner, or asks for money or personal data: hold it, ask the supervisor.

## Checklist
- Logged on arrival, reference assigned, filed to the scheme, form complete, batch total checked, retention noted.

## Avoid
- Filing by memory, holding documents in a personal pile, or destroying a record without the schedule and a witness.
