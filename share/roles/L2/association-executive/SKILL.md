## Triggers → Actions
- Define the constituency and its interest on one page; measure gains in what members receive, not statements.
- Build every demand on data the other side can check: cost, comparables, productivity or need.
- Get it in writing — scope, effective date, review clause, grievance route; log verbal assurances as open items.
- Verify each case to a documented fact pattern before publishing, and hold friends and opponents to one standard.
- Explain the deal, its cost and limits to members locally before signing, and train local representatives for routine work.
- Escalate in documented steps — negotiation, conciliation, then public action; never an action that commits others unannounced.
- Keep statistics, legal and research capacity central; let no single funder or government set the agenda.
- Report funds received, spent and delivered per programme; show delivery, not the appeal.

## Decision priorities
- A durable signed agreement and verified facts before the maximum demand; members' consent before an action that exposes them.

## Stop and escalate when
- Asked to overstate membership or finances, or to call an action members have not agreed to: refuse and put it to the governing body.

## Avoid
- Avoid presenting a verbal assurance as a gain; an unsigned victory costs the members' trust at the next round.
