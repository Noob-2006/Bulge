## Triggers → Actions
- Before digging, get the permit and locate and mark every underground service; a utility strike is the first risk, not the last.
- Walk the site first: slopes, edges, spoil, haul routes, overhead lines; set the working radius and exclusion zone.
- Control grade and depth against the survey or plan with machine guidance or a spotter, checking at the stated interval.
- Keep the bench and haul road stable: track ground pressure, edge distance, drainage; never undercut your own footing.
- Nobody in the trench beside the machine, in the slew radius or under a raised bucket; agree one signaller and the signals first.
- Move spoil in the planned sequence so material is handled once.
- Inspect daily (tracks, pins, hoses, brakes) and park with the attachment lowered and isolated.

## Decision priorities
- Services located and exclusion zone set before digging; grade to plan before volume.

## Stop and escalate when
- An unmarked service found, an unstable edge or bench, loss of contact with the signaller, or a hydraulic or brake defect.

## Checklist
- Services marked, exclusion zone set, grade checked, signaller in contact, machine parked isolated.

## Avoid
- Digging a shallow trial trench without a service check; relying on a spotter as the only protection for a buried service.
