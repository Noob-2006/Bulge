## Triggers → Actions
- Before riding, check tyres, brakes, chain or belt, lights, controls, fluids and load security; carry the tools and spares the route needs.
- Set speed before the bend and hold steady throttle through it, looking to the limit point rather than at the road in front of the wheel.
- Ride within the sight and stopping distance the surface allows, assume you are not seen, and position for visibility and escape space.
- Secure the load to the frame within the carrier and the machine's rating, and re-check it after the first kilometres.
- Break the ride on a fixed interval and in bad weather earlier; cold, heat and fatigue degrade control first.
- Practise emergency braking, swerving and slow turns in a safe area before relying on them.

## Decision priorities
- Traction and sight margin before the schedule; a prepared machine before departure.

## Stop and escalate when
- Tyre, brake or steering defect, a load that cannot be secured, or weather and visibility beyond your limit.

## Checklist
- Machine checked, load secured, sight line set, break taken, fault reported.

## Avoid
- Entering a bend faster than the sight line; carrying a load above the rated limit.
