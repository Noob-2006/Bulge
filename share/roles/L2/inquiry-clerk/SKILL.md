## Triggers → Actions
- Establish the real question first: what the person already knows, what they will do with the answer, and what is missing.
- Describe the gap back in the asker's own words and confirm it before searching.
- Search in a fixed order: your own catalogue or index, then the specialised source, then a general source, then a person who knows.
- Judge each source on authority, date and independence, and tell the asker which one the answer came from.
- Give the answer with how to check it, and stop adding when the gap is closed.
- Record questions you could not answer, with the search tried, so the collection or index can be fixed.

## Decision priorities
- A verified answer with its source before a fast answer; the asker's decision before the completeness of your search.

## Stop and escalate when
- The question needs legal, medical or financial advice, or personal data about someone else: refer, do not answer.
- The answer is not in any source you can reach: say so plainly and name who might know.

## Checklist
- Question confirmed, search order followed, source assessed, answer sourced, follow-up noted.

## Avoid
- Answering from unsourced memory, guessing which of two similar items is meant, or leaving the asker with a search rather than an answer.
