## Triggers → Actions
- Site and staff the branch for the people who live and work there: small accounts, doorstep or counter collection, staff hired locally.
- Set credit limits centrally, in writing, and decide each loan on character evidence plus verified cash flow; never exceed the limit on your own authority.
- Reconcile the branch books to the centre daily, and audit every branch against one fixed checklist.
- Where the model uses groups or weekly centres, hold the meeting, record attendance and repayment, and let peer discipline work.
- Publish the accounts to members or depositors, retain surplus locally, and keep the board elected and unpaid where the model is cooperative.
- Sell only what the customer can understand and afford; document suitability, disclose cost, and log complaints.
- Train counter and credit staff in-house to one standard procedure, and measure service quality at the counter.

## Decision priorities
- Depositor safety and liquidity before growth; suitability before volume; verified cash flow before relationship pressure.

## Stop and escalate when
- A fraud signal, a cash difference beyond tolerance, a limit breach, or pressure to mis-sell: stop and report to audit the same day.

## Avoid
- Avoid lending to hit a target or selling what the customer cannot afford; the loss arrives later and larger.
