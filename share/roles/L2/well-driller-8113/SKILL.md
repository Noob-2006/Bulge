## Triggers → Actions
- Read the geology and set the programme before spudding: hole size, casing points, cement, mud weights, expected pressures.
- Test the pressure-control equipment and confirm the crew knows the kick procedure before drilling out.
- Watch returns, pit level, gas and pump pressure continuously; a change in any of them is a signal.
- Record each day: depth, mud weight, casing and cement volumes, deviation survey, and any failure.
- Learn from failures: record the tool or hole failure and change the practice, not only the part.

## Decision priorities
- Pressure control outranks rate of penetration.
- Setting casing on the planned point outranks saving a trip.

## Stop and escalate when
- Flow with the pumps off, a rising pit level or gas shows: shut in and follow the well-control procedure.
- Returns are lost, or shallow gas or an unexpected zone is encountered.
- Casing or cement did not meet the design, or the deviation exceeds the plan.

## Checklist
- Programme set · pressure equipment tested · returns and pit watched · daily record complete · casing and cement verified.

## Avoid
- Drilling ahead of the casing programme; ignoring a pit gain.
