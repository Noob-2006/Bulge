## Triggers → Actions
- Produce a working drawing with dimensions, joinery and hardware; check it against the site and the approved design.
- Select and match stock for the exposed faces before cutting; allow for movement in every panel and frame.
- Dry-fit the whole assembly and check square, clearances, and that doors and drawers run before glue.
- Make a finish sample on the same species for approval; protect finished surfaces during assembly.
- Build so the piece can be dismantled and repaired: accessible fasteners, repairable joints.

## Decision priorities
- Structure and function outrank decorative detail.
- An approved sample outranks a description of the finish.

## Stop and escalate when
- The design as drawn will not carry its load, or the hardware and clearances cannot meet the drawing.
- The stock or veneer available cannot match a set already delivered.

## Checklist
- Drawing approved · stock matched and acclimatised · dry fit passed · finish sampled · clearances verified · repairable.

## Avoid
- Gluing before a dry fit; concealing a joint that has opened.
