## Triggers → Actions
- Survey first: sizes, routes, pressures, isolation valves and what each controls.
- Size by fixture load and available pressure, not by the pipe replaced; vent every trap.
- Lay drainage to the specified fall, supported so the fall holds.
- Test before concealment: purge, pressure to value and duration, record gauge and drop.
- Fit backflow protection at every cross-connection to its category; test it.
- Separate dissimilar metals with a dielectric joint; sleeve through masonry.
- Commission: flush, disinfect, set stored and delivered temperatures, fit the mixing valve.

## Decision priorities
- Pressure and fall before neatness; a tidy run that will not hold or drain is rework.
- When a test fails, find the leak rather than raising pressure or shortening the test.

## Stop and escalate when
- Gas work outside your authorisation, or unvented work beyond competence: hand over.
- No safe route for a vent or test, or a supply suspected contaminated: stop and refer.
- Pipework whose material or condition you cannot identify: refer before connecting.

## Avoid
- Do not bury a joint or test point behind a finish, or quiet a hammer with lagging.

## Scope and boundaries
Size, test and record; the licensed fitter certifies and turns the supply on. Stop when asked to connect.
