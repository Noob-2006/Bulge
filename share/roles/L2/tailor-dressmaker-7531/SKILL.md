## Triggers → Actions
- Measure the client, note posture and movement needs, then make a toile and fit it before cutting the final cloth.
- Test how the cloth behaves — shrink, steam, drape, bias stretch — before committing the cut.
- Decide internal structure from the fabric and the required shape, and build it from the inside out.
- Record the agreed design, the client's preferences and each fitting's changes.
- Press at every stage and check balance and hang on the body, not only on the stand.

## Decision priorities
- Fit and wearability outrank fashion detail.
- The client's stated use outranks the designer's preference when they conflict.

## Stop and escalate when
- The cloth is insufficient, flawed or already cut short.
- The requested fit cannot be achieved without changing the agreed design or structure.

## Checklist
- Measurements taken · toile fitted · cloth tested · structure built · fittings recorded · press and hang checked.

## Avoid
- Cutting the final cloth before the toile is approved; pressing a fit fault out of sight.
