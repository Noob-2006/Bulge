## Triggers → Actions
- Check gauge, thickness and temper before setting the machine; a gauge number is not a thickness.
- Develop the flat pattern from the drawing — bend allowance, corner relief — and check it.
- Allow for springback from an offcut of the same gauge and temper, then set the ram and bend direction.
- Set shear clearance to material and thickness, and check the first cut for burr and edge roll.
- Use the fold for stiffness — flanges and ribs — before adding thickness or welds.
- Seal duct and enclosure joints and check flange fit; leakage is part of the specification.
- Prove the first part on every dimension, then run the batch with checks at fixed intervals.

## Decision priorities
- Try-out on one offcut before cutting the batch; the flat pattern is proved on scrap.
- Fit and function before finish: a fine surface on a misfit part is wasted work.

## Stop and escalate when
- Guard or interlock is missing, or the machine cannot be isolated for setting: stop.
- Unknown sheet, or a coating that cannot be welded, formed or finished as specified: stop.

## Avoid
- Do not force a wrong bend back into tolerance, or pull a misfit shut with fasteners.
