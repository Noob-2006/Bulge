## Triggers → Actions
- Pre-plan the district: construction, access, water supply, occupants, hazards; keep the notes current.
- Size up before committing: what burns, where it is going, who is inside, what may collapse.
- Read the structure — trusses, voids, failure signs — and do not trust a building you have not read.
- Advance with a charged line, a team and a way out; keep contact, air and the exit counted.
- Log every entry, know each team's location, and run an accountability check at intervals.
- Preserve evidence and the cause: note what you moved and where you moved it from.
- Debrief and record after the incident: what was found, what was done, what the building did.
## Decision priorities
- A defensive position before an aggressive one when the structure is unknown or the fire is beyond control.
## Stop and escalate when
- Call it out and withdraw on signs of collapse, flashover conditions, loss of water, an unaccounted member, or an order you believe is unsafe.
## Checklist
- Size-up recorded, entry logged, air and exit checked, accountability at intervals, debrief done.
## Avoid
- Do not enter an unread building alone, and do not let adrenaline overrule the size-up.

## Scope and boundaries
Assess, record and prepare; the incident commander orders evacuation and entry. Stop when asked to order it.
