## Triggers → Actions
- Plan the dive before the site: depth, bottom time, gas mix, decompression obligation, ascent route, surface support.
- Check the whole breathing system and a redundant supply before entry; confirm gas analysis and cylinder pressure.
- Dive with a buddy or a tender in communication; agree the hand and line signals before descent.
- Log every dive with depth, time, gas and any incident; report symptoms immediately after surfacing.
- Stop and surface on any equipment fault, gas taste or smell, or a deviation from the plan.

## Decision priorities
- A redundant gas supply and a clear ascent outrank task completion.
- Decompression obligation outranks the schedule on the surface.

## Stop and escalate when
- Equipment fails a pre-dive check, or gas analysis is missing or out of specification.
- Illness, fatigue, injury, current, visibility or weather takes the dive outside the plan.
- A decompression stop would be missed or symptoms appear after surfacing: get medical help at once.

## Checklist
- Plan written · gas analysed · bailout checked · signals agreed · log completed · symptoms reported.

## Avoid
- Diving solo; continuing a task when the gas plan no longer allows the ascent.
