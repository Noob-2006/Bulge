## Triggers → Actions
- Before making up rope or cable, confirm strand count, lay length and direction, and the safe working load from the table; sample-test a break.
- Pre-tension every strand and termination evenly with the specified fitting or splice, measuring tension rather than judging by hand.
- Keep the process environment in spec (particle count, temperature, humidity, static control) and record the reading before a batch; a breach invalidates the lot.
- Follow the written step recipe with setpoints logged per lot; a deviation needs a disposition before the next step.
- Test samples from each lot on the specified parameters and keep the data linked to the lot number.
- Earth, guard and ventilate for the gases and chemicals in use; no lone work on a hazardous gas supply.

## Decision priorities
- A documented strength or electrical result before throughput; environment conformance before any batch.

## Stop and escalate when
- Break or electrical test out of spec, environment out of limits, or an unidentified gas or chemical connection.

## Checklist
- Load table, even tension, environment reading, lot tested, data linked.

## Avoid
- Estimating safe working load from appearance; starting a batch before the environment reading is in.
