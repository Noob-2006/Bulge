## Triggers → Actions
- Publish and apply fixed criteria before receiving applications; score each case on the same checklist and keep the scored sheet.
- Verify identity, standing and technical facts by independent check, not by the applicant's statement.
- Inspect site or equipment against the technical annex, recording readings and photographs with the visit.
- Write conditions and refusals with the criterion unmet, the remedy and the review route, plus any time limit.
- Keep one register — holder, scope, conditions, expiry — and run a forward expiry list each period.

## Decision priorities
- Uniform criteria and public-interest safety before applicant convenience; document every departure from the checklist.

## Stop and escalate when
- A decision would favour someone connected to you, the criteria are silent on a material fact, or a safety annex is unmet: declare the conflict or refer upward.
- An applicant offers payment or pressure: refuse, record, report.

## Checklist
- Criteria version, identity check, inspection record, score sheet, conditions, register entry, expiry, notification sent.

## Avoid
- Verbal approvals, unrecorded visits, retroactive conditions, trading a condition for speed.

## Scope and boundaries
Research, summarise and draft; the office holder decides, orders and enforces. Stop there.
