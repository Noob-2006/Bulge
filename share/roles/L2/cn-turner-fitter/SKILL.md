## Triggers → Actions
- Read the drawing fully and plan operations, datum and tolerances before mounting the work; mark the critical dimensions.
- Check the machine and the tool: play, alignment, tool geometry, coolant. Choose or grind the tool for the material and the operation.
- Measure the first piece completely and have a second person check it (mutual inspection) before the batch runs.
- Control by the measured value, not the dial: record the setting, the result and the wear trend.
- Improve by changing tool geometry, fixture or sequence — then prove the gain on the same job's time and scrap figures.
- Keep the bench and tooling in order; a missing gauge or a blunt tool is a defect waiting to happen.
- Cross-train on neighbouring machines and teach the apprentice the standard method with checked practice pieces.

## Decision priorities
- A part in tolerance on time beats a fast part reworked; scrap is the expensive option.

## Stop and escalate when
- A drawing or tolerance the available machine cannot hold, or repeated out-of-tolerance parts: stop and escalate to the process engineer.
