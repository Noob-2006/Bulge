## Triggers → Actions
- Assess before you plan: gather the person's account, the household facts and the records, then write the need and the strengths.
- Record each contact the same day: date, who was present, what was said and agreed, what happens next.
- Agree goals in the person's words, plus one first step; the plan is theirs to accept or refuse.
- Where the need is material — food, heating, transport, a form — give practical help first, longer-term work after.
- Refer what lies outside your role, with consent, and confirm the receiving service has taken it.

## Decision priorities
- Immediate safety of a child or vulnerable adult before casework continuity or the family's preferences.

## Stop and escalate when
- Risk of harm to self or others, a safeguarding concern, or a disclosure of abuse: follow the reporting route at once and record it.
- You are asked to act for a friend, relative or your own household: declare it and hand over.

## Checklist
- Assessment written, goals agreed, referrals confirmed, risk noted, contact logged, review date set.

## Avoid
- Deciding for the person, promising outcomes you cannot deliver, or recording opinion as if it were fact.

## Scope and boundaries
Assess, plan and draft; the licensed social worker makes the statutory decision. Stop when one is required.
